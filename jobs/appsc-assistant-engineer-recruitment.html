APPSC Assistant Engineer Recruitment 2025 - 166 Posts, Notification, Online Form,

Published by: RozgarDwaar Editorial Desk | Updated: 2025-06-07
Apply Online (Official Website) Download Notification PDF Official Website

APPSC Arunachal Engineering Service Examination 2025 Notification Out. The Arunachal Pradesh Public Service Commission (APPSC) invites online applications from Indian citizens for the Arunachal Engineering Service Examination 2025 to fill 166 Assistant Engineer posts in various departments of the Government of Arunachal Pradesh. In this article, job aspirants can find details about APPSC Assistant Engineer Recruitment 2025, including vacancies, eligibility criteria, age limit, salary, application fee, selection process, important dates, detailed notification PDF, and online application form link.

Assistant Engineer (Civil, Electro-Mechanical, Electrical)

Total Vacancies

166

General: Born on/after 01/01/1989; APST: Born on/after 01/01/1984; Relaxation for PwBD/Govt. Servants as per rules

Pay Matrix Level-10 (₹ 56,100 – ₹ 1,77,500)

Educational Qualification

B.E/B.Tech in relevant engineering disciplines

Selection Process

Recruitment Test (if needed), Written Mains Exam, Viva-Voce

Arunachal Pradesh

Eligibility Criteria::

✔️ Age Limit:

General candidates: Born on or after 01/01/1989; APST candidates: Born on or after 01/01/1984. Additional relaxation for PwBD and regular Govt. servants as per Arunachal Pradesh Govt. rules.

✔️ Educational Qualifications:

Assistant Engineer (Civil):

B.E/ B.Tech in Civil Engineering (Public Work, PHE & Water Supply, Urban Development, Urban Local Bodies, Hydro Power Development, Water Resource Departments); Civil or Agricultural Engineering (Rural Work Department).

Assistant Engineer (Electro-Mechanical):

B.E/ B.Tech in Electrical, Mechanical, Electronics & Communication, or Computer Engineering (Hydro Power Development).

Assistant Engineer (Electrical):

B.E/ B.Tech in Electrical, Mechanical, Electronics & Communication, or Computer Engineering (Department of Power).

Selection Process::

✔️ Recruitment Test: General Awareness & Logical Reasoning (100 marks), Technical Paper (200 marks).
✔️ Written Mains Exam: General Paper (150 marks, Objective), Technical Paper-I (150 marks, Descriptive), Technical Paper-II (150 marks, Descriptive).
✔️ Viva-Voce: 50 marks.

Application Fee::

✔️ ₹ 150/- for APST candidates.
✔️ ₹ 200/- for other candidates.
✔️ No fee for PwBD candidates.
✔️ Payment through online mode only.

How to Apply for APPSC Assistant Engineer Recruitment 2025::

➢ Eligible candidates must apply online through the APPSC website (appsc.gov.in) from 15/05/2025 (17:00 hrs) to 08/06/2025 (17:00 hrs).
➢ Complete One Time Registration (OTR) before applying; existing OTR users can log in directly.
➢ Fill in personal, educational, and other details, and upload scanned copies of signature (40-50 kb, jpg), recent passport-size photo (40-100 kb, jpg, white background), degree certificates/ mark sheets, Matriculation certificate, APST certificate/ PRC/ Aadhar (if applicable), PwBD certificate (if applicable), and NOC/ intimation for Govt. servants.
➢ Pay the application fee online and submit the application with the highest Registration ID (RID) if multiple applications are submitted.
➢ Retain a printed copy of the application for future reference.
➢ Original documents must be produced at the interview.
