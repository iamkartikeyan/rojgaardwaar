Bank of Baroda LBO Recruitment 2026 Eligibility Criteria::
  • Educational Qualification: Graduation in any discipline from a recognized University/Institution. Preferred Certifications: IC 38 (Insurance Institute of India), NISM Series V-A (Mutual Fund Distributors), NISM Series XXI-A (PMS Distributors), NISM Series XIII (Common Derivatives).
  • Post Qualification Experience: Minimum 1 year of post-qualification experience as an Officer in any Scheduled Commercial Bank as listed in the Second Schedule of the Reserve Bank of India.
  • Local Language Proficiency: Candidates must be proficient in reading, writing, speaking and understanding the local language of the State they are applying for.
  • Age Limit: Minimum 21 years and Maximum 30 years.
  • Age Relaxation: SC/ST – 5 years, OBC (NCL) – 3 years, PwBD – 10 years (and other categories as per Government norms).
Bank of Baroda LBO Salary 2026::
  • Scale of Pay (JMG/S-I): ₹48,480 – 2,000/7 – 62,480 – 2,340/2 – 67,160 – 2,680/7 – 85,920
  • Selected candidates will be posted within the State for which they apply for the first 12 years of their service or up to their promotion to SMGS-IV Grade, whichever is earlier. Thereafter, they are liable to be posted anywhere in India.
Bank of Baroda LBO Recruitment 2026 Selection Process::
  • Online Test
  • Psychometric Test and/or Personal Interview
  • Document Verification

Final Merit: Based on performance in Online Test, Psychometric Test/Interview and fulfilment of eligibility.

How to Apply for Bank of Baroda Local Bank Officer Recruitment 2026::

Eligible candidates must apply ONLINE ONLY through the official Bank of Baroda website. No other mode of application will be accepted.

➢ Step 1: Visit the official website www.bankofbaroda.bank.in → Career section → Current Opportunities.

➢ Step 2: Click on the link for “Recruitment of Local Bank Officers” or go to the Apply Online link.

➢ Step 3: Complete the registration process by providing basic details and generate Registration Number & Password.

➢ Step 4: Fill the online application form carefully with personal, educational, experience and other required details (apply for one State only).

➢ Step 5: Upload scanned copies of Photograph, Signature, Bio-data and other documents related to eligibility as per the prescribed format.

➢ Step 6: Pay the application fee online through Debit Card / Credit Card / Net Banking / UPI.

➢ Step 7: Submit the application form and take a printout of the confirmation page for future reference.
