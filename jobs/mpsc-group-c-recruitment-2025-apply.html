Published by: RozgarDwaar Editorial Desk | Updated: 2025-10-26
Apply Online (Official Website) Download Notification PDF Official Website

The Maharashtra Public Service Commission (MPSC) has announced a fresh recruitment drive for 938 positions in Group C categories. Eligible individuals across India can submit their online applications via the MPSC online application portal. The official MPSC job alert for Group C posts notification released on October 6, 2025, with the online submission window opening on October 7, 2025, at 2:00 PM and closing on October 27, 2025. Aspiring applicants should review the full MPSC circular thoroughly prior to starting their online submission to ensure compliance.

How to Apply MPSC Group C Recruitment 2025::

The MPSC online enrollment occurs via the official candidate portal at mpsconline.gov.in. Follow these straightforward steps for the MPSC Group C application procedure:

➢ Step 1: Visit the MPSC online application portal starting October 7, 2025, and select the Group C Combined Preliminary Examination option.

➢ Step 2: Complete the registration with personal details, educational info, and upload scanned copies of photo, signature, and supporting documents (degree certificates, ID proof, category certificate if applicable).

➢ Step 3: Proceed to pay the required fee based on your category using the integrated online gateway. Finalize and submit before October 27, 2025, at 11:59 PM; take a printout for records.

Frequently Asked Questions::
What is the age criterion for MPSC Group C Recruitment 2025?:

Maximum 38 years for general category as of February 1, 2026. Extensions apply: 43 years for reserved categories, EWS, orphans, and sportspersons; 45 years for PwD candidates, plus other rule-based relaxations.

What qualifications are needed for MPSC Group C Recruitment 2025?:

Bachelor’s degree for Clerk-Typist, Tax Assistant, and Technical Assistant; Diploma in Engineering for Industry Inspector, all from approved institutions.

What stages are involved in MPSC Group C Recruitment 2025 selection?:

Initial screening via Combined Preliminary Exam, advanced to Mains Exam, concluding with document scrutiny.

When is the deadline for MPSC Group C Recruitment 2025 applications?:

October 27, 2025, until 11:59 PM.

How many openings are there in MPSC Group C Recruitment 2025?:

A total of 938 positions distributed as Clerk-Typist (852), Tax Assistant (73), Industry Inspector (9), and Technical Assistant (4) within Maharashtra.
