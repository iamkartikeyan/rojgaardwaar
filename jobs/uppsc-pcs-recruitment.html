Apply Online (Official Website) Download Notification PDF Official Website

Uttar Pradesh Public Service Commission (UPPSC), Prayagraj, invites online applications from eligible graduates for the Combined State / Upper Subordinate Services (PCS) Examination 2026 for direct recruitment to 500 posts on a regular basis.

The UPPSC PCS Recruitment 2026 for 500 Vacancies Online Registration process starts from 25th June 2026 and closes on 03rd August 2026 (Extended). This article provides complete information about UPPSC PCS 2026, including eligibility criteria, vacancy details, official notification PDF, and online application link.

How to Apply UPPSC PCS Recruitment 2026?:

Eligible candidates must apply online only through the UPPSC portal. The online registration steps are follows:-

  • Step 1: Visit the official application portal and complete One Time Registration (OTR) using valid email and mobile number (if not already done).
  • Step 2: Fill the online application form carefully with personal, educational, experience, and category details.
  • Step 3: Upload scanned copies of Photograph, Signature, and other required documents as per guidelines.
  • Step 4: Pay the application fee online and submit the form.
  • Step 5: Take a printout of the system-generated application form for future reference.
