OSSSC Radiographer Recruitment 2024 Apply Online (378 Vacancies)

Published by: RozgarDwaar Editorial Desk | Updated: 2024-04-20
Apply Online (Official Website) Download Notification PDF Official Website

OSSSC Radiographer Recruitment 2023 Apply for 378 District Cadre Group C Posts The Odisha Subordinate Staff Selection Commission (OSSSC) invites online applications for 378 Radiographer positions in various district establishments and 13 Medical Colleges and Hospitals under the Health and Family Welfare Department of the Government of Odisha. If you have a Diploma in Radiography, seize this opportunity and apply now through osssc.gov.in.

The OSSSC Radiographer Recruitment Notification has reopened again. The online registration will start from April 5, 2024, and continue until April 15, 2024. Candidates who have already submitted their online applications earlier are not required to apply again.

✅ OSSSC Radiographer Recruitment 2024 Age Limit:

✔️ Minimum 21 years and Maximum 38 years as on the date of Advertisement.
✔️ The Upper age limit is relaxable by 05 years for SC / ST / SEBC / Women Category and 10 years for PwD.

✅ OSSSC Radiographer Recruitment 2024 Salary: ₹ 25500 - 81100, Pay Matrix Level-7, Cell-01

✅ OSSSC Radiographer Recruitment 2024 Eligibility Criteria:

(1) 10+2 Examination Passed with Science subject under Council of Higher Secondary Education, Odisha or equivalent.
(2) Diploma in Medical Laboratory Technology from Government recognized Institute / University.
(3) Higher Qualification (BMLT or MLT) are also eligible to apply OSSSC Radiographer 2023.
(4) A Candidate must have registered his / her name in Radiographers Council in the state and have valid registration certificate as on the date of advertisement.
(5) A Candidate must be able to speak, read and write Odia and must have passed the Middle School / Matric or equivalent qualification with Odida as a language subject.

✅ OSSSC Radiographer 2024 Exam Pattern:

✔️ The Written test for 100 Marks, the total duration is 02 Hours.
✔️ The Exam is Multiple Choice Questions type in OMR System or CBRT System in One Paper only.
✔️ The Test shall be negative marking @ 0.25 mark per question for wrong answer or multiple answers.
✔️ The Minimum Qualifying marks for SC, ST, Pwd, Ex-servicemen and Sportsperson category candidates shall be 30% and for other candidates it shall be 35%.

✅ How to Apply OSSSC Radiographer Recruitment 2024:

➢ Eligible Candidates should apply online through OSSSC Online Web Portal (www.osssc.gov.in).
➢ The candidates are required to fill all required details and upload Full Specimen Signature, recent passport size coloured photograph, Mark sheets etc.
➢ Applicants must have their own personal email ID and Mobile Number with validity till the completion of the recruitment process.
➢ The last date for registration of online applications is 15/04/2024 (Monday). The last date for submission of online applications is 20th April 2024.
