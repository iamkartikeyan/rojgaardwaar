GSSSB Additional Assistant Engineer Recruitment 2026 – Apply Online for 110 Posts | Last Date 29-06-2026

Published by: RozgarDwaar Editorial Desk | Updated: 2026-06-18
Apply Online (Official Website) Download Notification PDF Official Website

Gujarat Secondary Service Selection Board (GSSSB) invites online applications from eligible Diploma holders in Mechanical or Automobile Engineering for the post of Additional Assistant Engineer (Mechanical), Class-3 under Gujarat Water Supply and Sewerage Board (GWSSB).

The GSSSB Additional Assistant Engineer (Mechanical) Recruitment 2026 for 110 Vacancies Online Application process starts from 15th June 2026 and closes on 29th June 2026. This article provides complete information about GSSSB Additional Assistant Engineer Mechanical Recruitment 2026, including category-wise vacancies, eligibility criteria, pay scale, exam pattern, official notification PDF, and online application link.

How to Apply GSSSB Additional Assistant Engineer (Mechanical) Recruitment 2026?:

Eligible candidates must apply online only through the OJAS portal. The steps are as follows:-

  • Step 1: Visit the official OJAS website https://ojas.gujarat.gov.in.
  • Step 2: Click on “Apply” → Select GSSSB → Choose Advertisement No. 419/202627.
  • Step 3: Click on “Apply Now” and fill in Personal Details and Educational Details.
  • Step 4: Upload Photograph and Signature in the prescribed format.
  • Step 5: Review all details and click on “Confirm Application”.
  • Step 6: Pay the application fee online before 01 July 2026.
  • Step 7: Take a printout of the confirmed application form for future reference.
