Assam Police Constable Recruitment 2026 Apply Online for 1715 Vacancies | Last Date 16.01.2026

Published by: RozgarDwaar Editorial Desk | Updated: 2026-01-15
Apply Online (Official Website) Download Notification PDF Official Website

State Level Police Recruitment Board (SLPRB), Assam, Guwahati, has invited online applications from eligible 10th/12th passed candidates for recruitment of 1052 Constable (UB) and 663 Constable (AB) posts in Assam Police. Interested candidates can apply online from 16th December 2025 to 16th January 2026.

The Applicants must be permanent residents of Assam, registered with local Employment Exchange, and fluent in Assamese or any other state language. Selected candidates will have to submit proof of residence after final select list publication (exempt for SC/ST/OBC/MOBC).

Assam Police Constable 2026 Age Limit::
  • 18-25 years as on 01-01-2026 (born not before 01-01-2001 and not later than 01-01-2008).
  • Relaxations: SC/ST(P)/ST(H) - 5 years; OBC/MOBC - 3 years; Home Guards/VDPs/Civil Defence (3+ years service) - Additional 3 years; FMMOs/SPOs - Additional 10 years.
Assam Police Constable 2026 Eligibility Criteria::
  • Citizen of India and Permanent Resident of Assam (proof required post-selection, exempt for SC/ST/OBC/MOBC).
  • Registered with a local Employment Exchange in Assam.
  • Fluent in speaking Assamese or any other state language.
  • Educational: Constable (UB) - H.S. or Class XII from recognized Board/Council; Constable (AB) - H.S.L.C or Class X/Matriculation from recognized Board/Council.

How to Apply Assam Police Constable Recruitment 2026::

Applications must be submitted online through the official portal. Follow these steps carefully:

➢ Step 1: Visit the apply online link apcap.in starting from 16th December 2025.

➢ Step 2: Register once using exact name as per HSLC Certificate, Father's/Mother's name, valid mobile number, Employment Exchange Number, Highest Qualification, Permanent District, Gender, Caste/Category, Date of Birth, and Aadhaar Number. Multiple registrations will lead to cancellation.

➢ Step 3: After registration, an Application ID will be generated. Use it to complete the form and upload required documents.

➢ Step 4: Submit the application on or before 16th January 2026.
