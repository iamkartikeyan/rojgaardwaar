India Post GDS July Recruitment 2026 Apply Online for 23757 Posts | Last Date 21-09-2026

Published by: RozgarDwaar Editorial Desk | Updated: 2026-08-31
Apply Online (Official Website) Download Notification PDF Official Website

India Post (Department of Posts) has released the Gramin Dak Sevak (GDS) Online Engagement Schedule-II, July 2026 notification for engagement of Branch Postmaster (BPM), Assistant Branch Postmaster (ABPM) and Dak Sevak posts across various Postal Circles in India. More than 23,000 vacancies are expected to be filled on the basis of 10th standard marks without any written examination or interview.

The India Post GDS Recruitment 2026 one-time registration will start from 31st August 2026 and online application with fee payment will be accepted from 02nd September 2026 to 21st September 2026. This article provides complete details of India Post GDS July Vacancy 2026 including eligibility, TRCA, application fee, selection process and official apply link.

India Post GDS Recruitment 2026 Eligibility Criteria::
  • Age Limit: Minimum 18 years and Maximum 40 years. Age Relaxation: SC/ST: +5 years, OBC: +3 years, PwBD: +10 years.
  • Educational Qualification: Secondary School Examination (10th) pass with passing marks in Mathematics and English from a recognized Board. Candidate must have studied the local language at least up to 10th standard. Equivalent qualifications from other boards are not accepted.
India Post GDS 2026 Selection Process::

The selection will be made through the following process:

  • Automated Merit List prepared on the basis of 10th standard marks (accuracy up to 4 decimal places)
  • No weightage for higher education
  • Document Verification of shortlisted candidates
  • In case of tie: older candidate will be given preference, followed by category preferences
India Post GDS Recruitment 2026 Application Fee::
  • UR / OBC / EWS Male candidates: ₹100/-
  • Female candidates (all categories), SC, ST, PwBD and Transwomen candidates: Exempted (NIL)
  • Payment Method: Online (Credit Card / Debit Card / Net Banking / UPI)

How to Apply India Post GDS Recruitment 2026?:

Eligible candidates must apply online only through the official GDS portal. The steps are as follows:

  • Step 1: Visit https://www.indiapost.gov.in/gdsonlineengagement and complete One-Time Registration using mobile number and email ID (OTP verified).
  • Step 2: Enter Aadhaar (if available), Board and Year of 10th passing, and upload scanned photograph (less than 50 KB) and signature (less than 20 KB).
  • Step 3: Fill the application form, select Postal Circle/Division and post preferences carefully.
  • Step 4: Upload 10th certificate and other required documents.
  • Step 5: Pay the application fee (if applicable) online and submit the form. Take a printout of the submitted application for future reference.
