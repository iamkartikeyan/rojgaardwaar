SBI Specialist Cadre Officer Recruitment 2026 – Apply Online for 38 Various Posts | Last Date 27-08-2026

Published by: RozgarDwaar Editorial Desk | Updated: 2026-08-25
Apply Online (Official Website) Download Notification PDF Official Website

State Bank of India (SBI) has released three detailed notifications for recruitment of Specialist Cadre Officers (SCO) on Regular / Contractual basis under Advertisement Nos. CRPD/SCO/2026-27/11, CRPD/SCO/2026-27/12 and CRPD/SCO/2026-27/13. A total of 38 vacancies are available across various specialised posts in Cyber Security, AI, IT, HR, Enterprise Architecture and Technology domains, offering excellent career opportunities for experienced professionals.

The SBI SCO Recruitment 2026 online registration process began on 07 August 2026, and the last date to apply is 27 August 2026. On this page, banking job aspirants can find all important details of SBI Specialist Cadre Officer Vacancy 2026, including official notifications and application links.

SBI SCO Recruitment 2026 Eligibility Criteria::
  • For DVP – Cyber Innovation & Simulation Lab (Advt 11): BE/B.Tech or MCA/ME/M.Tech/M.Sc in any discipline + Preferred certifications (CISSP/CISM/CISA/CCSP/PMP/MBA). Minimum 12+ years experience (at least 6 years in Technology/Cyber Innovation related domains).
  • For DVP – Citizen Centric Initiatives / Cyber Security (Advt 11): BE/B.Tech or MCA/ME/M.Tech/M.Sc + Preferred certifications. Minimum 12 years in BFSI IT/Cyber Security (at least 6 years in relevant specialised areas).
  • For Deputy Manager – AI Specialists (Advt 11): B.Tech/B.E. (relevant streams with min 50%) or MCA or M.Tech/M.Sc (relevant) + 4 years post-qualification IT experience (out of which 2 years in ML/Deep Learning/GenAI/RAG/Cloud ML etc.).
  • For Deputy Manager – IT Security Expert / Cyber Security Analyst (Advt 11): B.Tech/B.E. (relevant with min 50%) or MCA or M.Tech/M.Sc + Minimum 4 years post-qualification experience in Cyber Security / IT Domain.
  • For Manager (Manpower Planning & Recruitment) / Manager (Learning & Development) (Advt 12): MBA/PGDM/PGDBM with specialisation in HR/HRD/HRM + Preferred certifications (HR Analytics/SHRM). Minimum 6 years post-qualification experience in HR (preferably IT/BFSI).
  • For Dy. CTO (Enterprise Architecture) (Advt 13): Engineering Graduate / MCA (preferred relevant streams) + Compulsory TOGAF Certification. Minimum 18 years IT experience in BFSI/Tech (at least 5 years senior executive + 8 years Architectural functions).
  • For Vice President (Enterprise & Technology Architect) (Advt 13): Engineering Graduate / MCA (preferred relevant streams) + Compulsory TOGAF Certification. Minimum 15 years IT experience in BFSI/Tech (at least 3 years senior executive + 5 years Architectural functions).

How to Apply for SBI SCO Recruitment 2026::

Eligible candidates must apply ONLINE ONLY through the official SBI website. No other mode of application will be accepted.

➢ Step 1: Visit the official website https://sbi.bank.in/web/careers/current-openings.

➢ Step 2: Click on the respective Advertisement No. (CRPD/SCO/2026-27/11 or 12 or 13) and then on “Apply Online”.

➢ Step 3: Complete the registration process by providing basic details and generate Registration Number & Password.

➢ Step 4: Fill the online application form carefully with personal, educational, experience and other required details.

➢ Step 5: Upload scanned copies of Photograph, Signature, Biodata, Resume, ID Proof, Date of Birth Proof, Educational Certificates, Experience Certificates, Caste/EWS/PwBD Certificate (if applicable), Preferred Certifications, Form-16/Offer Letter/Salary Slip and other documents as prescribed.

➢ Step 6: Pay the application fee online (if applicable) through Debit Card / Credit Card / Net Banking / UPI.

➢ Step 7: Submit the application form and take a printout of the system-generated confirmation page for future reference.

FAQs::
What is the total number of vacancies in SBI SCO Recruitment 2026?:

A total of 38 Specialist Cadre Officer posts are available under three advertisements (34 + 2 + 2).

What is the last date to apply for SBI SCO Recruitment 2026?:

The last date to submit the online application is 27 August 2026.

What is the age limit for SBI SCO Recruitment 2026?:

Age limit varies by post (ranging from 25–35 years for Deputy Managers to 42–50 years for Dy. CTO) as on 31.07.2026. Age relaxation is applicable as per government rules.

What is the educational qualification required for SBI SCO 2026?:

What is the selection process for SBI SCO 2026?:

Shortlisting of applications followed by Interview (and CTC Negotiation for contractual posts). No written exam.

What is the application fee for SBI SCO 2026?:

₹750 for General/EWS/OBC candidates and Nil for SC/ST/PwBD candidates.

How to apply for SBI SCO Recruitment 2026?:

Candidates must apply online only through the respective links on the official SBI website between 07 August 2026 and 27 August 2026.
