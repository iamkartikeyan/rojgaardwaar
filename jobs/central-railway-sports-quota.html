Central Railway Sports Quota Recruitment 2026 – Apply Online for 59 Group C & D Posts | Last Date 14-08-2026

Published by: RozgarDwaar Editorial Desk | Updated: 2026-08-13
Apply Online (Official Website) Download Notification PDF Official Website

Central Railway, under the Ministry of Railways, Government of India, has released Employment Notice No. RRC/CR/01/2026 for recruitment against the Sports Quota for the year 2026-27. Online applications are invited from eligible Indian sportspersons for 59 Group 'C' and Level-1 (Erstwhile Group 'D') posts in various sports disciplines.

The Central Railway Sports Quota Recruitment 2026 for 59 Vacancies Online Application process starts from 15th July 2026 and closes on 14th August 2026. This article provides complete information about Central Railway Sports Quota 2026, including eligibility criteria, vacancy details, official notification, and online application procedure.

  • The Fee payment will have to be made online through payment gateway as part of online application process.

How to Apply Central Railway Sports Quota Recruitment 2026?:

Eligible candidates must apply online only through the official portal. The online application steps are as follows:-

  • Step 1: Visit the RRC Central Railway website rrccr.com from 15 July 2026 (10:00 AM).
  • Step 2: Register using a valid email ID and mobile number to obtain login credentials.
  • Step 3: Log in and fill the online application form with personal details, educational qualifications, and sports achievements (name, father’s name, and date of birth must match certificates).
  • Step 4: Upload scanned copies of recent color photograph, signature, educational certificates (10th/12th/Degree), sports certificates, and Aadhaar card.
  • Step 5: Pay the application fee online (if applicable) before 14 August 2026 (6:00 PM).
  • Step 6: Review, submit the application, download and print the confirmation page for future reference.
