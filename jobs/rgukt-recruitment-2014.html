RGUKT Recruitment 2023: 660 Faculty Vacancies - Notification, Apply Online

Published by: RozgarDwaar Editorial Desk | Updated: 2023-11-20
Apply Online (Official Website) Download Notification PDF Official Website

Rajiv Gandhi University of Knowledge Technologies (RGUKT) Recruitment Notification of Faculty Posts 2023-24: The RGUKT University invites applications online from Indian citizens and also Overseas Citizens of India (OCIs) for recruitment of Professor, Associate Professor, Assistant Professor and Lecturer Posts. recruitments.universities.ap.gov.in Website is a official Online Application Portal for RGUKT Faculty Jobs 2023.

On this page, job seekers can find the latest RGUKT Recruitment Notification for regular, backlog, S/ST, and BC category vacancies. Postgraduate degree holders and Ph.D. scholars are eligible to apply for Andhra University Teaching Recruitment 2023. The last date for registration of online applications is 20th November 2023.

RGUKT Recruitment 2023 Eligibility Criteria::

Educational Qualifications:

Professor and Associate Professor:

(1) A good academic record, with a Ph.D. Degree in relevant discipline.
(2) A Master‘s Degree in relevant discipline with at least 55% marks (or an equivalent grade on a point-scale, wherever the grading system is followed.

Assistant Professor:

(1) A Master‘s degree with 55% marks (or an equivalent grade in a point-scale wherever the grading system is followed) from an Indian University, or an equivalent degree from an accredited foreign university.
(2) The candidate must have cleared the National Eligibility Test (NET) conducted by the UGC or the CSIR, or AP - SLET/AP SET. (OR) Who have been awarded a Ph. D. Degree exempted from NET/AP-SLET/AP-SET.

Experience:

✔️ For Professor: A minimum of ten years of teaching experience in university/ college as Assistant Professor/ Associate Professor/ Professor, and / or research experience at equivalent level.
✔️ For Associate Professor: A minimum 08 years experience in teaching and / or research in an academic/ research position equivalent.

RGUKT Recruitment 2023 Salary::

✔️ Professor: Level–14 ₹ 1,44,200 - 2,18,200
✔️ Assistant Professor Level–10 ₹ 57,700 - 1,82,400
✔️ Associate Professor Level–13A ₹ 1,31,400 - 2,17,100

RGUKT Recruitment 2023 Selection Process::

✔️ Personal Interview
✔️ Documents Verification.

RGUKT Recruitment 2023 Application Fee::

✔️ For Professor: ₹ 3000 for all categories.
✔️ For Associate Professor: ₹ 3000 for all categories.
✔️ For Assistant Professor: ₹ 2500 for General / BC / EWS, ₹ 2000 for SC / ST / PBDs.

How to Apply RGUKT Recruitment 2023::
  • Eligible candidates should apply online through the AP University Recruitment Portal.
  • Applicants are required to apply online using the following link: http://recruitments.universities.ap.gov.in.
  • Candidates should create a username and password through mobile number verification.
  • They should also upload scanned copies of all supporting documents during the online registration.
  • The last date for the registration of online applications is 20/11/2023, up to 5:00 PM IST.
