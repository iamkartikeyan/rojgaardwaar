Assistant Professor Salary in Government Colleges 2026:

The Assistant Professor salary in government colleges follows the 7th Pay Commission with Academic Level 10 (entry pay ₹57,700). Along with basic pay, government Assistant Professors receive Dearness Allowance (DA), House Rent Allowance (HRA), medical benefits, provident fund, research grants, and opportunities for promotions to Associate Professor and Professor levels.

Assistant Professor Eligibility Criteria 2026 (UGC Guidelines):

To apply for government Assistant Professor jobs 2026, candidates must meet UGC-prescribed qualifications:

  • Educational Qualification: Master’s degree in the relevant subject with at least 55% marks (50% for SC/ST/OBC/PwD candidates).
  • NET/SET/PhD: Clearance of UGC NET, SET/SLET, or PhD (as per UGC Regulations 2009/2016/2018) is mandatory for most positions. PhD holders are often exempt from NET requirement.
  • Additional relaxations and reservations apply as per Government of India rules.
