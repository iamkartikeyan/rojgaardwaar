The MECON Executive Recruitment 2026 online registration process began on 15 August 2026, and the last date to apply is 13 September 2026. On this page, job aspirants can find all important details of MECON Manager Vacancy 2026, including the official notification and application link.

MECON Executive Recruitment 2026 Eligibility Criteria::
  • Post Code 01 – Deputy Manager (MAS) E-2: Post Graduation in Economics (Mathematics as one subject up to Graduation level) + Minimum 05 years post-qualification experience in forecasting using econometric modelling/statistical tools, micro & macro-economics, socio-economic studies, social cost-benefit analysis and economic policies.
  • Post Code 02 – Deputy Manager (MAS) E-2: Engineering Graduate Degree with MBA/PGDM (02 years duration) + Minimum 05 years post-qualification experience in market survey/research (demand & supply analysis of industrial products/metals/minerals/power/energy/traffic etc.) and forecasting using econometric modelling/statistical tools.
  • Post Code 03 – Manager (MAS) E-3: Engineering Graduate Degree with MBA/PGDM (02 years duration) + Minimum 09 years post-qualification experience in cost-estimation, project appraisal, building financial models, forecasting and budgeting on Metals/Mining/Energy/Ports/Other Infrastructure projects.
  • Post Code 04 (A) – Deputy Manager (Architecture) E-2: Bachelor in Architecture (preference to graduates from Institutes of National Importance) + Minimum 05 years post-qualification experience in design of Industrial/Infrastructure facilities with exposure to Revit, AutoCAD (3D views, Walkthrough, estimation preferred).
  • Post Code 04 (B) – Manager (Architecture) E-3: Same qualification as above + Minimum 09 years post-qualification experience in design of Industrial/Infrastructure facilities with exposure to Revit, AutoCAD (3D views, Walkthrough, estimation preferred).
  • Post Code 05 – Manager (Civil & Structural) E-3: B.E./B.Tech. in Civil/Mechanical (preference to Institutes of National Importance; PG Diploma/Certificate in Construction Management preferred) + Minimum 09 years post-qualification experience (out of which at least 06 years in construction of Industrial/Infrastructural Steel Structural works at site).
  • Post Code 06 – Senior Manager (Civil & Structural) E-4: Same qualification as above + Minimum 13 years post-qualification experience (out of which at least 09 years in construction of Industrial/Infrastructural Steel Structural works at site).

Note: Educational Qualification and Post Qualification Work Experience as on 08.07.2026.

How to Apply for MECON Executive Recruitment 2026::

Eligible candidates must apply ONLINE ONLY through the official MECON website. No other mode of application will be accepted.

➢ Step 1: Visit the official website www.meconlimited.co.in → Careers → Career Opportunities → Click here to apply Online (or directly visit https://careers.meconlimited.co.in/).

➢ Step 2: Register and fill the online application form carefully with personal, educational and experience details.

➢ Step 3: Upload scanned copies of recent passport size colour photograph (jpg/jpeg, max 40 KB), signature (jpg/jpeg, max 40 KB) and self-attested documents (PDF) including age proof, educational certificates, mark sheets, experience certificates, PAN, Aadhaar, caste/PwD certificates (as applicable).

➢ Step 4: Pay the application fee online (if applicable) through the payment gateway.

➢ Step 5: Submit the application form and take a printout of the confirmation page for future reference.
