Sainik School Balachadi Recruitment 2026 Eligibility Criteria::
  • PGT Posts (Physics, Chemistry, Biology & English): Master’s Degree in the concerned subject with minimum 50% marks + B.Ed. with 50% marks (or equivalent integrated courses). Proficiency in English and Hindi medium is mandatory.
  • TGT Posts (English, Hindi, Science, Maths): Bachelor’s Degree with minimum 50% marks in concerned subject + B.Ed. with 50% marks. CTET/TET Paper-2 qualified candidates will be preferred. Must have studied the subject as main/elective for at least 3 years/6 semesters.
  • Art Master: Master’s Degree in Fine Arts (Painting/Sketching) OR Graduate with 4-year Diploma in Fine Art/Drawing & Painting from a recognized institute.
  • Lower Division Clerk (LDC): Matriculation + Typing speed of 40 WPM in English. Knowledge of shorthand and computer will be an added advantage.
  • General Employees: Matriculation pass. Multi-skilled candidates with experience will be preferred.
  • School Medical Officer: MBBS Degree from a recognized university. Must be ready to reside in the campus.
  • Nursing Sister: Nursing Diploma/Degree with minimum 5 years of experience.
  • Physical Training Instructor cum Matron (Female): B.P.Ed. or Graduation + Diploma in Physical Education from a recognized university.
  • Age Limit (as on 01 October 2026): PGTs: 21 to 40 years,  TGTs & Art Master: 21 to 35 years, LDC & General Employees: 18 to 50 years, Contractual Posts: 18/21 to 50 years. Age relaxation applicable as per Sainik Schools Society rules.

How to Apply for Sainik School Balachadi Recruitment 2026::

Interested and eligible candidates must apply through Offline Mode only. Follow the steps given below:

➢ Step 1: Download the official notification from the link given above and read all the details carefully.

➢ Step 2: Prepare a neatly typed/handwritten application on plain paper mentioning all required details.

➢ Step 3: Attach one recent passport-size photograph, self-attested copies of all educational certificates, experience certificates, and other relevant documents.

➢ Step 4: Prepare a Demand Draft of ₹500/- in favour of “Principal, Sainik School Balachadi” payable at Jamnagar.

➢ Step 5: Send the complete application along with all documents and Demand Draft to the following address:

Principal,
Sainik School Balachadi,
Jamnagar, Gujarat – 361230

➢ Step 6: Write “Application for the post of _____________” on the top of the envelope. The application must reach on or before 10 August 2026 (upto 06:00 PM).
