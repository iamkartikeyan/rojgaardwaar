The IBPS Clerk XVI 2026 online registration process began on 01 August 2026, and the last date to apply online is extended up to 28 August 2026.

IBPS CSA Recruitment 2026 Eligibility Criteria::
  • Educational Qualification (as on 21.08.2026): A Degree (Graduation) in any discipline from a University recognised by the Govt. of India or any equivalent qualification recognized as such by the Central Government. The candidate must possess valid Mark-sheet / Degree Certificate that he/she is a graduate on the day of registration.
  • Computer Literacy: Operating and working knowledge in computer systems is mandatory. Candidates should have Certificate/Diploma/Degree in computer operations/Language or should have studied Computer / Information Technology as one of the subjects in the High School/College/Institute.
  • Local Language Proficiency: Candidates applying for vacancies of a particular State/UT must be proficient in reading, writing, speaking and understanding the specified/opted local language of that State/UT.
  • Age Limit (as on 01.08.2026): Minimum 20 years and Maximum 28 years (A candidate must have been born not earlier than 02.08.1998 and not later than 01.08.2006 – both dates inclusive).
  • Age Relaxation: SC/ST – 5 years, OBC – 3 years, PwBD – 10 years, and other categories as per Government of India rules.
IBPS CSA Salary 2026::
  • Pay Scale: ₹24,050 – 1,340/3 – 28,070 – 1,650/3 – 33,020 – 2,000/4 – 41,020 – 2,340/7 – 57,400 – 4,400/1 – 61,800 – 2,680/1 – 64,480
  • In-hand salary of an IBPS CSA (Clerk) is approximately ₹35,000 – ₹40,000 per month after including DA, HRA, Special Allowance and other benefits (varies as per posting location).
IBPS CSA Recruitment 2026 Selection Process::
  • Online Preliminary Examination
  • Online Mains Examination
  • Local Language Proficiency Test (LLPT)

Final Merit: Based on marks obtained in the Mains Examination only (100% weightage). There is no interview.

How to Apply for IBPS CSA Recruitment 2026::

Eligible candidates must apply ONLINE ONLY through the official IBPS website. No other mode of application will be accepted.

➢ Step 1: Visit the official website www.ibps.in.

➢ Step 2: Click on the link “CRP CSA-XVI” or “Apply Online for Customer Service Associate”.

➢ Step 3: Complete the registration process by providing basic details and generate Registration Number & Password.

➢ Step 4: Fill the online application form carefully with personal, educational and other required details.

➢ Step 5: Upload scanned copies of Photograph, Signature, Left Thumb Impression and Handwritten Declaration as per the prescribed format.

➢ Step 6: Pay the application fee online through Debit Card / Credit Card / Net Banking / UPI.

➢ Step 7: Submit the application form and take a printout of the confirmation page for future reference.
