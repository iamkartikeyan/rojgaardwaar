How to get Computer Jobs in Government Sector?

rozgardwaar.com listing latest Computer based Government Jobs and Data Entry Operator Jobs in Govt Sector. Indian Citizens who have passed 10+2 with Computer Science Subject or Diploma in Computer are eligible to apply.

What is the Computer Job?

Computer Operators is a unit of work execution, who collecting office data’s in different computer databases, manage and maintain effective record keeping. They also maintain the computers in Office and also responsible for computer based office works.

Which Government Sector wants Data Entry Operators?

Various Government Sector Organizations, Institutions requires Computer Operators and DEO Operators.

✔️ Public Sector Banks
✔️ Indian Railways
✔️ State Government Services
✔️ Central Government Departments

What is the Minimum Qualification for Computer / Data Entry Jobs?

✔️ Matriculation or 12th Class Pass with Computer Knowledge.
✔️ Diploma in Computer Application or equivalent.
✔️ ITI in PASAA or COPA.
✔️ Bachelor’s Degree in Computer Science or equivalent computer course.
