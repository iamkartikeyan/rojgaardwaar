North Central Railway Cultural Quota Recruitment 2026 – Apply Online for 02 Posts | Last Date 19-09-2026

Published by: RozgarDwaar Editorial Desk | Updated: 2026-08-20
Apply Online (Official Website) Download Notification PDF Official Website

North Central Railway (NCR), Railway Recruitment Cell, Prayagraj has released Notification No. EN CQ-02/2026 for recruitment against Cultural Quota (Open Advertisement) for the year 2026-27. A total of 02 vacancies are available in Level-2 (Grade Pay ₹1900/-) for Sitar and Tabla streams.

The North Central Railway Cultural Quota Recruitment 2026 online application process began on 20 August 2026, and the last date to apply is 19 September 2026 (up to 23:59 hrs). On this page, aspirants can find all important details of NCR Cultural Quota Vacancy 2026, including the official notification and application link.

NCR Cultural Quota Recruitment 2026 Eligibility Criteria::
  • Educational Qualification: Intermediate or equivalent examination passed with 50% aggregate marks from any recognized Board/University. The 50% marks condition is not insisted upon for SC/ST/Ex-Servicemen/PwBD candidates and those possessing higher qualification (Graduation/Post Graduation). OR Passed Matriculation from recognized Board plus ITI approved by NCVT/SCVT. For Technical Posts: Matriculation/SSLC plus ITI from recognized institutions of NCVT/SCVT (OR) Matriculation/SSLC plus Course Completed Act Apprenticeship.
  • Cultural Qualification: Candidates must possess the required proficiency in the relevant cultural stream (Sitar / Tabla) as assessed during Practical Demonstration and Testimonials/Prizes.
  • Age Limit (as on 01.01.2027): 18 to 30 years (born not earlier than 01.01.1997 and not later than 01.01.2009).
  • Age Relaxation: OBC – 03 years, SC/ST – 05 years, PwBD – 10 years (provided the post is identified as suitable for PwBD).
NCR Cultural Quota Recruitment 2026 Selection Process::
  • Written Test – 50 Marks
  • Assessment of Talent (Practical Demonstration – 35 Marks + Testimonials/Prizes – 15 Marks)
  • Total: 100 Marks
  • Written Test Details: 50 Objective Type Questions (General Awareness, Mathematics, General Intelligence, Reasoning and General Knowledge on Cultural Activities). Duration: 60 minutes. Bilingual (Hindi & English). Negative marking of 1/3rd marks for each wrong answer.
NCR Cultural Quota Application Fee 2026::
  • General / OBC: ₹500/-
  • SC / ST / Ex-Servicemen / PwBD / Transgender / Female / Minorities / Economically Backward Classes: ₹250/- (refundable, after deducting bank charges, to those who appear in the written examination)

How to Apply for NCR Cultural Quota Recruitment 2026::

Eligible candidates must apply ONLINE ONLY through the official RRC/PRYJ website. No other mode of application will be accepted.

➢ Step 1: Visit the official website www.rrcpryj.org or direct apply link https://rrcweb.tech/cultural.

➢ Step 2: Read the detailed notification carefully before applying.

➢ Step 3: Fill the online application form with personal details, BIO-DATA, educational and cultural qualifications.

➢ Step 4: Upload scanned colour photograph (3.5 cm × 3.5 cm, JPEG, 20-30 KB) and signature (JPEG, 10-15 KB).

➢ Step 5: Upload relevant certificates in PDF format whose details are filled in the application.

➢ Step 6: Pay the application fee online and submit the form.

➢ Step 7: Note down the Registration Number and take a printout of the application for future reference.
