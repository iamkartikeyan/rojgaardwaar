INMAS DRDO Recruitment 2026: Apply for 15 Research Associate (RA) & Junior Research Fellow (JRF) Posts. Institute of Nuclear Medicine and Allied Sciences (INMAS), Delhi under Defence Research and Development Organisation (DRDO) has officially released the advertisement for the award of 04 Research Associate (RA) and 11 Junior Research Fellow (JRF) positions. This is a prestigious opportunity for young and meritorious Indian Nationals to pursue defence-related research in Life Sciences, Chemistry, Pharmaceutical Sciences and Dental Sciences at a premier DRDO laboratory.

Eligible candidates can send their duly filled application by email. The last date to submit application is 30 June 2026. Interviews will be conducted in July 2026. Shortlisted candidates will be informed through email.

INMAS DRDO RA & JRF 2026 Emoluments & Benefits:

Research Associate (RA):

  • ₹67,000/- + HRA per month (for entire 2-year tenure)
  • Contingency grant: ₹20,000/- per annum

Junior Research Fellow (JRF):

  • ₹37,000/- + HRA per month (1st & 2nd year)
  • ₹42,000/- + HRA per month (3rd to 5th year as SRF, subject to performance)
  • Contingency grant: ₹15,000/- per annum (JRF) / ₹20,000/- per annum (SRF)

Tenure: RA – 2 years (non-extendable); JRF – 5 years (2 years JRF + 3 years SRF on satisfactory performance).

INMAS DRDO RA & JRF 2026 Eligibility Criteria:

Age Limit (as on closing date of application):

  • RA: Maximum 35 years
  • JRF: Maximum 28 years

Age relaxation: SC/ST – 5 years, OBC – 3 years and other entitled categories as per GoI rules.

Educational Qualification:

  • RA-01 (Life Sciences): Ph.D. in Life Sciences / Biotechnology / Microbiology / Biochemistry / Molecular Biology + desirable experience in cell culture, molecular techniques, LC-MS etc.
  • RA-02 (Chemistry): Ph.D. in Material Science / Chemistry / Chemical Science + experience in nanomaterials, polymers etc.
  • RA-03 (Pharmaceutical Sciences): Ph.D. in Pharmaceutical Sciences / Pharmaceutics / Pharmacology / Medicinal Chemistry etc.
  • JRF-01 (Life Sciences): First Class M.Sc. / B.Tech. / M.Tech. in relevant subjects with NET/GATE qualification (or exempted as per rules).
  • JRF-02 (Chemistry): First Class M.Sc. / B.Tech. / M.Tech. in relevant subjects with NET/GATE qualification.
  • JRF-03 (Pharmaceutics): First Class M.Pharm. / M.S.(Pharm.) / M.Tech.(Pharm.) with NET/GPAT.
  • JRF-04 (Dental Sciences): First Class BDS with valid DCI / State Dental Council registration.

How to Apply for INMAS DRDO RA & JRF Recruitment 2026:
  1. Download the Application Form from the official DRDO website https://drdo.gov.in
  2. Fill the form completely with all required details
  3. Attach self-attested copies of all certificates / testimonials
  4. Send the scanned application form along with supporting documents by email to the address mentioned in the official notification
  5. Candidates must bring original documents and one set of self-attested photocopies on the date of interview
  6. Reporting time: 08:00 AM to 09:30 AM at Main Gate Reception, INMAS, Timarpur, Delhi
