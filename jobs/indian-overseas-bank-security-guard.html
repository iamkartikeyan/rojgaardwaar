Indian Overseas Bank Security Guard Recruitment 2026 – Apply Online for 25 Posts | Last Date 14-09-2026

Published by: RozgarDwaar Editorial Desk | Updated: 2026-09-02
Apply Online (Official Website) Download Notification PDF Official Website

Indian Overseas Bank (IOB), a leading Public Sector Bank with headquarters in Chennai, has released the detailed notification for recruitment of Security Guards from eligible Ex-Servicemen/ Ex-Agniveers for appointment in Chennai/ Tamil Nadu. A total of 25 vacancies are to be filled under Advertisement No. HRMD/SS/RECT/01/2026-27, offering a great opportunity for eligible Ex-Servicemen and Ex-Agniveers.

The IOB Security Guard Recruitment 2026 online registration process began on 25 August 2026, and the last date to apply is 14 September 2026. On this page, job aspirants can find all important details of IOB Security Guard Vacancy 2026, including the official notification and application link.

IOB Security Guard Recruitment 2026 Eligibility Criteria::
  • Who Can Apply: Only eligible Ex-Servicemen / Ex-Agniveers (excluding disabled Ex-Servicemen).
  • Educational Qualification: A pass in 10th standard (Matriculation) or its equivalent examination. The candidate should not have passed Graduation examination or its equivalent. The candidate should be able to speak, read and write in Tamil language.
  • Age Limit (as on 01.08.2026): Minimum 18 years and Maximum 26 years.
  • Age Relaxation for Ex-Servicemen:
    • General: 26 Years + Actual period of service rendered in the defence forces + 3 Years (Maximum 45 Years)
    • SC/ST: 31 Years + Actual period of service + 3 Years (Maximum 45 Years)
    • OBC: 29 Years + Actual period of service + 3 Years (Maximum 45 Years)
    • Agniveer: 21 Years + Actual Period of service as Agniveer + 3 Years
IOB Security Guard Salary 2026::

Pay Scale (Subordinate Staff Cadre as per 12th Bipartite Settlement): ₹19,500 – 665/4 – 22,160 – 830/5 – 26,310 – 990/4 – 30,270 – 1,170/3 – 33,780 – 1,345/3 – 37,815

Basic pay will be fixed based on the last drawn salary during defence service. In addition, selected candidates are entitled to emoluments as per the Industry-wide Bipartite Settlement and Bank policies/rules.

How to Apply for IOB Security Guard Recruitment 2026::

Eligible candidates must apply ONLINE ONLY through the official IOB website / IBPS registration portal. No other mode of application will be accepted.

➢ Step 1: Visit the official website www.iob.bank.in and go to the “Careers” page. Click on the link “Recruitment of Security Guards” and then “Apply Online”.

➢ Step 2: Click on “Click here to Register Online” and complete the registration process. Note down the Registration Number and Password (also sent via Email/SMS).

➢ Step 3: Login and fill the online application form carefully with all required personal, educational and service details.

➢ Step 4: Upload Photograph, Signature, Left Thumb Impression and Handwritten Declaration as per the prescribed specifications. Capture live photo in the portal.

➢ Step 5: Submit the application by clicking “COMPLETE REGISTRATION” and take a printout of the system-generated application form for future reference.
