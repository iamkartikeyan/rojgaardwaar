KPSC Veterinary Officer Recruitment 2024 Apply Online - 400 Vacancies

Published by: RozgarDwaar Editorial Desk | Updated: 2024-08-06
Apply Online (Official Website) Download Notification PDF Official Website

Karnataka Veterinary Officer Recruitment 2024 Notification for 400 Veterinary Officer Posts: Karnataka Public Service Commission (KPSC), Bengaluru invites online applications from eligible candidates for Veterinary Officers, Group-A posts in Department of Animal Husbandry and Fisheries. The KPSC Veterinary Officer Vacancy 2024 Online Registration start from 12th August 2024 to 12th September 2024.

KPSC Veterinary Officer Recruitment 2024 Notification:

Name of the Post

No of Vacancy

Veterinary Officer

400

✅ Age Limit:

Minimum 18 years.
Maximum 35 years for General Category.
Maximum 38 years for Category 2A, 2B, 3A & 3B.
Maximum 40 years for SC/ST/ Category - 1.

✅ Monthly Salary:

✔️ The KPSC Veterinary Officer consolidated monthly salary is ₹ 52640 - 97100/- per month.

✅ Eligibility Criteria:

(1) Must be a holder of degree in B.V.Sc / B.V.Sc & AH.
(2) Must have registered in KVC / IVC.

✅ Selection Process:

Computer Based Recruitment Test (CBRT)

Documents Verification

✅ How to Apply:

Eligible and Interested candidates should apply online for Veterinary Officers through Karnataka KPSC Official website kpsc.kar.nic.in from 12th August 2024. The last date for registration of online applications is 12/09/2024.

For any queries, contact helpline phone number: 080-30574957 / 30574901.
