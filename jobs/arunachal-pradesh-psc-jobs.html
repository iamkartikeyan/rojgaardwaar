Arunachal Pradesh PSC Jobs 2024: Apply Online for 140 Vacancies, Notification

Published by: RozgarDwaar Editorial Desk | Updated: 2026-04-13
Apply Online (Official Website) Download Notification PDF Official Website

Latest and Upcoming Arunachal Pradesh Govt Jobs 2026 Free Job Alert for both Fresher and Experienced Indian Citizens: The Arunachal Pradesh Public Service Commission (APPSC) invites online applications from eligible Graduate, Post Graduate, Diploma, ITI and 10th 12th Pass for recruitment of various level positions in Arunachal Pradesh State Departments.

Arunachal Pradesh Govt Jobs 2026 – Recent Updates:
    About APPSC::

    The Arunachal Pradesh Public Service Commission (APPSC) Itanangar conducts different competitive examinations (APCS, APPS, Forest Service, Educational Services) and interviews to recruit various state level state-level administrative and civil service positions. The APPSC Official Website named as appsc.gov.in.

    North Eastern Regional Institute of Science and Technology (NERIST) [Location: Nirjuli, Papum Pare District]

    Oil and Natural Gas Corporation (ONGC) [Location: Likabali, West Siang District]
    Hydro Power Development Corporation of Arunachal Pradesh (HPDCAP) [Location: Itanagar, Arunachal Pradesh]
    Arunachal Pradesh State Industrial and Investment Corporation Limited (APSICL) [Location: Itanagar, Arunachal Pradesh]
