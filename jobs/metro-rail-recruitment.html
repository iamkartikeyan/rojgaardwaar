Frequently Asked Questions::

✅ How many Metro Rails in India?

Currently, 13 Indian Cities functioning Metro Railway networks. These cities are Kolkata, Delhi, Gurgaon, Bengaluru, Chennai, Mumbai, Hyderabad, Jaipur, Noida, Kochi, Lucknow, Ahmedabad and Nagpur. Navi Mumbai Metro, Pune Metro, Madhya Pradesh Metro, Kanpur Metro, Agra Metro, Meerut Metro and Surat Metro are under construction.

✅ What is the Qualification for Metro Railway Jobs?

Both Fresher and Experienced Graduates, Post Graduates, Professionals in required fields, 10th 12th Pass, ITI, Diploma and Engineering candidates are eligible to apply for Metro Rail vacancies.

✅ How to Apply for Metro Rail Jobs?

Eligible Indian Citizens visit our RozgarDwaar blog Metro Rail Recruitment page, Get latest Metro Rail Job notifications. Click Metro Rail Corporation website link to apply particular job.

✅ What type of jobs in Metro Railway?

Metro Rail Corporations requires eligible candidates for recruitment / engagement of Directors, Managerial level posts, Officers, Assistants, Metro Rail Driver, Token Operator, Engineers and Technicians.

✅ What are upcoming Metro Rails in India?

Currently. more than 05 Rail Projects under constructions. Such as Navi Mumbai Metro (NMM), Pune Metro Rail, Madhya Pradesh Metro Rail, Uttar Pradesh Metro Rail (Kanpur Metro, Agra Metro, Meerut Metro Projects) and Gujarat Metro Rail.
