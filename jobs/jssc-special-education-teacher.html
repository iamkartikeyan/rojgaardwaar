JSSC Special Education Teacher Recruitment 2026 (3451 Posts) Online Form, Notification | Last Date 05.02.2026

Published by: RozgarDwaar Editorial Desk | Updated: 2026-02-04
Apply Online (Official Website) Download Notification PDF Official Website

Jharkhand Staff Selection Commission (JSSC) has released Advertisement No. 08/2025 (JIGTSEATCCE-2025) for recruitment to the post of Special Education Trained Assistant Teacher in Government Schools of Jharkhand for Classes 1 to 8. Eligible candidates can apply online from 14 December 2025 to 5th February 2026 through the official JSSC portal.

The Candidates are strongly advised to carefully read the detailed official notification before applying to confirm eligibility regarding age, qualification, category, and other criteria. Age relaxation applicable as per Jharkhand Government guidelines.

How to Apply JSSC Special Education Teacher Recruitment 2025:

Eligible candidates must apply ONLINE only through the JSSC official website. No other mode of application will be accepted.

➢ Step 1: Visit https://jssc.jharkhand.gov.in and go to the application section (from 14.12.2025).

➢ Step 2: Complete One-Time Registration (if not already registered).

➢ Step 3: Login and fill the online application form for Advt No. 08/2025 (JIGTSEATCCE-2025).

➢ Step 4: Upload scanned copies of photograph, signature, and required certificates/documents.

➢ Step 5: Pay the application fee online and submit the application before 05.02.2026 (11:59 PM).

➢ Step 6: Take printout of the finally submitted application form for future reference.
