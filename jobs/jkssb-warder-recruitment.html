JKSSB Warder Recruitment 2026: Apply Online for 288 Posts | Last Date 25-08-2026

Published by: RozgarDwaar Editorial Desk | Updated: 2026-08-24
Apply Online (Official Website) Download Notification PDF Official Website

Jammu & Kashmir Services Selection Board (JKSSB) invites online applications from eligible domicile candidates of UT of Jammu & Kashmir for direct recruitment to 288 UT Cadre posts of Warder in the Home Department (J&K Prisons Department) under Advertisement Notification No. 05 of 2026 dated 06.07.2026.

The JKSSB Warder Recruitment 2026 for 288 Vacancies Online Registration process starts from 27th July 2026 and closes on 25th August 2026. This article provides complete information about JKSSB Warder 2026, including eligibility criteria, vacancy details, official notification PDF, and online application link.

JKSSB Warder 2026 Selection Process::

The selection on the basis of following steps: -

  • Written Examination (Objective MCQ with negative marking)
  • Physical Standard Test (PST) – Qualifying
  • Physical Endurance Test (PET) – Qualifying
  • Medical Examination
  • Document Verification
JKSSB Warder 2026 Application Fee::
  • All other candidates: ₹700/-
  • SC / ST-1 / ST-2 / EWS candidates: ₹600/-
  • Payment Method: Online Mode only (Net Banking / Credit / Debit Card).

How to Apply JKSSB Warder Recruitment 2026?:

Eligible candidates must apply online only through the JKSSB portal. The online registration steps are follows:-

  • Step 1: Visit the official application portal and register using valid email and mobile number.
  • Step 2: Fill the online application form carefully with personal, educational, category, and domicile details.
  • Step 3: Upload scanned copies of Photograph, Signature, educational certificates, domicile certificate, and category certificate (if applicable).
  • Step 4: Pay the application fee online and submit the form.
  • Step 5: Take a printout of the system-generated application form for future reference.
