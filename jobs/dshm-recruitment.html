DSHM Recruitment 2026 - Online Form for 144 Various Contract Posts | Last Date 12th May

Published by: RozgarDwaar Editorial Desk | Updated: 2026-05-11
Apply Online (Official Website) Download Notification PDF Official Website

Delhi State Health Mission (DSHM) has published recruitment notification DSHM Various Posts Vacancy 2026 for fill up 144 Vacancies. BDS / Graduate / Post Graduate / MBBS / LLB / MCA / B.Tech qualified candidates with required experience are eligible to apply for Dental Surgeon, Field Monitor, Public Health Manager, Lady Health Visitor and other contractual posts under different health programmes. The DSHM online application has started on 28 April 2026, and eligible candidates can apply until 12 May 2026 (till 5:00 PM).

Eligible Candidates must check the complete details regarding Post wise Vacancies, eligibility, application fee, selection process and the application process for the DSHM Various Posts Recruitment 2026 provided below.

DSHM Various Posts Recruitment 2026:
rozgardwaar.com:

Post Name

Dental Surgeon / Field Monitor / Public Health Manager / Lady Health Visitor / District Quality Assurance Coordinator / District Program Manager / District IEC/BCC Officer / Nutritionist / Medical Lecturer / DBA / Quality Assurance Consultant / State IEC/BCC Consultant / Legal Consultant

Total Vacancies

144 Posts (UR-72, OBC-35, SC-20, ST-8, EWS-9)

Age Limit

Post-wise (up to 45 years max) as on closing date (Relaxation as per Delhi Govt rules)

Salary

₹28,853 – ₹85,050 per month (Post-wise Consolidated)

Minimum Qualification

BDS / Graduate / PG / MBBS / LLB / MCA / B.Tech + Relevant Experience (post-wise)

Application Fee

Nil (No Application Fee)

Selection Process

Scrutiny / Shortlisting + Document Verification + Personal Interview

Job Location

Delhi

Last Date

12 May 2026 (05:00 PM)

DSHM Various Posts Recruitment 2026 Eligibility Criteria::

Age Limit (as on closing date): Post-wise maximum age limit mentioned below (relaxation as per Govt. of NCT of Delhi / GoI norms – 5 years SC/ST, 3 years OBC-NCL, 10 years PwBD).

Qualification & Experience (Post-wise):

  • Dental Surgeon (25 Posts): BDS + 2 years post-qualification experience (or 1 year in Public Hospital) + Delhi Dental Council registration. Age: 35 years.
  • Field Monitor (33 Posts): Graduate + 3 years field experience in Immunization/Pulse Polio/health programs + own transport. Age: Up to 45 years.
  • Public Health Manager (33 Posts): Graduate (Dental/AYUSH/Nursing/Life/Social Science) + Master in Public Health + 2 years experience. Age: Up to 45 years.
  • Lady Health Visitor (33 Posts): 12th + ANM Diploma / LHV Training + 2-3 years experience + Delhi Nursing Council registration. Age: Up to 30 years.
  • District Quality Assurance Coordinator (4 Posts): MBBS/Dental/AYUSH/Nursing Graduate + MHA + 2 years experience. Age: 40 years.
  • District Program Manager (2 Posts): MBA/PGD in Health/Hospital Administration + 3-5 years experience. Age: 45 years.
  • District IEC/BCC Officer (3 Posts): Master in Mass Communication + 2 years experience. Age: 35 years.
  • Nutritionist/Dietician (3 Posts): Master in Food & Nutrition / Clinical Nutrition / Dietetics (or B.Sc. + PG Diploma + 1 year experience). Age: Up to 30 years.
  • Medical Lecturer (2 Posts): MD/DNB + 3 years teaching experience + Delhi Medical Council registration. Age: Up to 45 years (64 years for retired Govt. servants).
  • Data Base Administrator (1 Post): MCA/B.Tech (IT/CS)/M.Sc. (CS) + 3 years database experience. Age: 45 years.
  • Quality Assurance Consultant (1 Post): MBBS/Dental/AYUSH/Nursing Graduate + MHA + 5 years experience (1 year in quality). Age: 45 years.
  • State IEC/BCC Consultant (1 Post): Master in Journalism / Mass Communication + 3 years experience. Age: 35 years.
  • Legal Consultant (3 Posts): LLB + 3 years experience in Court/Govt. Institution + Bar Council enrolment. Age: 45 years.

DSHM Various Posts Recruitment 2026 Selection Process::

The selection on the basis of following steps:-

  • Scrutiny / Shortlisting of online applications
  • Document Verification
  • Personal Interview
DSHM Various Posts Recruitment 2026 Application Fee::
  • No Application Fee is to be paid by any candidate.

How to Apply DSHM Various Posts Recruitment 2026::

Eligible & Interested candidates must apply online only through the DSHM portal.

➢ Step 1: Visit the official DSHM online application portal given in Important Links.

➢ Step 2: Register / Login with valid details and fill the online application form carefully with personal, educational, experience and category information.

➢ Step 3: Upload required documents (if any) and review the application.

➢ Step 4: Submit the form on or before 12 May 2026 (05:00 PM) and take a printout of the submitted application / acknowledgement for future reference.

➢ Step 5: Shortlisted candidates will be called for Document Verification and Personal Interview. Bring all original documents along with one set of self-attested copies at the time of verification.
