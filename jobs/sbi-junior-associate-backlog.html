SBI Junior Associate Backlog Recruitment 2026 Eligibility Criteria::
  • Educational Qualification (as on 31.12.2026): Graduation in any discipline from a recognised University or any equivalent qualification recognised by the Central Government. Candidates with Integrated Dual Degree (IDD) must have passed on or before 31.12.2026. Final year/semester students may apply provisionally and must produce proof of passing by 31.12.2026. Matriculate Ex-servicemen with Indian Army Special Certificate of Education (or equivalent) after 15 years of service (dated on/before 31.12.2026) are also eligible.
  • Local Language Proficiency: Candidates applying for vacancies of a particular State/UT must be proficient in reading, writing, speaking and understanding the specified/opted local language of that State/UT. Candidates who have studied the opted local language in 10th or 12th standard are exempt from LLPT.
  • Age Limit (as on 01.04.2026): Minimum 20 years and Maximum 28 years (A candidate must have been born not earlier than 02.04.1998 and not later than 01.04.2006 – both dates inclusive).
  • Age Relaxation: OBC – 3 years, SC/ST – 5 years, PwBD (OBC) – 13 years, PwBD (SC/ST) – 15 years, Widows/Divorced/Judicially separated women (not remarried) – 7 years, Ex-Servicemen/Disabled Ex-Servicemen – as per rules, Trained Apprentices of SBI – additional relaxation as applicable.
SBI Junior Associate Salary 2026::
  • Pay Scale: ₹24,050 – 1,340/3 – 28,070 – 1,650/3 – 33,020 – 2,000/4 – 41,020 – 2,340/7 – 57,400 – 4,400/1 – 61,800 – 2,680/1 – 64,480
  • Starting Basic Pay: ₹26,730/- (₹24,050 + two advance increments for graduates).
  • In-hand salary of an SBI Junior Associate (Clerk) is approximately ₹46,000 per month at Metro centres like Mumbai (inclusive of DA, other allowances). Allowances vary as per posting location. Additional benefits include PF, NPS, Medical facilities, LFC and other perquisites.
SBI Junior Associate Backlog Recruitment 2026 Selection Process::
  • Online Preliminary Examination
  • Online Mains Examination
  • Local Language Proficiency Test (LLPT) – where applicable
  • Final Merit: Based on marks obtained in the Mains Examination only (state-wise & category-wise). Trained SBI Apprentices (eligible) get 5 marks bonus in Mains aggregate. There is no interview.

How to Apply for SBI Junior Associate Backlog Recruitment 2026::

Eligible candidates must apply ONLINE ONLY through the official SBI website. No other mode of application will be accepted.

➢ Step 1: Visit the official website sbi.bank.in/web/careers/current-openings.

➢ Step 2: Click on the link “RECRUITMENT OF JUNIOR ASSOCIATES SPECIAL RECRUITMENT DRIVE FOR SC/ST/OBC BACKLOG VACANCIES” or go to the Apply Online link.

➢ Step 3: Complete the registration process by providing basic details and generate Registration Number & Password.

➢ Step 4: Fill the online application form carefully with personal, educational and other required details (apply for one State/UT only).

➢ Step 5: Upload scanned copies of Photograph, Signature, Left Thumb Impression and Handwritten Declaration as per the prescribed format.

➢ Step 6: Pay the application fee online (if applicable) through Debit Card / Credit Card / Net Banking / UPI.

➢ Step 7: Submit the application form and take a printout of the confirmation page for future reference.
