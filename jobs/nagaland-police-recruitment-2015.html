Nagaland Police Recruitment 2015 (308 Vacancies)

Published by: RozgarDwaar Editorial Desk | Updated: 2025-11-22
Apply Online (Official Website) Download Notification PDF Official Website

The Government of Nagaland, Office of the Director General of Police, invites online applications from eligible Indigenous Inhabitants Tribes of Nagaland (Male & Female) belonging to any Indigenous Naga Tribes for filling up 1176 posts of Constable (GD) through OPEN RALLY in the Nagaland Police Department. Non-Indigenous applicants shall not be eligible. This opportunity is for candidates meeting the educational and physical standards to serve in various districts of Nagaland.

The Nagaland Police Constable GD Vacancy 2025 online application form is available from 6th October 2025 to 22nd November 2025 (extended). Interested and eligible candidates must carefully read the detailed Nagaland Police notification and Addendum before filling out the application form.

Nagaland Police Constable GD 2025 Age Limit::
  • 18 to 38 years as on 30.09.2025 (date of advertisement).
  • Age concession for serving Government employees as per Government Notifications.
Nagaland Police Constable GD 2025 Eligibility Criteria::
  • Eligible only for Indigenous Inhabitants Tribes of Nagaland (Male/Female) belonging to any Indigenous Naga Tribes. Non-Indigenous applicants not eligible.
  • District-specific: Apply against only one district as per tribal ratio; must be resident with supporting documents.
  • Educational: Class-6 Passed (or higher) for Backward Tribes; Class-8 Passed (or higher) for other Naga Tribes from NBSE or equivalent recognised Board.
  • Physical Standards (PST - Qualifying): Male - Height 162 cm, Chest 78/83 cm; Female - Height 152 cm, Chest 74/79 cm; Weight proportionate to height.
  • Medical Fitness: No colour blindness, vision 6/6 & 6/9, no deformities, etc. (Detailed disqualifying conditions apply).

How to Apply Nagaland Police Constable GD Recruitment 2025::

Only online applications are accepted through the Nagaland Police Registration Portal. The process is as follows:-

➢ Step 1: Visit nagalandpolicerecruitment.in and register/ the online application form from 6th October 2025.

➢ Step 2: Upload required documents: Educational Certificates, Identity Proof, Indigenous Inhabitant Certificate (issued by authority not below ADC), ST/Backward Tribe Certificates, DOB Proof. Employed candidates need No Objection Certificate.

➢ Step 3: Pay the ₹300/- fee online. Generate Admit Card after submission. Submit on or before 22nd November 2025. Option to change district for certain applicants as per Addendum.
