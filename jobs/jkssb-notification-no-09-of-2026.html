JKSSB Notification No. 09 of 2026 – Apply Online for 2863 MTS & Sanitation Worker Posts | Last Date 03-11-2026

Published by: RozgarDwaar Editorial Desk | Updated: 2026-09-02
Apply Online Now Download Notification PDF ← Back to Portal Home

Jammu and Kashmir Services Selection Board (JKSSB) has released Advertisement Notification No. 09 of 2026 dated 01 September 2026 for recruitment to Class-IV (MTS) and Sanitation Worker posts in various departments of the Union Territory of Jammu & Kashmir. A total of 2,863 vacancies have been advertised under UT Cadre, Divisional Cadre and District Cadre through General Administration Department, offering a major opportunity for 8th / 10th / 12th pass candidates who are domiciles of J&K.

The JKSSB Class IV / MTS Recruitment 2026 online application process will begin on 05 October 2026, and the last date to apply is 03 November 2026. On this page, job aspirants can find all important details of JKSSB Vacancy 2026, including the official notification and application information.

✅ JKSSB Class IV / MTS Recruitment 2026 Eligibility Criteria:

  • Domicile: Candidate must be a domicile of the Union Territory of Jammu & Kashmir and must possess a valid Domicile Certificate issued by the Competent Authority on or before the last date of application (03.11.2026).
  • Educational Qualification (as on 03.11.2026):
    • Class-IV (MTS): Minimum Matriculation (10th Pass) and Maximum 10+2 (as per S.O. 184 of 2020 read with SRO 99 of 2008).
    • Sanitation Worker: Minimum 8th Pass and Maximum 10+2 (as per S.O. 132 of 2022).
  • Important: Candidates possessing qualification higher than 10+2 are not eligible for these posts.
  • Age Limit (as on 01.01.2026):
Category Max. Age Not Born Before Not Born After
OM 40 Years 01.01.1986 01.01.2008
SC / ST-1 / ST-2 / RBA / ALC-IB / OBC / EWS 43 Years 01.01.1983 01.01.2008
Physically Challenged Person (PwBD) 42 Years 01.01.1984 01.01.2008
Ex-Servicemen Service period deducted from actual age; resultant age should not exceed the prescribed max age by more than 3 years

Additional 5 years upper age relaxation is available to eligible casual workers claiming additional weightage as per applicable J&K Class-IV special recruitment rules.

✅ JKSSB Class IV / MTS Salary 2026:

  • Pay Scale: Level SL-1 – ₹14,800 – ₹47,100
  • Selected candidates will also be entitled to DA, HRA and other allowances as applicable to Class-IV posts in the UT of Jammu & Kashmir.

✅ JKSSB Class IV / MTS Recruitment 2026 Selection Process:

  • Written Examination (Objective Type MCQs)
  • Additional Weightage Marks (as per cadre)
  • Document Verification
  • Medical Examination

Final merit is prepared out of 100 marks as per S.O. 184 of 2020 and SRO 404 of 2018.

✅ JKSSB Class IV / MTS Exam Pattern 2026:

  • Objective Type Multiple Choice Questions (MCQs)
  • Question paper in English language only
  • Negative Marking: 1/4th of the marks assigned to a question for each wrong answer
  • Detailed syllabus will be notified separately on the JKSSB official website

✅ JKSSB Class IV / MTS Recruitment 2026 Application Fee:

  • OM / OBC / RBA / ALC-IB: ₹600/-
  • SC / ST-1 / ST-2 / EWS / PwBD: ₹500/-
  • Payment Mode: Online only (Net Banking / Credit Card / Debit Card)

✅ How to Apply for JKSSB Class IV / MTS Recruitment 2026:

Eligible candidates must apply ONLINE ONLY through the official JKSSB website. No other mode of application will be accepted.

➢ Step 1: Visit the official website https://jkssb.nic.in.

➢ Step 2: Click on the link for Advertisement Notification No. 09 of 2026 / Apply Online.

➢ Step 3: Complete the registration process and generate Registration Number & Password.

➢ Step 4: Fill the online application form carefully with personal, educational, cadre and category details. Apply only for the eligible Item Number / cadre.

➢ Step 5: Upload photograph, signature and other required documents as per the prescribed format.

➢ Step 6: Pay the application fee online through Net Banking / Credit Card / Debit Card.

➢ Step 7: Submit the application form and take a printout of the confirmation page for future reference.

✅ FAQs:

What is the total number of vacancies in JKSSB Notification No. 09 of 2026?

A total of 2,863 Class-IV (MTS) and Sanitation Worker posts have been advertised under UT, Divisional and District Cadres.

What is the last date to apply for JKSSB Class IV / MTS 2026?

The last date to submit the online application is 03 November 2026.

What is the age limit for JKSSB Class IV Recruitment 2026?

For Open Merit, maximum age is 40 years as on 01.01.2026 (born not earlier than 01.01.1986 and not later than 01.01.2008). Reserved categories get relaxation up to 43 years, and PwBD up to 42 years.

What is the educational qualification required for JKSSB Class IV / MTS 2026?

Class-IV (MTS): Minimum 10th Pass and Maximum 10+2. Sanitation Worker: Minimum 8th Pass and Maximum 10+2. Candidates with qualification higher than 10+2 are not eligible. J&K domicile is mandatory.

What is the selection process for JKSSB Class IV 2026?

Written Exam + additional weightage marks (home division/district, family not in govt. service, widows/divorced/orphan girls, casual workers) + Document Verification / Medical Examination. Final merit is out of 100 marks.

What is the application fee for JKSSB Class IV 2026?

₹600 for OM/OBC/RBA/ALC-IB candidates and ₹500 for SC/ST-1/ST-2/EWS/PwBD candidates.

How to apply for JKSSB Notification No. 09 of 2026?

Candidates must apply online only through the official JKSSB website between 05 October 2026 and 03 November 2026.
