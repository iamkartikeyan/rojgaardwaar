NSPCL Advertisement 01/2026 – Apply Online for 17 Engineer & Officer Posts | Last Date 22-09-2026

Published by: RozgarDwaar Editorial Desk | Updated: 2026-08-28
Apply Online (Official Website) Download Notification PDF Official Website

NTPC-SAIL Power Company Limited (NSPCL), a joint venture of NTPC Limited and SAIL, has released Advertisement No. 01/2026 for recruitment to Senior Assistant Officer and Senior Assistant Engineer posts in E1 Grade. A total of 17 vacancies are available across HR, Law, Electrical, Mechanical, C&I and IT/ERP disciplines.

The online application process for NSPCL Recruitment 2026 will begin on 07 September 2026 at 10:00 AM and close on 22 September 2026 at 6:00 PM. Eligible candidates can find complete details of the vacancy, eligibility, pay scale, selection process and application link on this page.

NSPCL Recruitment 2026 Age Limit::
  • Maximum age: 32 years as on the last date of online application (22 September 2026).
  • Age Relaxation: SC/ST – 5 years, OBC (NCL) – 3 years, PwBD – 10 years, Ex-Servicemen – as per Government rules.
NSPCL Recruitment 2026 Pay Scale::

All posts are in E1 Grade with pay scale of ₹40,000 – ₹1,40,000 (IDA Pattern).

Selected candidates will also be entitled to Dearness Allowance, perquisites, allowances, terminal benefits and other benefits as per company rules applicable from time to time.

NSPCL Recruitment 2026 Eligibility Criteria (Post-wise)::

1. Senior Assistant Officer (HR) – 04 Posts

  • Qualification: Graduate with two-year full-time PG Degree / PG Diploma / PG Programme in Management (specialization in HR / Industrial Relations / Personnel Management) OR Master’s in Social Work / MHRD / MBA (HR) from a recognised University/Institute with minimum 60% marks. Computer knowledge is essential. Degree in Law is desirable.
  • Experience: Minimum 02 years post-qualification experience in Human Resources / Personnel & Administration in a reputed organisation.

2. Senior Assistant Officer (Law) – 01 Post (UR)

  • Qualification: Full-time Bachelor’s Degree in Law (LLB or equivalent) from a recognised University/Institute with minimum 60% marks.
  • Experience: Minimum 02 years post-qualification experience in litigation, drafting & vetting of contracts, legal opinions, arbitration, statutory compliance, coordination with advocates and regulatory bodies. Experience in power / infrastructure / engineering / manufacturing sector is desirable.

3. Senior Assistant Engineer (Electrical) – 04 Posts

  • Qualification: B.E./B.Tech in Electrical / Electrical & Electronics with minimum 60% marks from a recognised University/Institution.
  • Experience: Minimum 02 years post-qualification executive experience in power sector (Coal / Gas / Lignite) in Operation / Maintenance / Commissioning / Erection / Engineering of power plant / power project.

4. Senior Assistant Engineer (Mechanical) – 04 Posts

  • Qualification: B.E./B.Tech in Mechanical / Production with minimum 60% marks from a recognised University/Institution.
  • Experience: Minimum 02 years post-qualification executive experience in power sector in Operation / Maintenance / Commissioning / Erection / Engineering of power plant / power project.

5. Senior Assistant Engineer (C&I) – 02 Posts (UR)

  • Qualification: B.E./B.Tech in Electronics / Control & Instrumentation / Instrumentation with minimum 60% marks from a recognised University/Institution.
  • Experience: Minimum 02 years post-qualification executive experience in power sector in Operation / Maintenance / Commissioning / Erection / Engineering of power plant / power project.

6. Senior Assistant Engineer (IT/ERP) – 02 Posts

  • Qualification: B.E./B.Tech in Information Technology / Computer Science / Electronics & Communication with minimum 60% marks from a recognised University/Institution.
  • Experience: Minimum 02 years post-qualification experience in IT infrastructure, systems & operations, network security, software development, database management or communication systems in a reputed organisation. Valid CCNA / CCNP or equivalent certification is preferable.

Note: SC/ST/PwBD candidates with pass marks in the qualifying degree are eligible. Candidates can apply for only one post.

NSPCL Recruitment 2026 Selection Process::
  • Stage 1 – All India Online Selection Test: Consists of Subject Knowledge Test (SKT) and Executive Aptitude Test (EAT). Candidates must qualify both parts separately.
  • Stage 2 – Personal Interview: Shortlisted candidates (based on merit and requirement) will appear for interview. Minimum qualifying marks in interview are mandatory.

Final Weightage: Online Selection Test – 85% | Personal Interview – 15%.

Candidates can choose any two cities as Preference-I and Preference-II for the test centre. NSPCL reserves the right to cancel/add/change centres.

NSPCL Recruitment 2026 Application Fee::
  • General: ₹300/- (non-refundable)
  • SC / ST / PwBD / Female Candidates: Nil
  • Payment Mode: Online (Net Banking / Debit Card / Credit Card / UPI)

Service Agreement Bond: Selected General category candidates must execute a bond of ₹5,00,000/- (SC/ST/PwBD: ₹2,50,000/-) to serve NSPCL for a minimum of 4 years after joining.

How to Apply for NSPCL Recruitment 2026::

Eligible candidates must apply ONLINE ONLY through the official NSPCL website. No other mode of application will be accepted.

➢ Step 1: Visit the official website https://www.nspcl.co.in and go to the Recruitment / Careers section.

➢ Step 2: Select Advertisement No. 01/2026 and carefully read the detailed instructions.

➢ Step 3: Click on the online application link and register using basic details. Note down the Unique Registration Number generated.

➢ Step 4: Fill the complete online application form accurately with personal, educational and experience details.

➢ Step 5: Upload the required documents (photograph, signature, certificates, experience proofs etc.).

➢ Step 6: Select preferred Test Centre-I and Test Centre-II.

➢ Step 7: Pay the applicable registration fee online (if applicable) and submit the application.

➢ Step 8: Download and retain the Application Slip containing the unique application number for future reference.

Important: No documents need to be sent by post. Candidates with private-sector experience must ensure complete documentary proof of pay and experience. Government / PSU candidates should apply through proper channel or produce NOC at the time of interview.
