GSL Junior Project Executive Recruitment 2025 - 30 Posts, Notification, Online Form

Published by: RozgarDwaar Editorial Desk | Updated: 2025-09-07
Apply Online (Official Website) Download Notification PDF Official Website

Goa Shipyard Limited (GSL), a Schedule ‘B’ Mini Ratna Category-I Company under the Ministry of Defence, Government of India, is seeking applications for Junior Project Executive roles on a contract basis through Advertisement No. 07/2025. The initial contract duration is three years, with a possible extension of one year. Qualified individuals can submit their applications online via the official portal from August 25, 2025, to September 24, 2025.

GSL Junior Project Executive Recruitment 2025 - 30 Vacancies:
Name of the Post No of Vacancies

Junior Project Executive (Mechanical)

15

Junior Project Executive (Electrical)

10

Junior Project Executive (Electronics)

03

Junior Project Executive (Civil)

02

Age Limit (as on 31.07.2025)::

✔️ UR/EWS: 32 years.
✔️ OBC-NCL: 35 years.
✔️ SC/ST: 37 years.
✔️ The Upper Age relaxation as per Government rules.

Consolidated Salary::

₹ 45,000/- (1st Year) to ₹ 55,000/- (4th Year if extended)

Eligibility Criteria::

✔️ Full-time B.E./B.Tech/B.Sc (Engineering) (4 years) in the relevant discipline from a recognized institution.
✔️ Minimum 3 years post-qualification experience in the relevant area.

Selection Process::

✔️ Written Test (85%)
✔️ GD/Interview (15%)

Application Fee::

✔️ ₹ 500/- (non-refundable) paid online via SBI e-pay (debit/credit card/net banking).
✔️ Exempt for SC/ST/PwBD/Ex-Servicemen and GSL Graduate Apprentices/Advanced Trainees (current or former).

How to Apply for GSL Recruitment 2025::

➢ Eligible candidates can apply online for GSL Careers section. The online registration portal will remain open from 25th August 2025 (00:00 hrs) to 24th September 2025 (17:00 hrs).

Applicants must upload scanned copies of essential documents, including:

  • Passport-size photograph (under 450 KB)
  • Signature (under 450 KB)
  • SSC certificate (under 500 KB)
  • Educational qualification certificates (up to 1 MB)
  • Caste/Disability certificate, if applicable (under 500 KB)
  • Proof of work experience (up to 1 MB)
  • Aadhaar card (under 500 KB)

Candidates applying for more than one post must submit separate applications with individual fees. After completing the online process, it is advised to print two copies of the application form for reference.
