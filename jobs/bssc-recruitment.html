BSSC Recruitment 2025 Apply Online 432 Stenographer Posts | Last Date 03 November

Published by: RozgarDwaar Editorial Desk | Updated: 2025-11-03
Apply Online (Official Website) Download Notification PDF Official Website

Bihar Staff Selection Commission (BSSC) has announced the recruitment drive for 432 positions of Stenographer/Steno Typist Grade-III under the Bihar Police Subordinate Services in Patna. This opportunity targets eligible candidates for these roles specifically in the Gaya district, with applications accepted exclusively online via the official BSSC portal.

The recruitment alert, referenced under Advertisement No. 07/2025, was published on September 19, 2025. Individuals meeting the criteria, including those with 10th and 12th qualifications along with relevant skills, can register from September 25, 2025, until November 5, 2025. Applicants should carefully check the requirements for age, fees, and selection stages before submitting their forms.

How to Apply BSSC Recruitment 2025?:
  • Eligible Candidates should apply online through BSSC Online Application Portal (www.onlinebssc.com) from 25th September 2025.
  • The candidates are required to fill basic, personal and qualification details and upload photo-copies of required documents.
  • The candidates should required to upload Live Photo during filling online application form.
  • The closing date for filling online application and payment is 05/11/2025 up to 11:59 PM IST.

Frequently Asked Questions::

What is the age criteria for BSSC Stenographer/Steno Typist Grade-III Posts?

Applicants should be between 18 and 37 years as of August 1, 2025, with provisions for age concessions under government guidelines for eligible groups.

What qualifications are needed for BSSC Stenographer/Steno Typist Grade-III Posts?

Candidates must hold 10th and 12th qualifications from approved institutions, plus skills in shorthand, typing, and computer usage.

What stages are involved in the BSSC Stenographer/Steno Typist Grade-III selection?

The process includes a written examination, skill assessment, document scrutiny, and medical check-up.

When is the deadline for BSSC Stenographer/Steno Typist Grade-III applications?

Applications close on November 5, 2025, at 11:59 PM.

How many openings are there for BSSC Stenographer/Steno Typist Grade-III in 2025?

There are 432 total positions, distributed across various categories.
