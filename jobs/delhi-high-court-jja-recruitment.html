Delhi High Court JJA Recruitment 2026: 152 Posts, Notification, Online Form

Published by: RozgarDwaar Editorial Desk | Updated: 2026-02-22
Apply Online (Official Website) Download Notification PDF Official Website

High Court of Delhi, New Delhi invites online applications from eligible candidates for the post of Junior Judicial Assistant / Restorer (Open) Examination - 2026. The Delhi High Court JJA Recruitment 2026 notification has been released for 152 vacancies. The DHC JJA Vacancy 2026 Online registration and fee payment starts from 04th February 2026 and closes on 23rd February 2026.

How to Apply Delhi High Court JJA Recruitment 2026:

Eligible candidates must apply ONLINE only through the official Delhi High Court website. No other mode of application will be accepted.

➢ Step 1: Visit the official website delhihighcourt.nic.in and go to the Recruitment section.

➢ Step 2: Register using a valid Email ID and Mobile Number.

➢ Step 3: Fill the online application form with personal, educational, and other required details.

➢ Step 4: Upload scanned copies of photograph, signature, left hand thumb impression, and other required documents.

➢ Step 5: Pay the application fee online (if applicable) through the available payment modes.

➢ Step 6: Submit the application and take a printout of the confirmation page for future reference.
