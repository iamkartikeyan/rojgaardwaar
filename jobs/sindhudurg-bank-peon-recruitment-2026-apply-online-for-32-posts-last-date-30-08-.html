Sindhudurg Bank Peon Recruitment 2026 - Apply Online for 32 Posts | Last Date 30-08-2026

Published by: RozgarDwaar Editorial Desk | Updated: 2026-08-29
Apply Online (Official Website) Download Notification PDF Official Website

Sindhudurg District Central Co-operative Bank Ltd., Sindhudurg (Sindhudurg Bank) invites online applications for recruitment of Shipai (Peon) for fill up 32 vacancies. The recruitment process is being conducted by the Kolhapur District Nagari Banks Co-operative Association Ltd., Kolhapur, offering a good opportunity for 12th-pass candidates who are residents of Sindhudurg district.

The Sindhudurg Bank Peon Recruitment 2026 online registration process began on 20 August 2026, and the last date to apply is 30 August 2026. On this page, banking job aspirants can find all important details of Sindhudurg Bank Shipai Vacancy 2026, including the official notification and application link.

Sindhudurg Bank Peon Recruitment 2026 Eligibility Criteria::
  • Educational Qualification: Candidates must have passed at least the 12th standard (HSC) examination from a recognised board.
  • Age Limit: Minimum 21 years and Maximum 40 years. Candidates must have been born between 30.08.1986 and 30.08.2005 (both dates inclusive). Candidates born before 30.08.1986 or after 30.08.2005 are not eligible.
  • Domicile Requirement: The candidate must be a resident (domicile) of Sindhudurg district. A valid Domicile Certificate issued by a competent authority must be uploaded at the time of application. Applications without the domicile certificate will not be considered.
  • Physical Fitness: Candidates should be physically capable and fit to carry out the physical duties associated with the Shipai (Peon) post.
Sindhudurg Bank Peon Recruitment 2026 Selection Process::
  • Written Examination (Offline)
  • Interview

Final Merit: Based on combined marks of Written Exam (70 marks) + Interview (30 marks). Candidates will be shortlisted for interview in the ratio of 1:3. A waiting list equal to 20% of vacancies will also be prepared (valid for one year).

How to Apply for Sindhudurg Bank Peon Recruitment 2026::

Eligible candidates must apply ONLINE ONLY through the official website. No other mode of application will be accepted.

➢ Step 1: Visit the official website www.kopbankasso.co.in.

➢ Step 2: Read the detailed notification and instructions carefully.

➢ Step 3: Open the online application form and fill in accurate details of educational qualification, age and domicile/residence.

➢ Step 4: Upload the required documents including Domicile Certificate of Sindhudurg district, educational certificates, recent photograph (JPEG, up to 20 KB, 3.5 cm × 4.5 cm) and signature (JPEG, up to 20 KB, 2.5 cm × 1.5 cm).

➢ Step 5: Pay the non-refundable examination fee of ₹600/- through online payment mode (payment status may take 24-48 hours to update).

➢ Step 6: Recheck all details carefully. Details cannot be changed after final submission.

➢ Step 7: Click “Submit” and then “Submit my Form” after confirming the declaration. Note down the Form ID and Mobile Number and take a printout of the acknowledgement for future reference.
