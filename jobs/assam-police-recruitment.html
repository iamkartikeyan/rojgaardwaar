Assam Police Recruitment 2024 Online Apply 269 Constable Posts - apcap.in

Published by: RozgarDwaar Editorial Desk | Updated: 2024-02-14
Apply Online (Official Website) Download Notification PDF Official Website

Assam Police Notification 2024 of 269 Constable Posts under the Directorate of Civil Defence and Home Guards, Assam. The Office of the Assam State Level Police Recruitment Board (SLPRB), located in Guwahati, Assam, invites online applications from eligible candidates to fill up the 269 sanctioned vacant posts of Constable (Grade III) under the Directorate of Civil Defence and Home Guards, Assam. Eligible candidates should apply online through the SLPRB Assam official website (slprbassam.in) starting from 1st February 2024. There is no application fee for this recruitment. The last date for receiving online applications is 15th February 2024.

Assam Police Constable Recruitment 2024 Age Limit: (As on 01/01/2023):

✔️ The Assam Constable posts Minimum Age 18 years and Maximum Age 40 years as on 1st January 2024.
✔️ The Candidate must be born on or before 01.01.2006 and on or after 01.01.1984.
✔️ The upper age limit will be relaxed for 05 years for SC / ST, 03 years for OBC / MOBC and 02 years additional for Ex-Servicemen.

Assam Police Constable Exam 2024 Details::

✔️ Written test will consist of 100 multiple choice type questions to be answered on an OMR answer sheet. For each correct answer the Candidate will get half mark. Total marks for the Written Test will be 50. There will be no negative marking.
✔️ The subjects to be covered will be as follows:- Elementary Arithmetic, General English, Logical reasoning/ Mental ability, Assam’s History, Geography, Polity, Economy, General Awareness/ General Knowledge and current affairs.
✔️ The question paper for Written Exam will be in the following languages: Assamese/ Bodo/ Bengali/ English.
✔️ Written Test will be conducted at Guwahati. The date and venue of the written test will be notified in due course.

How to Apply Assam Police Constable Recruitment 2024::

The Assam Police Recruitment 2024 registration process is detailed as follows:

  • Registration Mode: All applications must be submitted online through the SLPRB Police APCAP Recruitment Portal (apcap.in), which is www.slprbassam.in. This process begins on 1st February 2024.

  • Valid Mobile Number: During registration, use a valid mobile number to create your account.

  • Recruitment ID: After a successful registration, candidates will receive a unique Recruitment ID. It's essential to note that candidates generating multiple Recruitment IDs will have their candidature canceled.

  • Document Upload: Candidates are required to upload high-quality documents, including a passport size photograph, signature, and proof of documents such as age proof, qualification certificates, caste certificates, etc.

  • Application Deadline: The last date for receiving online applications is 15/02/2024 up to Midnight.

  • Help Desk: For any queries contact Phone Number is +91-88267-62317 (OR) Email to slprb2023@gmailcom (10:00 AM to 5:00 PM on all working days).
