NIC STA Recruitment 2026 - Apply Online for 376 Fresher Posts | Last Date 30-09-2026

Published by: RozgarDwaar Editorial Desk | Updated: 2026-09-01
Apply Online (Official Website) Download Notification PDF Official Website

National Informatics Centre (NIC), under the Ministry of Electronics and Information Technology, Government of India, invites online applications from eligible freshers for direct recruitment to 376 Scientific/ Technical Assistant-A (Group-B S&T non-Gazetted) posts in Level-6 of the 7th CPC pay matrix.

The NIC STA Recruitment 2026 for 376 Fresher Posts online registration starts on 01st September 2026 and closes on 30th September 2026. This article provides complete information about NIC Scientific/Technical Assistant-A 2026, including eligibility criteria, vacancy details, official notification PDF, and online application link.

NIC STA Recruitment 2026 Eligibility Criteria::
  • Computer Sciences & Information Technology: M.Sc./ MS/MCA/B.E./B.Tech in Electronics, Computer Science, IT or related fields + Valid GATE 2024/2025/2026 score in CS paper.
  • Electronics and Communication Engineering: M.Sc./MS/MCA/B.E./B.Tech in relevant Electronics & Communication fields + Valid GATE 2024/2025/2026 score in EC paper.
  • Data Science and Artificial Intelligence: M.Sc./MS/MCA/B.E./B.Tech in relevant fields + Valid GATE 2024/2025/2026 score in DA paper.
  • Age Limit: 30 years for UR / EWS, 35 years for SC / ST, 3 years for OBC NCL and 40 years for PwBD.
  • Note: No experience is required. Age relaxations are applicable for SC/ST/OBC/PwBD/Service/Ex-Servicemen candidates as per Government rules.
NIC STA Recruitment 2026 Selection Process::

The selection will be based on the following stages:

  • Merit list prepared on the basis of valid GATE Score (2024/2025/2026)
  • Document Verification
NIC STA Recruitment 2026 Application Fee::
  • SC / ST / PwBD / Women candidates: Nil
  • UR / OBC (NCL) / EWS candidates: ₹800/- per discipline
  • Payment Method: Online Mode only.

How to Apply NIC STA Recruitment 2026?:

Eligible candidates must apply online only through the NIC recruitment portal. The online registration steps are as follows:

  • Step 1: Visit  recruitment.nic.in and complete Registration using valid email and mobile number.
  • Step 2: Fill the online application form carefully and upload required documents.
  • Step 3: Pay the application fee online (if applicable).
  • Step 4: Download the Confirmation Page after successful submission.
