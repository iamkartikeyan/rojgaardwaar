Goa Shipyard Limited Non Executive Recruitment 2025 - Apply Online for 102 Posts

Published by: RozgarDwaar Editorial Desk | Updated: 2026-01-12
Apply Online (Official Website) Download Notification PDF Official Website

Goa Shipyard Limited (GSL), a Schedule ‘B’ Mini Ratna Category-I Central Public Sector Undertaking under the Ministry of Defence, engaged in designing and building ships for the Indian Navy, Indian Coast Guard and other customers, has invited applications from qualified and talented Indian Nationals for the recruitment of Experts/Specialists on fixed-term contract basis.

THe closing date for submission of applications is 30th January 2026. The Candidates are strongly advised to carefully read the detailed official notification before applying to confirm eligibility regarding age, qualification, experience and other criteria.

Goa Shipyard Limited Expert Recruitment 2026:
rozgardwaar.com :
🏢 Organization Goa Shipyard Limited (GSL), Ministry of Defence
💼 Post Name Expert / Specialist (Various Disciplines) - Fixed Term Contract
📊 Total Vacancies 10 Posts
🎯 Age Limit Maximum 62 Years (as on 31.12.2025)
💰 Monthly Salary Consolidated (Last drawn Basic + DA for Ex-Govt/PSU employees)
🎓 Minimum Qualification Degree / Diploma in Engineering or Equivalent
💳 Application Fee Nil
📝 Selection Process Interview
📍 Job Location Vasco-Da-Gama, Goa
⏰ Last Date 31 January 2026
Goa Shipyard Limited 2026 Vacancy Details::
Post Name No of Vacancies Key Scope / Equipment
Expert / Specialist – GES & SR (Patrol Boats) 01 Support construction & maintenance of boats, liaison with end user, trials, delivery
Expert / Specialist - Outfitting Electrical (Electronics) 01 Cable laying, installation, trials of various electrical/electronic systems (IMCS, RCS, Radar, etc.)
Expert / Specialist – EA(R), EL(R) 03 Maintenance of ASR, SSR, communication systems, CMS, Nav Aids; Talwar/Teg class preferred
Expert / Specialist – Hull Artificer / ERA / EA(P) / EA 01 Production coordination, compartment/equipment readiness; refits on frontline warships
Expert / Specialist – EA(P) / ERA / MECH (Controls) 01 GT Controls on IN ships; GTTT experience desirable
Expert / Specialist – HVACR 02 HVAC / AC & Refrigeration systems on IN/CG ships
Expert / Specialist - GES & SR 01 Ballast control system installation, trials, training for Floating dock
Goa Shipyard Limited 2026 Age Limit::
  • Upper age limit: 62 years (as on 31.12.2025) for all posts.
  • No relaxation mentioned (targeted at retired/experienced personnel).
Goa Shipyard Limited 2026 Eligibility Criteria::
  • Expert / Specialist – GES & SR (Patrol Boats) (01 Post): Degree/Graduate from recognized university + Minimum 10 years experience in installation/maintenance of engineering equipment/systems from defence forces (preferably Indian Army).
  • Expert / Specialist - Outfitting Electrical (Electronics) (01 Post): Diploma in Electronics Engineering or equivalent + Minimum 05 years experience in Outfit Electrical/Electronics jobs on Naval/Coast Guard vessels.
  • Expert / Specialist – EA(R), EL(R) (03 Posts): Diploma in Engineering or equivalent + Minimum 05 years experience in maintenance of ASR, SSR, communication systems on IN ships; Desirable: FTTT/WATT experience, Talwar/Teg class preferred.
  • Expert / Specialist – Hull Artificer / ERA / EA(P) / EA (01 Post): Diploma in Engineering or equivalent + Minimum 05 years experience in refits on frontline warships; Talwar/Teg class preferred.
  • Expert / Specialist – EA(P) / ERA / MECH (Controls) (01 Post): Diploma in Engineering or equivalent + Minimum 05 years experience in GT controls on IN ships; Desirable: 02 years GTTT (controls), Talwar/Teg class preferred.
  • Expert / Specialist – HVACR (02 Posts): Diploma in Engineering or equivalent + Minimum 05 years onboard experience in HVAC/AC & Ref systems on IN/CG ships.
  • Expert / Specialist - GES & SR (01 Post): Diploma in Engineering or equivalent + Minimum 05 years experience in ballast control system on Floating dock/FPSO/Marine platforms.

How to Apply Goa Shipyard Limited Expert Recruitment 2026::

Eligible candidates are required to apply OFFLINE only in the prescribed format. No other mode will be accepted.

➢ Step 2: Fill the application form accurately in the prescribed format.
