BSSC 4th Graduate Level Recruitment 2025 Apply Online 1481 Posts | Last Date 21 November

Published by: RozgarDwaar Editorial Desk | Updated: 2025-11-20
Apply Online (Official Website) Download Notification PDF Official Website

Bihar Staff Selection Commission (BSSC) has issued the official notification for the Fourth Graduate Level Joint Competitive Examination 2025 (BSSC CGL 4) under advertisement no. 05/2025. Released on 4th August 2025, this recruitment drive aims to fill 1481 graduate-level posts across various departments in Bihar. Eligible candidates can apply online from 18th August 2025 to 21st November 2025 via the official BSSC portal.

BSSC 4th Graduate Level Recruitment 2025 Notification PDF Download:

The BSSC CGL 4 Notification 2025 PDF, available from 4th August 2025, details the recruitment process for 1481 vacancies. Candidates with a graduate degree from a recognized university are invited to review the notification and apply online through the BSSC portal. Ensure eligibility before submitting applications.

BSSC CGL Age Limit::
  • 21–37 years for UR Male (as on 01/08/2025).
  • Upper age relaxation for reserved categories as per Bihar government norms.
BSSC CGL Salary::
  • Assistant Section Officer, Planning Assistant, Junior Statistical Assistant: Pay Level 7 (₹44,900–₹1,42,400/-).
  • Data Entry Operator: Pay Level 6 (₹35,400–₹1,12,400/-).
  • Auditor, Auditor (Cooperative Societies): Pay Level 5 (₹29,200–₹92,300/-).
BSSC CGL Educational Qualifications::
  • Assistant Section Officer (ASO): Graduation from a recognized university (General Administration Department, Bihar, Patna).
  • Planning Assistant: Graduation from a recognized university (Planning & Development Department).
  • Junior Statistical Assistant (JSA): Graduation in Mathematics, Economics, Commerce, or Statistics (Department of Labor Resources, Bihar, Patna).
  • Data Entry Operator (DEO): Graduation with PGDCA, BCA, BSc (IT), or equivalent (Finance Department, Bihar, Patna).
  • Auditor: Bachelor’s degree in Commerce, Economics, Statistics, or Mathematics (Audit Directorate, Finance Department, Bihar, Patna).
  • Auditor (Cooperative Societies): Graduate with Mathematics or Bachelor of Commerce (Co-operative Department, Bihar, Patna).
BSSC CGL Application Fee::
  • UR/BC/EBC, Outside Bihar (Male/Female): ₹540/-.
  • SC/ST (Bihar Residents), Women (Bihar Residents), Disabled Persons: ₹135/-.
  • Payment Method: Online via BHIM UPI, Net Banking, or Visa/Mastercard/Maestro/RuPay Debit cards.

BSSC CGL Selection Process::
  • Stage 1 – Preliminary Exam: Objective-type exam (600 marks) to shortlist candidates for Mains.
  • Stage 2 – Mains Exam: Two papers (Paper 1: Hindi, 400 marks; Paper 2: General Studies, General Science & Maths, Reasoning, 600 marks).
  • Stage 3 – Document Verification: For candidates who clear both exams.
BSSC CGL Exam Pattern 2025::

Preliminary Exam:

  • Total 150 questions (600 marks).
  • Subjects: General Science & Maths (50 questions, 200 marks), General Studies (50 questions, 200 marks), Comprehension/Logic/Reasoning/Mental Ability (50 questions, 200 marks).
  • Each question: +4 marks; Negative marking: -1 mark.
  • Duration: 2 hours 15 minutes.

Mains Exam:

  • Paper 1 (Hindi): 100 questions, 400 marks, 2 hours 15 minutes, 30% minimum qualifying marks.
  • Paper 2: General Studies (50 questions, 200 marks), General Science & Maths (50 questions, 200 marks), Reasoning Ability (50 questions, 200 marks); Total: 600 marks, 2 hours 15 minutes.

How to Apply for BSSC CGL Recruitment 2025::

Eligible candidates must apply online through the BSSC portal (onlinebssc.com/4thgralevel/) from 18th August to 16th October 2025. Follow these steps:

  • Visit the BSSC official website.
  • Locate the BSSC CGL 4 registration link and click it.
  • Register by providing details like name, email, and phone number.
  • Receive login credentials (post number and password) via email.
  • Log in, complete the application form, and upload required documents (educational certificates, identity proof).
  • Pay the application fee online by 21st November 2025.
  • Submit the form and save a copy for reference. 

Frequently Asked Questions::

What is the age limit for BSSC CGL Recruitment 2025?

21–37 years for UR Male (as on 01/08/2025). Reserved categories receive upper age relaxation as per Bihar government rules.

What are the eligibility criteria for BSSC CGL Recruitment 2025?

Graduation from a recognized university; specific posts require additional qualifications (e.g., PGDCA/BCA for DEO, Mathematics/Commerce for Auditors).

Preliminary Exam (600 marks), Mains Exam (Paper 1: 400 marks, Paper 2: 600 marks), and Document Verification.

What is the last date to apply for BSSC CGL Recruitment 2025?

21st November 2025 (application); 24th November 2025 (registration and fee payment).

What is the total number of vacancies for BSSC CGL Recruitment 2025?

1481 vacancies (510 reserved for women).
