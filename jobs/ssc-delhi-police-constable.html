SSC Delhi Police Constable Executive Vacancy 2025 Apply Online for 7565 Posts | Last Date 31st October

Published by: RozgarDwaar Editorial Desk | Updated: 2025-10-31
Apply Online (Official Website) Download Notification PDF Official Website

Staff Selection Commission (SSC) in collaboration with Delhi Police has issued a fresh recruitment notification for Constable (Executive) posts for Male and Female candidates, aiming to fill up 7565 tentative vacancies. These Group 'C' positions are open to eligible 10+2 pass applicants from across India. The last date for submission of online application is extended up to 31st October 2025.

SSC Delhi Police Constable Notification 2025 Out :

Aspiring Indian candidates should carefully read SSC Delhi Police Constable 2025 Notification details and apply online via ssc.gov.in official portal before the closing date of 21st October 2025. Essential information on eligibility, age criteria, pay structure, fee details, selection stages, and more is outlined below.

SSC Delhi Police Constable Age Limit::
  • Age Limit: 18-25 Years (born not earlier than 02-07-2000 and not later than 01-07-2007).
  • The upper age relaxations: 5 years for SC/ST, 3 years for OBC, up to 10 years for certain sportspersons (SC/ST), up to 45 years for departmental SC/ST candidates, and more as per rules.
SSC Delhi Police Constable Pay Scale::
  • Pay Level-3: ₹ 21,700 - 69,100 per month.
SSC Delhi Police Constable Eligibility Criteria::
  • Essential Qualification: 10+2 (Matriculation + Senior Secondary) from a recognized board (relaxable to 11th for wards of Delhi Police personnel/ MTS or specific departmental roles).
  • Additional for Males: Valid LMV driving license (Motorcycle or Car) by PE&MT date.
  • Nationality: Indian citizen only.
SSC Delhi Police Constable Application Fee::
  • General/OBC/EWS: ₹ 100/-.
  • SC/ST/Female/ESM: Exempted.
  • Payment Mode: Online via BHIM UPI, Net Banking, Visa/Mastercard/Maestro/RuPay Debit Cards (up to 22/10/2025, 23:00 Hrs).
SSC Delhi Police Constable 2025 Selection Process::

Qualified applicants will be called for the SSC Delhi Police Constable 2025 selection process stages:

  • Computer Based Examination (CBE).
  • Physical Endurance & Measurement Test (PE&MT) – Qualifying.
  • Document Verification.
  • Medical Examination.

How to Apply SSC Delhi Police Constable 2025 Online Form::

Eligible candidates must follow these steps for online submission:

  • Complete One-Time Registration (OTR) on ssc.gov.in if not done already.
  • Fill the Online Application Form with personal, educational, and other details.
  • Capture live photo and upload scanned signature as per guidelines.
  • Pay the fee online if applicable.
  • The last date for registration of online applications is 31st October 2025.

What is the age limit for SSC Delhi Police Constable Recruitment 2025?

The age limit is 18-25 years as on 01/07/2025, with various relaxations like 5 years for SC/ST and up to 45 years for certain departmental candidates.

What are the eligibility criteria for SSC Delhi Police Constable Recruitment 2025?

Candidates need 10+2 pass from a recognized board; males require a valid LMV driving license. Relaxations apply for wards of Delhi Police personnel.
