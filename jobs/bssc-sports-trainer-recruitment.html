BSSC Sports Trainer Recruitment 2025 Apply Online for 379 Posts | Last Date Extended 11 December

Published by: RozgarDwaar Editorial Desk | Updated: 2025-12-10
Apply Online (Official Website) Download Notification PDF Official Website

The Bihar Staff Selection Commission (BSSC), Patna, has released the official notification for Sports Trainer Recruitment 2025. The BSSC invites online applications from eligible Indian citizens for engagement of 379 Sports Trainer (क्रीड़ा प्रशिक्षक) posts under Adv No. 08/25. This opportunity is for candidates who possess Graduation Degree, Diploma in Sports Coaching, and have participated in International/National Level Championship to apply for these positions in Bihar.

The BSSC Sports Trainer Notification was published recently, and the online application form will be available from 9th October 2025 to 11th December 2025. Interested and eligible candidates must carefully read the detailed BSSC notification before filling out the application form.

How to Apply BSSC Sports Trainer Recruitment 2025::

The BSSC Online Registration will be made through the official BSSC Recruitment Portal (www.onlinebssc.com/sportsbsc25/). The BSSC Sports Trainer Online Registration Process Steps as follow:-

➢ Step 1: Interested candidates should apply online through the BSSC Recruitment Portal from 9th October 2025.

➢ Step 2: Upload colour photograph, signature, and required documents/certificates (Date of Birth, Educational Certificates, Experience Certificates, Category Certificate).

➢ Step 3: Pay the application fee (if applicable) through online payment gateway. Submit the application form on or before 21/11/2025 up to 11:59 PM.
