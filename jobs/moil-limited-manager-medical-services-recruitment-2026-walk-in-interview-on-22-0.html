MOIL Limited Manager Medical Services Recruitment 2026 - Walk in Interview on 22-09-2026

Published by: RozgarDwaar Editorial Desk | Updated: 2026-09-01
Apply Online (Official Website) Download Notification PDF Official Website

MOIL Limited (formerly Manganese Ore India Limited), a Schedule ‘A’ Miniratna Category-I PSU under the Ministry of Steel, Government of India, has invited candidates for recruitment to the post of Manager (Medical Services) (E-02). A total of 3 vacancies (2 UR and 1 OBC) will be filled through Walk-in Interview under Advertisement No. Med. Ser/08/2026.

Desirous and eligible MBBS doctors can attend the Walk-in Interview on 22 September 2026 at MOIL Head Office, Nagpur. On this page, candidates can find all important details of MOIL Manager Medical Services Recruitment 2026, including eligibility, salary, interview schedule, documents required and official notification link.

MOIL Manager Medical Services Recruitment 2026 Eligibility Criteria::
  • Educational Qualification: M.B.B.S. from a recognized University/Institute.
  • Preferred Qualification: PG Diploma in Gynaecology and Obstetrician / Paediatrics / ENT / Ophthalmology / Dermatology / Orthopaedic / Emergency Medicine / Hospital Administration.
  • Experience: Minimum 1 year post-internship experience in a Hospital.
  • Age Limit: Below 45 years.

Note: Selected Manager (Medical Services) are generally posted at MOIL mines located in Nagpur and Bhandara districts of Maharashtra and Balaghat district of Madhya Pradesh, as per organizational requirement.

MOIL Manager Medical Services Salary 2026::

Pay Scale: ₹50,000 – 3% – ₹1,60,000/- (E-02 Grade)

In addition to basic pay, selected candidates will be entitled to DA, HRA/other perquisites and benefits as per MOIL rules applicable to E-02 executives.

MOIL Manager Medical Services Recruitment 2026 Selection Process::
  • Walk-in Interview
  • Document Verification

Candidates must appear in person with original documents, one set of self-attested copies, detailed Bio-data (prescribed proforma) and Demand Draft (if applicable).

How to Apply for MOIL Manager Medical Services Recruitment 2026::

There is no online application. Eligible candidates must attend the Walk-in Interview in person as per the schedule.

➢ Step 1: Download the official notification and Bio-data proforma from www.moil.nic.in.

➢ Step 2: Fill the Bio-data form carefully and attach one set of self-attested photocopies of all required documents.

➢ Step 3: Prepare Demand Draft of ₹590/- (if applicable) in favour of MOIL Limited, Nagpur, payable at Nagpur.

➢ Step 4: Report at MOIL Bhawan, Nagpur on 22 September 2026 between 10:00 A.M. and 1:00 P.M. with original documents + photocopies + Bio-data + DD.

➢ Step 5: Appear for document verification and Walk-in Interview.

Documents to carry: Academic certificates, professional qualification certificates, Date of Birth proof, caste certificate (if applicable), experience certificates, Bio-data form, Demand Draft (if applicable) and valid ID proof.
