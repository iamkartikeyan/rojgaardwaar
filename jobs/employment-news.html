About Employment News Paper::

Government of India every week publish Employment News Paper in both English and Hindi Version, named as “Employment News” and “Rozgar Samachar”. The Government Employment News Paper weekly publish Central Government, State Government, Banking, Railway and Public Sector Company Job Notifications and Advertisements. The Employment news Paper very useful for Government Job Seekers and Freshers. If you want Employment News E Paper PDF format,  Subscribe monthly or yearly Online through Employment News E-version Official Website e-employmentnews.co.in.

FAQs::

What is Employment News?

Employment News is a weekly newspaper. The Employment News papers provides latest govt job vacancies of Central, State Governments, Public Sector Undertakings, Autonomous Bodies, Universities, admission notices for professional courses, examination notices and exam results.

How to get Employment News Paper Online?

Here, rozgardwaar.com updates all latest Employment News paper Jobs details and Top vacancies in this page. Jobs seekers get complete employment news paper information's and apply links.

How to Subscribe Employment News?

A Candidate visit Employment News official website (www.employmentnews.gov.in) and click on e version tab or click on en.eversion to subscribed online or offline weekly newspapers. You can choose 6-Months, 1-year, 2-years and 3-years package.

What is Employment News paper subscription cost?

₹ 400 for 01 year, ₹ 750 for 02 years  and ₹ 1050 for 03 years subscription.
