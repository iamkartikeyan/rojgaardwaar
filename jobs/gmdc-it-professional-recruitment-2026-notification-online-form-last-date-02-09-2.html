GMDC IT Professional Recruitment 2026: Notification, Online Form | Last Date 02-09-2026

Published by: RozgarDwaar Editorial Desk | Updated: 2026-09-01

Gujarat Mineral Development Corporation Ltd. (GMDC), a Government of Gujarat Enterprise, has invited online applications from eligible candidates for recruitment to various IT Professional and other specialised positions on Fixed Term Employment Basis. The vacancies are primarily at the Corporate Office, Ahmedabad, with some posts at specific project locations.

The GMDC IT Professional Recruitment 2026 online application process is open, and the last date to apply is 02 September 2026. On this page, job aspirants can find all important details of GMDC Recruitment 2026, including the official notification and application link.

How to Apply for GMDC Recruitment 2026::

Eligible candidates must apply ONLINE ONLY through the official GMDC website. No other mode of application will be accepted.

➢ Step 1: Visit the official website www.gmdcltd.com.

➢ Step 2: Go to the “Current Career Opportunities” section.

➢ Step 3: Click on the relevant recruitment notification / Apply Online link.

➢ Step 4: Register / Login and fill the online application form carefully with personal, educational, experience and other required details.

➢ Step 5: Upload scanned copies of required documents (Photograph, Signature, Certificates, Experience proofs, etc.) as per the prescribed format.

➢ Step 6: Submit the application form before the last date and take a printout of the confirmation page for future reference.

Note: Candidates are advised to carefully read the detailed advertisement available on the Corporation’s website before applying.
