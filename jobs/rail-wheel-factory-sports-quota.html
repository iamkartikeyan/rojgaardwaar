Rail Wheel Factory Sports Quota Recruitment 2026 – Notification for 15 Posts, Application Form | Last Date 31-08-2026

Published by: RozgarDwaar Editorial Desk | Updated: 2026-08-31
Apply Online (Official Website) Download Notification PDF Official Website

Rail Wheel Factory (RWF), Yelahanka, Bengaluru under the Ministry of Railways, invites offline applications from eligible sportspersons for recruitment against Sports Quota for the year 2026-27. The RWF Sports Quota Recruitment 2026 notification has been released for 15 Group ‘C’ posts in Level-2 (GP 1900) and Level-1 (GP 1800). Interested and eligible candidates can submit applications offline by 24 August 2026. This article provides complete details about Rail Wheel Factory Sports Quota Recruitment 2026 including vacancy, sports eligibility, age limit, selection process and application procedure.

RWF Sports Quota 2026 Eligibility Criteria::

Criteria

Details

Age Limit (as on 01.01.2027)

18 to 25 years (Born between 02.01.2002 and 01.01.2009). No age relaxation admissible.

Educational Qualification (Level-2)

10th Pass + NAC/ITI in relevant trade OR 10th Pass (for training) OR 12th Pass (for Junior Clerk / Accounts Clerk)

Educational Qualification (Level-1)

10th Pass / ITI / National Apprenticeship Certificate (NAC)

Sports Eligibility

Recognized sports achievements in current or immediate previous two financial years. Must be active sportspersons (International / National Championships / Federation Cup / National Games / Inter University / World Rankings as applicable).

RWF Sports Quota 2026 Selection Process::
  • Document Verification
  • Sports Trial
  • Assessment of Sports Achievements
  • Educational Qualification Evaluation
  • Medical Examination

Distribution of Marks:

  • Sports Trial – 40 Marks
  • Sports Achievements – 50 Marks
  • Educational Qualification – 10 Marks
  • Total – 100 Marks

Minimum Qualifying Marks: Level-2 – 65 Marks | Level-1 – 60 Marks

RWF Sports Quota 2026 Application Fee::
  • General / OBC / EWS: ₹500/- (₹400 refundable after appearing in trials)
  • SC / ST / Ex-Servicemen / Minorities / EBC: ₹250/- (fully refundable after appearing in trials)
  • Fee payment only through Indian Postal Order (IPO) or Demand Draft (DD)

How to Apply for RWF Sports Quota Recruitment 2026?:

Eligible candidates must apply offline only. Follow these steps:

  • Step 1: Download the prescribed application form from the official RWF website.
  • Step 2: Fill the application form in English or Hindi using blue ballpoint pen.
  • Step 3: Affix a recent passport-size photograph and attach self-attested copies of educational certificates, sports achievements, caste certificate (if applicable), fee proof and other documents.
  • Step 4: Superscribe the envelope as “Application against Sports Quota (Open Advertisement) dated 25.07.2026 – Recruitment for the year 2026-27”.
  • Step 5: Send the completed application to:

The Assistant Personnel Officer
Personnel Department
Rail Wheel Factory (Ministry of Railways)
Administrative Building, Yelahanka
Bengaluru – 560064

Applications can also be dropped in the designated Drop Box at the Personnel Department before the last date.
