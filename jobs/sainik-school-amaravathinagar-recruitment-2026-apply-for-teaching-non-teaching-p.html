Sainik School Amaravathinagar Recruitment 2026 - Apply for Teaching & Non-Teaching Posts | Last Date 25-08-2026

Published by: RozgarDwaar Editorial Desk | Updated: 2026-08-29
Apply Online (Official Website) Download Notification PDF Official Website

Sainik School Ambikapur, Chhattisgarh, functioning under the Sainik Schools Society, Ministry of Defence, has invited applications from eligible candidates for Laboratory Assistant, PEM/PTI-cum-Matron, Band Master/Music Teacher and Ward Boy posts on regular and contractual basis.

The Sainik School Ambikapur Recruitment 2026 for 07 Vacancies application process started with the notification dated 22nd August 2026 and the last date to submit the prescribed application form is 11th September 2026. This article provides complete information on vacancy details, eligibility, pay, selection process, application fee, official notification and how to apply.

Sainik School Ambikapur 2026 Selection Process::

Shortlisted candidates will be called through email after document scrutiny. Selection stages are as follows:

  • Document Verification
  • Written Test
  • Class Demonstration (for Lab Assistant, PEM/PTI-cum-Matron and Band Master/Music Teacher)
  • Skill / Proficiency Test (for Ward Boy)
  • Interview and assessment of higher qualification & experience (as applicable)

For Laboratory Assistant, PEM/PTI-cum-Matron and Band Master/Music Teacher, the written test is of 100 marks (50% weightage), class demonstration 100 marks (30%), interview 100 marks (15%) and higher qualification & experience 5%. For Ward Boy, the written test is of 50 marks and the skill/proficiency test is of 50 marks. No walk-in candidate will be allowed. Reporting time is 08:30 hours on the test day. No TA/DA will be paid.

Sainik School Ambikapur 2026 Application Fee::
  • Regular post (Laboratory Assistant): ₹500/- (non-refundable)
  • Contractual posts: ₹300/- (non-refundable)
  • Payment Method: RTGS / NEFT / Digital payment only in favour of “Principal Sainik School Ambikapur”, payable at Ambikapur.

School Bank Details: Principal, Sainik School Ambikapur, State Bank of India, Main Branch, Ambikapur, Account No. 37923027067, IFSC: SBIN0000310. Applications without the prescribed fee will be rejected.

How to Apply Sainik School Ambikapur Recruitment 2026?:

Eligible candidates must apply in the prescribed application form available on the school website. The application steps are as follows:

  • Step 1: Download the official notification and prescribed application form from the school website.
  • Step 2: Fill personal, academic, experience, category and contact details carefully. Clearly mention email ID and mobile number.
  • Step 3: Pay the application fee through RTGS/NEFT/digital mode and attach the payment receipt/screenshot with UTR number.
  • Step 4: Attach self-attested copies of educational, experience and category certificates.
  • Step 5: Send the completed form so that it reaches Sainik School Ambikapur, Mendhra Kalan, Dist – Surguja, Chhattisgarh – 497001 on or before 11 September 2026. Applications received through email may be considered only if the prescribed fee is received by the last date.

Note: Laboratory Assistant is a regular post with Level-4 pay and other perks as per Sainik Schools Society rules. PEM/PTI-cum-Matron, Band Master/Music Teacher and Ward Boy are contractual posts for 11 months, extendable up to 3 years with a one-month break every year. Vacation pay is not applicable to contractual posts.
