UPPRPB UP Police 81000 Recruitment 2026 – Constable, SI & Other Posts | Latest Update

Published by: RozgarDwaar Editorial Desk | Updated: 2026-07-21
Apply Online (Official Website) Download Notification PDF Official Website

The Uttar Pradesh government has announced a massive recruitment drive for approximately 81,000 vacancies in the Uttar Pradesh Police Department for the 2026-27 recruitment cycle. The recruitment will be conducted in phases by the Uttar Pradesh Police Recruitment and Promotion Board (UPPRPB).

Chief Minister Yogi Adityanath announced that these 81,000+ posts will be filled with full transparency and as per reservation rules. The recruitment aims to modernise the Uttar Pradesh Police force and strengthen law and order in the state. Eligible candidates will be able to apply online in phases once the detailed notifications are released on the official website.

UP Police 81000 Recruitment 2026 Selection Process::

The selection process will be conducted in the following stages:

  • Stage 1: Computer Based Test (CBT) – Objective type exam covering General Knowledge, General Hindi, Reasoning, Mental Ability, Numerical Ability, and Current Affairs. (Law & Constitution for SI posts)
  • Stage 2: Physical Standard Test (PST) – Height, Chest, and other physical measurements as per post and category.
  • Stage 3: Physical Efficiency Test (PET) – Running test (qualifying in nature). Standards will vary post-wise and category-wise.
  • Stage 4: Document Verification
  • Stage 5: Medical Examination

How to Apply for UP Police 81000 Recruitment 2026?:

Once the application portal is activated, candidates can apply by following these steps:

  • Step 1: Visit the official website of UPPRPB (www.upprpb.in).
  • Step 2: Click on the relevant recruitment notification and read the eligibility criteria carefully.
  • Step 3: Register using a valid mobile number and email ID.
  • Step 4: Fill in the online application form with personal, educational, and other required details.
  • Step 5: Upload scanned copies of photograph, signature, and required documents.
  • Step 6: Pay the application fee through online mode (if applicable).
  • Step 7: Submit the application form and take a printout of the confirmation page for future reference.
