NGEL Manager Recruitment 2026 – Apply Online for 34 Posts | Last Date 31-08-2026

Published by: RozgarDwaar Editorial Desk | Updated: 2026-08-28
Apply Online (Official Website) Download Notification PDF Official Website

NTPC Green Energy Limited (NGEL), a subsidiary of NTPC Ltd., invites online applications for recruitment of Experienced Professionals for the posts of Manager-RE (E-4 Grade), Manager-Finance (E-4 Grade) and Manager-Land (E-4 Grade) on Regular Rolls. A total of 34 vacancies are available across India for Corporate/Station/Sites/Clusters/JVs and subsidiaries, offering excellent opportunities for experienced professionals in the renewable energy sector.

The NGEL Manager Recruitment 2026 online registration process began on 11 August 2026 and the last date to apply is 31 August 2026. On this page, job aspirants can find all important details of NGEL Manager Vacancy 2026, including the official notification and application link.

NGEL Manager Recruitment 2026 Eligibility Criteria::
  • Manager – RE (E-4 Grade): Full-time Graduate in Engineering (Electrical / Electrical & Electronics / Control & Instrumentation / Civil / Mechanical) with minimum 60% marks from a recognized University/Institution in India. Minimum 10 years post-qualification executive experience in Power Sector (Govt/PSU/Private), out of which at least 04 years in Business Development / Engineering / Contracts & Procurement / Commercial / Project Management / Corporate Planning / Strategy of Renewable Energy Developers having at least 50 MW or more grid-connected Solar and/or Wind and/or BESS Project(s).
  • Manager – Finance (E-4 Grade): CA / CMA (previously ICWA) qualified from institute recognized by appropriate statutory authority in India. Minimum 10 years post-qualification executive experience (excluding articleship, including training if any) in Finance/Accounts in Govt/PSU/Private Organization. Preference for candidates with SAP, MS Office, Data Analytics knowledge and those who have worked with RE Developer Company.
  • Manager – Land (E-4 Grade): Full-time PG Degree / PG Diploma / PG Programme in Rural Management / Rural Development / Displacement / Resettlement / Rehabilitation / Community Development / Local Governance / Local Development / Livelihoods / Social Entrepreneurship / Social Development / Social Administration / Sustainable Development / Development Policy & Practice / Development Studies or MSW or MBA or Graduate in Engineering with minimum 60% marks from a recognized University/Institution in India. Minimum 10 years post-qualification executive experience in land acquisition / land aggregation / land management, out of which at least 04 years for Renewable Energy developers having at least 50 MW or more grid-connected Solar and/or Wind and/or BESS Project(s). Preference for SAP, MS Office, Data Analytics knowledge.
  • For Govt/PSU candidates (E-4 level): Minimum 02 years experience in the scale of ₹60,000 – ₹1,80,000 or above (IDA E-3 or equivalent) out of the required 10 years experience.
  • Upper Age Limit: 40 years (relaxation as per Govt. of India / NGEL rules for reserved categories).
NGEL Manager Salary 2026::
  • Pay Scale: E-4 Grade / IDA (w.e.f. 01.01.2017): ₹70,000 – ₹2,00,000/-
  • Selected candidates will also be entitled to DA, HRA, other allowances, perquisites and benefits as per NGEL rules.
NGEL Manager Recruitment 2026 Selection Process::
  • Application Shortlisting / Screening (based on qualification, % of marks, years of experience etc.)
  • Written Test / Group Discussion / Interview (or combination thereof)
  • Management reserves the right to raise minimum eligibility standards or conduct multi-stage process as required.
NGEL Manager Recruitment 2026 Application Fee::
  • General / EWS / OBC: ₹500/- (non-refundable)
  • SC / ST / PwBD / XSM / Female candidates: Nil
  • Payment Mode: Online only through SBI Collect (Net Banking / Debit Card / Credit Card / UPI)

How to Apply for NGEL Manager Recruitment 2026::

Eligible candidates must apply ONLINE ONLY through the official NGEL careers portal. No other mode of application will be accepted.

➢ Step 1: Visit the official website www.ngel.in or directly go to https://careers.ngel.in/.

➢ Step 2: Click on the relevant advertisement “Recruitment of Experienced Professionals for the post of Manager-RE, Manager-Finance & Manager-Land (Advt. No. 03/26)”.

➢ Step 3: Register / Login and fill the online application form carefully with personal, educational, experience and other required details.

➢ Step 4: Upload all required self-attested documents (Class X certificate, Degree/Marksheets, Experience certificates, Caste/PwBD/EWS certificates, etc.) in the prescribed format.

➢ Step 5: Pay the application fee online (if applicable) through SBI Collect gateway.

➢ Step 6: Submit the application form and download/print the application slip with unique form number for future reference.

➢ Note: Applicants are advised to submit applications in a timely manner without waiting for the deadline. Keep a valid email ID active for all future correspondence.
