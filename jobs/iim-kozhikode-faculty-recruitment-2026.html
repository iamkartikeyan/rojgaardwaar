IIM Kozhikode Faculty Recruitment 2026 – Apply Online for 28 Posts | Last Date 30-09-2026

Published by: RozgarDwaar Editorial Desk | Updated: 2026-09-02
Apply Online Now Download Notification PDF ← Back to Portal Home

Indian Institute of Management Kozhikode (IIMK) has invited applications from Indian Nationals and Overseas Citizens of India (OCI) for faculty positions on a regular basis under Advertisement No. A-02/2026. Openings are for Assistant Professor Grade-I and Assistant Professor Grade-II in selected academic areas.

The IIM Kozhikode Faculty Recruitment 2026 online application process started on 17th August 2026 and will close on 30th September 2026 at 5:00 PM. This article covers vacancy details, specialisations, eligibility, publication norms, selection process, official notification and the apply-online link.

✅ IIM Kozhikode Faculty Recruitment 2026 Eligibility Criteria:

Post Name

Pay Level

Qualification, Experience & Publications

Assistant Professor Grade-I

Level 12 / 13A1

Ph.D. or equivalent with First Class (60% or above) in the preceding degree and an excellent academic record. Minimum 3 years teaching or equivalent research/industry experience after Ph.D. At least one journal paper in the specialisation in ABDC A or above / ABS 3 or above (WoS Q1 accepted in lieu for Corporate Law, Psychology, Mathematics, AI and Computer Science).

Assistant Professor Grade-II

Level 10 / 11 (contract)

Ph.D. with First Class in the preceding degree. Fresh Ph.D. holders or candidates with less than 3 years post-Ph.D. experience may apply. Significant contribution to at least one published journal paper in ABDC A or above / ABS 3 or above / WoS Q1 or Q2.

The Ph.D. must be from a recognised University / Deemed University or an approved autonomous institution meeting the Institute’s ranking requirements. Candidates may apply under only one area of specialisation.

✅ IIM Kozhikode Faculty Recruitment 2026 Selection Process:

Mere fulfilment of minimum eligibility does not guarantee a call. Shortlisting will follow Institute criteria.

  • Shortlisting of applications
  • Seminar
  • Interview at IIM Kozhikode / Kochi Campus

Shortlisted candidates will be given free travel and boarding within India for the seminar and interview.

✅ How to Apply IIM Kozhikode Faculty Recruitment 2026?

Eligible candidates must submit the online application after registration on the Institute faculty-recruitment portal. Apply under one specialisation only.

  • Step 1: Visit iimk.ac.in and complete registration.
  • Step 2: Fill the online form for the chosen area and post (Grade-I or Grade-II).
  • Step 3: Upload educational and experience certificates, job-market paper / publications and caste/category certificates, if applicable.
  • Step 4: Submit the application on or before 30 September 2026, 5:00 PM.
  • Step 5: Keep a copy of the submitted form for the seminar and interview.

✅ FAQs:

What are the posts in IIM Kozhikode Faculty Recruitment 2026?

Assistant Professor Grade-I (Level 12 / 13A1) and Assistant Professor Grade-II (Level 10 / 11, contract).

What is the qualification for IIM Kozhikode Faculty Recruitment 2026?

Ph.D. or equivalent in the relevant subject with First Class (60% or above) in the preceding degree, an excellent academic record and the prescribed journal publications.

What is the last date for IIM Kozhikode Faculty Recruitment 2026?

30 September 2026 up to 5:00 PM.

What is the selection process for IIM Kozhikode Faculty posts 2026?

Shortlisting followed by seminar and interview at IIM Kozhikode / Kochi Campus.

Can a candidate apply for more than one specialisation?

No. Candidates can apply under one area of specialisation only.

Is travel support provided for the IIM Kozhikode faculty interview?

Yes. Shortlisted candidates will receive free travel and boarding within India for the seminar and interview.
