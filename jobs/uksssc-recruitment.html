UKSSSC Recruitment 2026 – Apply Online 398 Kanisth Sahayak, Stenographer Posts | Last Date 06-07-2026

Published by: RozgarDwaar Editorial Desk | Updated: 2026-06-21
Apply Online (Official Website) Download Notification PDF Official Website

Uttarakhand Subordinate Services Selection Commission (UKSSSC) invites online applications from eligible candidates for 398 posts of Kanisth Sahayak (Junior Assistant) and Stenographer Grade-1/Personal Assistant in Civil Courts and Family Courts under the Nainital High Court, Uttarakhand.

The UKSSSC Kanisth Sahayak & Stenographer Recruitment 2026 for 398 Vacancies Online Application process starts from 22nd June 2026 and closes on 6th July 2026. This article provides complete information about UKSSSC Recruitment 2026, including post-wise vacancies, eligibility criteria, exam pattern, official notification PDF, and online application link.

UKSSSC Kanisth Sahayak & Stenographer Recruitment 2026:
rozgardwaar.com:

Post Name

Kanisth Sahayak (Junior Assistant) & Stenographer Grade-1 / Personal Assistant

Total Vacancies

398 Posts

Age Limit

21 to 35 years (as on 01.01.2026) with applicable relaxations

Level-03 / Level-05 / Level-07 (₹21,700 – ₹1,42,400)

Graduate + Typing / Shorthand Knowledge

Application Fee

₹300 (UR/OBC) | ₹150 (SC/ST/EWS/PwD) | ₹0 (Orphan)

Written Exam + Typing/Shorthand Skill Test + Document Verification

Uttarakhand (Civil & Family Courts)

06 July 2026

Court Type Pay Scale No. of Posts

Court Type Pay Scale No. of Posts
Civil Courts Level-05 (₹29,200 – ₹92,300) 128
Family Courts Level-07 (₹44,900 – ₹1,42,400) 11
Total Level-05 / Level-07 139
Eligibility Criteria::
Kanisth Sahayak (Junior Assistant):
  • Graduate from a recognized University.
  • Good knowledge of Hindi and English.
  • Hindi Typing speed: Minimum 25 WPM on Computer (English Typing 30 WPM preferred).
  • Basic knowledge of Computer Operation.
Stenographer Grade-1 / Personal Assistant:
  • Graduate from a recognized University.
  • Good knowledge of Hindi and English.
  • Hindi Shorthand: Minimum 80 WPM + Hindi Typing 25 WPM (English Shorthand 80 WPM & Typing 35 WPM preferred).
  • Basic knowledge of Computer Operation.
Selection Process::
  • Written Objective Type Competitive Examination (140 Marks)
  • Typing / Shorthand Skill Test (60 Marks) – Qualifying Nature
  • Document Verification
Application Fee::
  • Unreserved (UR) / OBC: ₹300/-
  • SC / ST / EWS / PwD (Uttarakhand): ₹150/-
  • Orphan (Anath): ₹0/-

How to Apply UKSSSC Recruitment 2026?:

Eligible candidates must apply online through the official UKSSSC website. The steps are as follows:-

  • Step 1: Visit the official website https://sssc.uk.gov.in and register using a valid mobile number and email ID.
  • Step 2: Fill in Personal Details, Educational Qualification, and other required information.
  • Step 3: Upload scanned Photograph, Signature, and Left Thumb Impression in the prescribed format.
  • Step 4: Pay the application fee online (if applicable).
  • Step 5: Review all details carefully and submit the application.
  • Step 6: Take a printout of the submitted application form for future reference.
