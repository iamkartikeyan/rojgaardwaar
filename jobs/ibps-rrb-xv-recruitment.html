IBPS RRB XV Recruitment 2026 – Apply Online for 13742+ Office Assistant and Officer Posts | Last Date 21-09-2026

Published by: RozgarDwaar Editorial Desk | Updated: 2026-09-01
Apply Online (Official Website) Download Notification PDF Official Website

IBPS CRP RRB V official Notification Out on 1st September 2026. Fresher and Experienced Graduates can apply online for Regional Rural Bank Vacancies from 1st September to 21st September 2026. The Institute of Banking Personnel Selection (IBPS) has announced and published the New Notification of Common Recruitment Process for Regional Rural Banks (CRP RRBs XV) to fill 13,000+ positions for Officers (Scale-I, II & III) and Office Assistants (Multipurpose) posts in participating Regional Rural Banks.

The IBPS RRB XV Recruitment 2026 online registration will start on 01st September 2026 and close on 21st September 2026. This article covers post details, eligibility, age limit, application fee, selection process, tentative exam schedule, official notification and application-related links.

IBPS RRB XV 2026 Selection Process::

The selection stages differ by post:

  • Officer Scale-I: Preliminary Exam → Main Exam → Interview → Provisional Allotment
  • Officer Scale-II & III: Single Online Examination → Interview → Provisional Allotment
  • Office Assistant (Multipurpose): Preliminary Exam → Main Exam → Provisional Allotment
IBPS RRB XV 2026 Application Fee::
  • General / OBC candidates: ₹850/-
  • SC / ST / PwBD / Ex-Servicemen candidates: ₹175/-
  • Payment Method: Online through Net Banking, Debit/Credit Card or UPI.

How to Apply IBPS RRB XV Recruitment 2026?:

Eligible candidates must apply online only through the official IBPS website. No other mode of application will be accepted. The steps are as follows:

  • Step 1: Visit www.ibps.in and open the CRP RRBs-XV apply online link (active from 01 September 2026).
  • Step 2: Complete new registration with a valid email ID and mobile number and note the provisional registration number and password.
  • Step 3: Fill personal, educational and post-related details carefully. Apply separately for each post.
  • Step 4: Upload photograph, signature and other documents as prescribed in the detailed notification.
  • Step 5: Pay the application / intimation charges online, submit the form and take a printout of the completed application for future reference.

FAQs::
What is the total number of vacancies in IBPS RRB XV Recruitment 2026?:

More than 10,000 posts are expected. Exact RRB-wise and post-wise vacancies will be given in the detailed notification.

What is the age limit for IBPS RRB XV Recruitment 2026?:

Office Assistant: 18–28 years; Officer Scale-I: 18–30 years; Officer Scale-II: 21–32 years; Officer Scale-III: 21–40 years, with category-wise relaxation.

What is the salary in IBPS RRB XV Recruitment 2026?:

Pay and allowances will be as per the rules of the allotted Regional Rural Bank.

What is the selection process for IBPS RRB XV 2026?:

Prelims and Mains for Office Assistant and Officer Scale-I; Single Exam and Interview for Officer Scale-II & III. Office Assistant has no interview.

What is the application fee for IBPS RRB XV Recruitment 2026?:

₹850 for General/OBC candidates and ₹175 for SC/ST/PwBD/Ex-Servicemen candidates.

What are the important dates for IBPS RRB XV Recruitment 2026?:

Online Registration: 01 September 2026 to 21 September 2026.
