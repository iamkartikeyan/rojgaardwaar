Assam Forest Recruitment 2020 Apply Online 1081 Vacancies | Last date 25/06/20

Published by: RozgarDwaar Editorial Desk | Updated: 2020-06-25
Apply Online (Official Website) Download Notification PDF Official Website

Assam Forest Job Vacancy 2020 for Class VIII, Higher Secondary, HSLC and Degree holders. State Level Police Recruitment Board (SLPRB) Assam has published Forest Recruitment advertisement of Forester, Stenographer posts, Forest Guard, Surveyor, Mahut, Carpenter and Driver posts under Forest Department, Government of Assam state. Apply Online through forest2020.slprbassam.in. The closing date for submission of online applications is 25th June 2020.

✅ Age Limit:

✔️ For Forester and Stenographer: 18 - 38 years as on 1st January, 2020 (candidate must be born on or before 01-01-2002 and on or after 01-01-1982)
✔️ For Forest Guard, Surveyor, Mahut, Carpenter and Driver: 18 - 38 years as on 1st January, 2020 (candidate must be born on or before 01-01-2002 and on or after 01-01-1982)
✔️ Age relaxation: 05 years for SC, ST (H), ST (P), 03 years for OBC / MOBC.

✅ Pay Scales:

✔️ Forester-I: ₹ 14000-60500 + GP ₹ 6400
✔️ Stenographer (Grade-III): ₹ 14000-60500 + GP ₹ 8700
✔️ Forest Guards: ₹ 14000-60500 + GP ₹ 5600
✔️ Surveyor: ₹ 14000-60500 + GP ₹ 6200
✔️ Mahut / Carpenter: ₹ 14000-60500 + GP ₹ 5000
✔️ Driver: ₹ 14000-60500 + GP ₹ 5200

✅ Educational Qualification and Experience:

✔️ Forester: Bachelor's Degree in any discipline from a recognized University or any Examination declared equivalent by the Government.
✔️ Stenographer Grade- III: Graduate in any stream from a recognized College/ Institutions affiliated to a recognized University with having National Trade Certificate in stenography from ITI in Assam or any other institute recognized by the Govt. of Assam / Govt. of India with 80 WPM in English Stenography.
✔️ Forest Guard: Higher Secondary (10+2) or equivalent examination passed from a recognized Board/ Council.
✔️ Surveyor: HSLC or equivalent examination passed and must have certificate in Draftsmanship from a recognized Institute.
✔️ Mahut: Class VIII or equivalent and must have minimum 03 years experience in elephant upkeeping.
✔️ Carpenter: HSLC or equivalent examination passed and must have trade certificate from recognized Institution.
✔️ Driver: HSLC or equivalent examination passed and must have valid four wheeler driving license issued by the any Competent Authority of Assam.

✅ Physical Standards:

✔️ For Forester:

(1) Height: Minimum 162.56 cm for General / OBC / SC / ST; 153 cm for ST (H) / ST (P).
(2) Chest (Men Only): 79 to 84 cm.
(3) Weight: Proportionate to height & age, as per medical standard.

✔️ For Forest Guard:

(1) Height: Minimum 163 cm (Male) / 150 cm (Female) for General / OBC / SC; 153 cm (Male) / 145 cm (Female) for ST (H) / ST (P).
(2) Chest (Men Only): 79 to 84 cm.
(3) Weight: Proportionate to height & age, as per medical standard.

✅ Selection Process:

✔️ First Phase Test
✔️ Second Phase Test

✅ Application Fee: There will be no application fee.

✅ How to Apply: Eligible Interested candidates should apply online through SLPRB Assam Forest 2020 Online Application Portal. The last date for registration of online applications is 25/06/2020.
