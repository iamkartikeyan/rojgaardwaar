StockHolding Executive Recruitment 2026 - Apply Online for 65 Posts | Last Date 30-08-2026

Stock Holding Corporation of India Limited (StockHolding), A Government of India Company has released vacancy notification for Fresher Graduate and Post Graduates for Fixed Term Engagement of Executives. A total of 65 vacancies are available across various states for the post of Executive (FT) under Vacancy Code HWD/Vacancy/26-27/FTEREG/01.

The StockHolding Executive Recruitment 2026 online application process began on 19 August 2026, and the last date to apply is 30 August 2026 (up to 6:00 PM). On this page, aspirants can find all important details of StockHolding Executive Vacancy 2026, including the official notification and application link.

StockHolding Executive Recruitment 2026 Eligibility Criteria::
  • Nationality: Only Indian Citizens are eligible to apply.
  • Educational Qualification (as on 30.06.2026): Graduate / Post Graduate in any discipline with a minimum 50% aggregate marks from a recognised University/Institution. Candidates must enter only the higher percentage of marks obtained either in Graduation or Post Graduation.
  • Age Limit (as on 30.06.2026): Minimum 21 years and Maximum 35 years (Candidate must have been born between 30.06.1991 and 30.06.2005 – both dates inclusive).
  • Terms of Contract: Fixed Term Engagement for 3 years or co-terminus with the completion / extension of the project, whichever is earlier. Tenure can be extended by 2 years at the discretion of SHCIL.
StockHolding Executive Salary / CTC 2026::
  • Annual CTC of approximately ₹3.93 Lakhs per annum (excludes Group Medical Insurance, Group Life Insurance and Group Accident Insurance, etc.).
Job Description::
  • Handling of day-to-day operations including handling of cash/cheque at Branch / Regional / HO Level.
  • Attending to Customer requirements over the counter.
  • Maintaining and updating customer data base.
  • Safe keeping of documents/records.
  • Any other role and responsibility which may be assigned as per requirement of the Corporation.
StockHolding Executive Recruitment 2026 Selection Process::
  • Preliminary Screening
  • Online Test
  • Interview
  • Document Verification (can be conducted at any stage)

Shortlisting:

  • Online Test: Up to 30 times the number of vacancies (based on aggregate percentage of marks or other criteria).
  • Interview: Up to 10 times the number of vacancies (based on Online Test merit).

Final Merit: Prepared state-wise based on performance in the Interview. A waiting list (valid for 6 months) may be maintained at the discretion of StockHolding.

Online Test Pattern::
Subject / Section
Logical Ability
Spatial Reasoning
Quantitative Ability
Basic Numerical Ability

Duration: Approximately 1 hour.
It is mandatory to pass in each Subject/Section (Minimum 40% in each section and overall 40%). Online Test is scheduled on 06 September 2026.

How to Apply for StockHolding Executive Recruitment 2026::

Eligible candidates must apply ONLINE ONLY through the official StockHolding website. No other mode of application will be accepted.

➢ Step 1: Visit the official careers page or direct apply link online.stockholding.com.

➢ Step 2: Register / Login and fill the online application form carefully with personal, educational and other required details.

➢ Step 3: Enter the higher percentage of marks (Graduation or Post Graduation).

➢ Step 4: Upload the required documents as per instructions.

➢ Step 5: Submit the application form before 30 August 2026 (6:00 PM) and take a printout of the confirmation page for future reference.

Note: Only system-related queries may be emailed to recruitment@stockholding.com with screenshot. Merit list will be prepared state-wise.
