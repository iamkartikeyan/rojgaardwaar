HPSSSB Recruitment 2021 Apply Online | 15 Clerk Vacancies | hpsssb.hp.gov.in

Published by: RozgarDwaar Editorial Desk | Updated: 2021-09-17
Apply Online (Official Website) Download Notification PDF Official Website

HPSSSB Recruitment 2021 hpsssb.hp.gov.in: Himachal Pradesh Staff Selection Commission (HPSSC) Hamirpur, District Hamirpur invites applications from eligible candidates for recruitment of Clerk for fill up 15 vacancies on direct recruitment basis. The last date for submission of applications is 25th September 2021.

Advertisement No. 37-2/2021:

Name of Post

Total Vacancies

Clerk

15

✅ Age Limit:

✅ Educational Qualifications: Regular Class-IV employees of the Departments of the Government of Himachal Pradesh possessing 10+2 or its equivalent qualification having 05 years regular service OR regular combined with continuous service rendered on daily wages or on contract basis, failing which by direct recruitment on a regular basis or by recruitment on contract basis, as the case may be.

✅ Selection Process:

✔️ Written Objective type Screening Test
✔️ Typing Skill Test

✅ Application Fee:

✔️ ₹ 360/- for General category candidates.
✔️ ₹ 120/- for Reserved category candidates.
✔️ The fee should be paid either through a Crossed Bank Draft or Crossed Indian Postal Order drawn in favour of the Secretary, H.P. Staff Selection Commission Hamirpur. The Demand draft should be payable at any Bank branch located at Hamirpur.

✅ How to Apply: The application in prescribed format on A-4 size paper. Candidates should also attach one self addressed envelope of 9”X4” (inch) size alongwith Rs. 5/- postage stamp pasted on it with the application form from 26th August 2021 to 25th September 2021. For the candidates serving in Lahaul & Spiti, Kinnaur District, Pangi & Bharmour Sub-Divisions of Chamba District and Dodra Kwar Sub-Division of Shimla District of HP on or before 10/10/2021.

FAQs:

✅ How to Apply HPSSSB Recruitment 2021?

Eligible candidates apply online for direct recruitment of various posts using the HPSSSB Himachal Pradesh website @ www.hpsssb.hp.gov.in. The online application can be filled up from 10th April 2021 to 30th May 2021 till 11:59 PM.

✅ How many vacancies in HPSSSB?

Total 379 vacancies of Nurse, Pharmacist, Junior Engineer, Technician and other posts in various departments of Himachal Pradesh state.

✅ How to contact HPSSSB for Job related queries?

In case of any guidance/ information/ clarification regarding HPSSSB Online Recruitment Applications (ORA), candidature etc. candidates may contact HPSSC Reception Counter in person or on Phone No. 01972-222204,222211, Toll Free No. 1800-180-8095 or on email ID i.e. sssb-hp@nic.in on any working day between 10:00 A.M. to 05:00 P.M.

✅ What is the selection process of HPSSSB?

✔️ Objective Type Screening Test (170 Multiple Choice Questions)
✔️ Skill Test
