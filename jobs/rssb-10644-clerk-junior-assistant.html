RSSB 10644 Clerk & Junior Assistant Recruitment 2026 – Notification, Online Form

Published by: RozgarDwaar Editorial Desk | Updated: 2026-02-11
Apply Online (Official Website) Download Notification PDF Official Website

Rajasthan Staff Selection Board (RSSB / RSMSSB), Jaipur has released the official notification for direct recruitment to 10,644 posts of Clerk Grade-II / Junior Assistant in various departments including Rajasthan Public Service Commission, Administrative Reforms Department, Rajasthan State Agriculture Marketing Board, Directorate of Agriculture Marketing, Rajasthan State Pollution Control Board, and Board of Secondary Education, Rajasthan.

This recruitment is being conducted through the Common Eligibility Test (Senior Secondary Level) – 2024, and only candidates who have been declared qualified in the CET (Sr. Secondary Level) 2024 are eligible to submit online applications. The posts are divided into Non-Scheduled (Non-TSP) and Scheduled (TSP) areas, offering a significant opportunity for 12th passed candidates with required computer qualifications and knowledge of Hindi Devnagari script and Rajasthan culture.

Candidates are strongly advised to carefully read the detailed RSSB Clerk / Junior Assistant Recruitment 2026 notification before applying to confirm eligibility regarding age, qualification, CET qualification, category, and other criteria.

RSSB Clerk / Junior Assistant 2026 Age Limit (As on 01 January 2027)::
  • Minimum Age: 18 Years
  • Maximum Age: 40 Years
  • Age relaxation applicable as per Rajasthan Government rules for reserved categories.
RSSB Clerk / Junior Assistant 2026 Eligibility Criteria::
  • Educational Qualification: Senior Secondary (12th) from recognized board.
  • Computer Qualification: Possess any one of: O Level / CCC / COPA / DPCS / Diploma / Degree / Certificate in Computer Science / Application / RSCIT / Equivalent.
  • Other: Working knowledge of Hindi written in Devnagari script and knowledge of Rajasthan culture.
  • Mandatory: Must be qualified in CET (Senior Secondary Level) – 2024 conducted by RSSB.

How to Apply RSSB Clerk / Junior Assistant Recruitment 2026::

Eligible candidates (CET 2024 qualified) must apply ONLINE only through the Rajasthan SSO Portal.

➢ Step 1: Visit sso.rajasthan.gov.in from 15 January 2026.

➢ Step 2: Register / Login using SSO ID.

➢ Step 3: Click on Recruitment Portal and select the Clerk / Junior Assistant application.

➢ Step 4: Fill the online form accurately, upload documents, photo, signature.

➢ Step 5: Pay the application fee online.

➢ Step 6: Submit the application before 13 February 2026 and take a printout.
