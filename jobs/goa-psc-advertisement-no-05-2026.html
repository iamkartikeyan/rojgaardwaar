Goa PSC Advertisement No 05 2026 - Apply Online for 25 Various Posts | Last Date 22 May

Published by: RozgarDwaar Editorial Desk | Updated: 2026-05-20
Apply Online (Official Website) Download Notification PDF Official Website

Goa Public Service Commission (GPSC) has released Advertisement No. 05/2026 dated 08.05.2026 for recruitment to 25 posts under various departments of the Government of Goa. Online applications are invited from eligible candidates for direct recruitment as well as transfer on deputation posts.

The Goa PSC Advertisement No. 05 2026 online registration starts immediately and the last date for filling up the applications (except deputation posts) is 22nd May 2026. This article provides complete information about Goa PSC Advt 05/2026, including vacancy details, eligibility criteria, application fee, selection process, and online application link.

How to Apply Goa PSC Advertisement No. 05 2026?:

Eligible candidates must apply online only. The steps are as follows:

  • Step 1: Visit https://cbes.goa.gov.in/landing or https://gpsc.goa.gov.in
  • Step 2: Read the instructions and advertisement carefully.
  • Step 3: Fill the online application form with all required details.
  • Step 4: Pay the application fee online or generate e-challan and pay at any SBI branch in Goa.
  • Step 5: Submit the form and download/print the application for future reference.
