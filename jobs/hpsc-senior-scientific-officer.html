HPSC Senior Scientific Officer Recruitment 2026 – Apply Online for 17 Vacancies

Published by: RozgarDwaar Editorial Desk | Updated: 2026-01-13
Apply Online (Official Website) Download Notification PDF Official Website

Haryana Public Service Commission (HPSC), Panchkula has released an official notification for the recruitment of Senior Scientific Officer (Scene of Crime) (Group-B) posts in the Forensic Science Laboratory, Madhuban, Karnal (Haryana). A total of 17 vacancies are available under Advertisement No. 20/2026.

Eligible candidates can apply online through the HPSC official website. Applicants are advised to carefully read the detailed notification before applying.

HPSC Senior Scientific Officer Recruitment 2026:
rozgardwaar.com :
🏢 Organization Haryana Public Service Commission (HPSC)
💼 Post Name Senior Scientific Officer (Scene of Crime) – Group B
📊 Total Vacancies 17 Posts
💰 Pay Scale FPL-9, Cell-1, ₹53,100 – ₹1,67,800
🎯 Age Limit 20 to 45 Years
🎓 Qualification M.Sc + 3 Years Experience
📝 Selection Process Screening Test + Subject Test + Interview
📍 Job Location Karnal, Haryana
⏰ Last Date 18 February 2026 (05:00 PM)
HPSC Senior Scientific Officer 2026 Age Limit:
  • Minimum Age: 20 Years
  • Maximum Age: 45 Years
  • Relaxation: 05 Years for SC / BC / Disabled / Women
HPSC Senior Scientific Officer 2026 Eligibility Criteria:
  • M.Sc (Minimum 2nd Division) in: Zoology / Botany / Chemistry / Physical Anthropology / Human Biology / Human Genetics / Biotechnology / Molecular Biology / Biochemistry / Microbiology / Physics / Mathematics / Statistics / Forensic Science
  • Minimum 03 Years research & analytical experience
  • Hindi or Sanskrit as a subject in Matric or Higher Education
HPSC Senior Scientific Officer 2026 Application Fee:
Category Fee
PwBD (Haryana) NIL
OSC, DSC, BC-A, BC-B (NCL), ESM, EWS, Women (Haryana) ₹250/-
DESM (UR Category) ₹1000/-
All Other Candidates ₹1000/-
HPSC Senior Scientific Officer 2026 Selection Process:
  • Screening Test
  • Subject Knowledge Test
  • Interview
How to Apply HPSC Senior Scientific Officer Recruitment 2026:

Eligible candidates must apply ONLINE through the HPSC official website.

➢ Step 1: Visit hpsc.gov.in

➢ Step 2: Complete registration and fill the application form

➢ Step 3: Upload required documents

➢ Step 4: Pay application fee (if applicable)

➢ Step 5: Submit the form before 18 February 2026 (05:00 PM)

Documents Required:-

  • Scanned Photograph
  • Scanned Signature
  • Educational Certificates & Mark Sheets
  • Experience Certificates
