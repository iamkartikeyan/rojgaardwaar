RSSB Forester Recruitment 2026 Apply Online for 259 Vacancies | Last Date 04.02.2026

Published by: RozgarDwaar Editorial Desk | Updated: 2026-02-04
Apply Online (Official Website) Download Notification PDF Official Website

Rajasthan Staff Selection Board (RSSB / RSMSSB), Jaipur, has released the detailed notification for direct recruitment to 259 Forester posts under the Forest Department. Eligible candidates can submit online applications from 06 January 2026 onwards. The board encourages applications from all eligible candidates, including women, to ensure diversity in the workforce.

The Candidates are strongly advised to thoroughly read the official RSSB Forester Recruitment 2026 notification before applying to verify eligibility conditions such as age, educational qualification, category reservations, and other requirements.

RSSB Forester 2026 Age Limit::
  • Minimum Age: 18 Years
  • Maximum Age: 40 Years (as on 01 January 2027)
  • Age relaxation applicable as per Rajasthan Government rules for reserved categories.
RSSB Forester 2026 Eligibility Criteria::
  • Educational Qualification: Senior Secondary (10+2) from a recognized Board + ITI Certificate in Civil Survey or Diploma in Civil Engineering from a recognized institution.
  • Mandatory: Must have passed RSSB CET (Senior Secondary Level) 2024.
  • Working knowledge of Hindi in Devanagari Script and knowledge of Rajasthani culture.

How to Apply RSSB Forester Recruitment 2026::

Eligible candidates must apply ONLINE only through the Rajasthan SSO Portal.

➢ Step 1: Visit sso.rajasthan.gov.in and login/register from 06 January 2026.

➢ Step 2: Go to Recruitment Portal and select Forester Recruitment 2026 application.

➢ Step 3: Fill the form with accurate details and upload required documents.

➢ Step 4: Pay the application fee online (if applicable).

➢ Step 5: Submit the application before 04 February 2026 (11:59 PM) and keep a printout for reference.
