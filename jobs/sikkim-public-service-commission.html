Sikkim Public Service Commission invites application from eligible local candidates for recruitment of Post Graduate Teachers (PGT) under Human Resource Development Department, Government of Sikkim through direct recruitment on temporary regular basis.

ADVERTISEMENT NO. 09/SPSC

Name of Post

Total Vacancies

Scale of Pay

Post Graduate Teachers (PGT)

125 (Political Science - 05, History - 08, Geography - 09, Economics - 08, Sociology - 04, Chemistry - 08, Physics - 12, Mathematics - 12,  Commerce - 09, Hindi - 16, Biology - 05, Nepali - 14, English – 17)

PB-2 of Rs. 9300-34800 + GP of Rs.5000/-

Age Limit: 18 to 30 Years.

Age Relaxation -> 5 years for SC/ST and BL candidates, four (4) years for MBC and three (3) years for OBC candidates, respectively. In the case of Government servant not more than 40 years.

Minimum Educational Qualifications:

(a) Master's Degree in respective subject with B.Ed.
(b) Master Degree without B.Ed. with 50% and above for General Category and 45% and
above for reserved category in respective subject can also apply. On selection, they
shall be given conditional appointment and they should acquire the B.Ed. qualification
within 3 (Three) years, failing which their service is liable to be terminated.

Selection Process:

1st Stage -> Written Examination (there will be 3 papers)
a. General English (Subjective) - 50 Marks (1 hour)
b. General Knowledge (Objective) - 50 Marks (1 hour)
c. Main Subjects (Subjective) - 300 Marks (3 hours).

2nd Stage -> After qualifying the Written Examination. Classroom demonstration/Personality Test - 50 Marks

How To Apply: The form should be filled up by the candidate himself/herself and submitted in the office of Joint Director of HRDD in their respective district along with original Bank Receipt of State Bank of Sikkim, for Rs. 150/- credited to '0051-SPSC'.
