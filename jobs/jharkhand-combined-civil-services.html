Jharkhand Combined Civil Services Examination 2026 - Apply Online for 103 Posts

Published by: RozgarDwaar Editorial Desk | Updated: 2026-02-03
Apply Online (Official Website) Download Notification PDF Official Website

JHARKHAND PUBLIC SERVICE COMMISSION (JPSC), Ranchi, invites online applications from eligible candidates for the Combined Civil Services Examination 2026 (Advt. No. 01/2026) to fill various Group-A & Group-B posts in different departments of Government of Jharkhand. The JPSC Combined Civil Services Recruitment 2026 for 103 Vacancies Online Registration starts from 31st January 2026 and closes on 14th February 2026 (05:00 PM). Last date for fee payment is 16th February 2026.

How to Apply JPSC Combined Civil Services Recruitment 2026:

Eligible candidates must apply ONLINE only through the JPSC official portal. No other mode of application will be accepted.

➢ Step 1: Visit https://www.jpsc.gov.in/online_application.php

➢ Step 2: Register with valid email ID and mobile number.

➢ Step 3: Fill the online application form with personal, educational, and category details.

➢ Step 4: Upload scanned photograph, signature, and required documents.

➢ Step 5: Pay the application fee online.

➢ Step 6: Submit the application and take a printout of the confirmation page for future reference.

Frequently Asked Questions::
What is the total number of vacancies in JPSC Combined Civil Services Examination 2026?:

103 posts across various Group-A & Group-B services including Deputy Collector, DSP, Probation Officer, Jail Superintendent, etc.

What is the age limit for JPSC Combined Civil Services Recruitment 2026?:

Minimum 21 Years; Maximum: 35 Years (Unreserved), 37 Years (Extremely BC), 38 Years (Female), 40 Years (SC/ST), 35 Years (EWS) as on 01/08/2026. Additional 5 years for Ex-Servicemen.

What is the educational qualification required for JPSC Combined Civil Services?:

Bachelor’s Degree in Any Stream from a recognized University.

What is the selection process for JPSC Combined Civil Services 2026?:

Preliminary Exam (Objective) → Main Exam (Written + Interview) → Document Verification → Medical Examination.

What is the application fee for JPSC Combined Civil Services Recruitment 2026?:

₹100/- for General/OBC/EWS; ₹50/- for SC/ST candidates, payable online.

What are the important dates for JPSC Combined Civil Services Recruitment 2026?:

Notification: 29 January 2026 | Application Starts: 31 January 2026 | Last Date: 14 February 2026 (05:00 PM) | Fee Last Date: 16 February 2026.
