Sainik School Jhunjhunu Recruitment 2026 – Apply for 18 Various Regular & Contractual Posts | Last Date 12-09-2026

Published by: RozgarDwaar Editorial Desk | Updated: 2026-08-26
Apply Online (Official Website) Download Notification PDF Official Website

Sainik School Jhunjhunu (Rajasthan), a residential school functioning under Sainik Schools Society, Ministry of Defence, invites offline applications from eligible Indian citizens for 18 Regular and Contractual posts including PGT, TGT, Counsellor, Lab Assistant, UDC, LDC, Driver and other positions.

The Sainik School Jhunjhunu Recruitment 2026 for Various Regular & Contractual Posts notification was issued on 22nd August 2026. Offline applications must reach the school by 12th September 2026. This article covers complete details of SS Jhunjhunu Vacancies 2026 including eligibility, pay scale, selection process, application fee and official notification PDF.

How to Apply Sainik School Jhunjhunu Recruitment 2026?:

Eligible candidates must apply offline separately for each post. The application steps are as follows:

  • Step 1: Download the prescribed application form from the Recruitment tab of www.ssjhunjhunu.com.
  • Step 2: Fill the form completely and mention percentage of marks from Class 10 onwards.
  • Step 3: Attach self-attested copies of all certificates, two passport size photographs and the Demand Draft of prescribed fee.
  • Step 4: Write “APPLICATION FOR THE POST OF ______” in capital letters on the envelope.
  • Step 5: Send the application through Speed Post only to The Principal, Sainik School Jhunjhunu, Vill & Post – Dorasar, District – Jhunjhunu (Rajasthan) – 333021 so as to reach on or before 12 September 2026. Applications sent by hand or other modes will not be accepted.
