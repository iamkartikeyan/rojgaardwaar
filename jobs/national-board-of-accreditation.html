National Board of Accreditation Recruitment 2026: Apply for 07 Manager, Director Posts | Last Date 31-08-2026

Published by: RozgarDwaar Editorial Desk | Updated: 2026-08-28
Apply Online (Official Website) Download Notification PDF Official Website

National Board of Accreditation (NBA), an autonomous organisation under the Ministry of Education, Government of India, invites applications in the prescribed proforma for filling up various posts on deputation / fixed tenure basis.

NBA Recruitment 2026 notification has been released for the posts of Senior Director (Accreditation), Manager and Deputy Manager. Interested and eligible candidates can submit their applications by 31 August 2026. This article provides complete details about National Board of Accreditation Recruitment 2026 including vacancy, eligibility, pay level, selection process and application procedure.

How to Apply for NBA Recruitment 2026?:

Eligible candidates must submit the application in offline mode only. Follow these steps:

  • Step 1: Download the prescribed Application Form (Annexure-V) from the official website.
  • Step 2: Fill the application form carefully and attach attested copies of all required documents.
  • Step 3: Send the duly filled application form along with documents to the following address:

The Member Secretary,
National Board of Accreditation,
4th Floor, NBCC Place, East Tower,
Bhisham Pitamah Marg, Pragati Vihar,
New Delhi – 110003

The application must reach by 17:00 hours on 31 August 2026. Applications received after the closing date will not be entertained.
