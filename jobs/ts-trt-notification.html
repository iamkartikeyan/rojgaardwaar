TS TRT Notification 2023 | 5089 Vacancies | tsdsc.aptonline.in Apply Online

Published by: RozgarDwaar Editorial Desk | Updated: 2023-10-28
Apply Online (Official Website) Download Notification PDF Official Website

Telangana TS DSC Notification 2023 of 5089 Teacher Vacancies, Online Application Form Available on or before 28th October 2023: Telangana Departmental Selection Committee (DSC), Government of Telangana has published recruitment notification of TS TRT or TS DSC 2023 for selection of 6612 Teacher Vacancies including 5089 posts for School Education and 1523 posts for Special Education Teachers. The TS DSC conducts Telangana Teachers Recruitment Test (TRT) examination scheduled to be held on 20th November to 30th November 2023 dates.

The TS TRT Online Application 2023 process start from 20th September 2023 and registration process end on 21st October 2023. The TS DSC TRT Exam 2023 for selection of School Assistants (S.A), Secondary Grade Teachers (S.G.T), Language Pandit Teachers (L.P.T), Physical Education Teachers (P.E.T) in Telangana state governments schools, Zilla Parishad, Mandal Parishad Schools and Schools Under the control of the Tribal Welfare Department and Municipalities, Telangana.

✅ TS DSC Application Last Date 2023: The last date for submission of Telangana TRT Vacancy 2023 Online Registration process extended up to 28th October 2023.

✅ TS DSC Exam Date 2023: Considering the General Assembly Elections scheduled for November 30, 2023, TSDSC have made the decision to postpone the DSC (Teacher Recruitment) Examinations that were originally planned from November 20 to November 30, 2023. The TSDSC will announce the new examination dates at a later time.

✅ TS DSC Recruitment 2023 Age Limit:

✔️ Minimum 18 years and Maximum 44 years as on 1st July 2023.
✔️ Age Relaxation: 05 years for SC/ ST/ BCs & EWS, 10 years for Physically Handicapped, 03 years for Ex-Servicemen and Up to 05 years for Telangana State Government.

✅ TS DSC Recruitment 2023 Salary: Level - 10 of 7th CPC (₹ 56100 - 177500)

✅ TS DSC Recruitment 2023 Eligibility Criteria:

✔️ Bachelor's Degree in relevant discipline / subjects.
✔️ Bachelor's degree in education (B.Ed.) from recognized university / Institute.
✔️ B.A.B.Ed / B.Sc.B.Ed from any institution recognized by NCTE with the concerned subject.

✅ How to Apply TS DSC Recruitment 2023:

➢ Eligible and Interested candidates can apply online through Telangana DSC Online Application Portal (tsdsc.aptonline.in) (OR) Telangana Public Service Commission Official Website (tspsc.gov.in).
➢ The candidates are required to fill basic details and create your account (Username and Password).
➢ After Registration, The candidates should login through TS TRT 2023 Online Recruitment Portal.
➢ After Login, the candidates can apply Telangana TRT Notification 2023 Apply Online.
➢ The candidates are required to fill application fee and upload scanned copy of Passport size photograph, Signature etc.
➢ The last date for submission of online applications is 21/10/2023 (Saturday).
