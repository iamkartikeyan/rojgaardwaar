Goa Police Recruitment 2021 Apply 1178 Constable, SI, ASI, Steno, Clerk Vacancies

Published by: RozgarDwaar Editorial Desk | Updated: 2021-05-26
Apply Online (Official Website) Download Notification PDF Official Website

Government of Goa, Office of the Director General of Police, Goa invites applications from eligible candidates for direct recruitment of following Police Sub Inspector (SI), Police Constable, Searcher, Assistant Sub Inspector (ASI), Photographer, Laboratory Technician, Stenographer and Lower Division Clerk posts. The last date for receipt of applications is 26th May 2021.

✅ Age Limit:

✔️ Police Sub Inspector: 20 to 28 years
✔️ Police Constable: 18 to 28 years
✔️ Searcher: Not exceeding 45 years
✔️ Assistant Sub Inspector (Wireless Operator): Not exceeding 45 years
✔️ Photographer, Lab Technician: Not exceeding 45 years
✔️ Police Constable (Bandman): Not exceeding 45 years
✔️ Police Constable (Mast Luskar): 18 to 22 years
✔️ Police Constable (Wireless Messenger): Not exceeding 45 years
✔️ Stenographer: Not exceeding 45 years
✔️ Lower Division Clerk: Not exceeding 45 years

✅ Pay Scales:

✔️ Police Sub Inspector: Pay Matrix Level 6 ₹ 35400 - 112400
✔️ Police Constable: Pay Matrix Level 2 ₹ 19900 - 63200
✔️ Searcher: Pay Matrix Level 6 ₹ 35400 - 112400
✔️ Assistant Sub Inspector (Wireless Operator): Pay Matrix Level 4 ₹ 25500 - 81100
✔️ Photographer: Pay Matrix Level 2 ₹ 19900 - 63200
✔️ Laboratory Technician: Pay Matrix Level 4 ₹ 25500 - 81100
✔️ Police Constable: Pay Matrix Level 2 ₹ 19900 - 63200
✔️ Stenographer: Pay Matrix Level 4 ₹ 25500 - 81100
✔️ Lower Division Clerk: Pay Matrix Level 2 ₹ 19900 - 63200

✅ Educational Qualifications:

✔️ Police Sub Inspector: Degree (OR) Higher Secondary School Certificate Exam (10+2) Pass. 03 years Diploma in Security and Investigation Technology.

✔️ Police Constable: Secondary School Certificate Examination (Matric / 10th Pass) or equivalent qualification.

✔️ Searcher: Any Degree.

✔️ Assistant Sub Inspector (Wireless Operator): SSC or equivalent. Diploma in Electronics.

✔️ Photographer: Diploma in Photography OR Certificate course in Composite Course in Reproduction Photography, Plate Making and Offset Printing.

✔️ Laboratory Technician: Bachelors Degree in Science with at least 2nd class.

✔️ Stenographer: Higher Secondary School Certificate or AICTE approved Diploma. Should be computer literate.

✔️ Lower Division Clerk: Possessing Higher Secondary School Certificate or AICTE approved Diploma. Knowledge of Computer Applications / Operations with typing speed of 30 words per minute in English.

✅ Selection Process:

✔️ Written Examination
✔️ Documents Verification

✅ Application Fee:

✔️ ₹ 200/- for General category candidates.
✔️ ₹ 100/- for SC / ST / OBC / Ex-Serviceman / PWD / EWS category candidates.

✅ How to Apply: The application forms completed in all respects should e personally submitted by the candidates at the counter opened to accept the Application Forms at Police Headquarters, Panaji-Goa between 10:00 Hours to 17:00 Hours on all working days, on or before 30/04/2021. Due to COVID 19, the last date for receipt of application has been extended up to 26/05/2021.
