OSSSC CRE Recruitment 2026: Notification, Online Form, 3250 Vacancies | Last Date 31.01.2026

Published by: RozgarDwaar Editorial Desk | Updated: 2026-01-29
Apply Online (Official Website) Download Notification PDF Official Website

Odisha Sub-ordinate Staff Selection Commission (OSSSC), Bhubaneswar, has announced the Combined Recruitment Examination-2026 (CRE-2026) for recruitment to 3250 various Group 'C' posts including Revenue Inspector (RI), Assistant Revenue Inspector (ARI), ICDS Supervisor, Amin, Junior Assistant (JA), Village Agricultural Worker (VAW), and Statistical Field Surveyor (SFS). Eligible Indian citizens are invited to apply online.

The Candidates are strongly advised to carefully read the detailed OSSSC CRE Recruitment 2026 notification before applying to confirm eligibility regarding age, qualification, category, and other criteria. The last date for online registration is extended up to 15th February 2026.

OSSSC CRE 2026 Age Limit::
  • Revenue Inspector (RI) & Assistant Revenue Inspector (ARI): 20-42 Years
  • All Other Posts: 21-42 Years
  • Age calculated as on 01 January 2025
  • Age Relaxation applicable as per Government Rules
OSSSC CRE 2026 Eligibility Criteria::
  • Revenue Inspector (RI): Bachelor Degree in any discipline with Computer knowledge as prescribed by Government.
  • Assistant Revenue Inspector (ARI): Bachelor Degree in any discipline or equivalent.
  • ICDS Supervisor (Female / Male): Bachelor Degree in any discipline from a recognized University.
  • Village Agricultural Worker (VAW):  Matric + 12th Passed with Science OR +2 Vocational course in Agriculture related subjects (CP / HORT / PDFM).
  • Junior Assistant (JA): Passed +3 in Arts / Science / Commerce or equivalent with knowledge of Computer Skill.
  • Statistical Field Surveyor (SFS): Matric + 12th passed with Mathematics / Statistics / Economics OR Diploma / Degree in Statistics.
  • Amin: Matric + 12th Class Passed and completed Amin Training / Certificate Course from recognized institution.

How to Apply OSSSC CRE Recruitment 2026::

Eligible candidates must apply ONLINE only through the OSSSC official website.

➢ Step 1: Visit www.osssc.gov.in from 07 January 2026.

➢ Step 2: Complete Registration / Re-registration (07.01.2026 to 15.02.2026).

➢ Step 3: Fill the online application form and upload required documents.

➢ Step 4: Pay the application fee (if applicable) online.

➢ Step 5: Submit the application before 22 February 2026 (11:59 PM) and take printout.

Frequently Asked Questions::
What is the total number of vacancies in OSSSC CRE Recruitment 2026?:

What is the age limit for OSSSC CRE Recruitment 2026?:

20-42 Years for RI & ARI, 21-42 Years for other posts (as on 01.01.2025) with relaxation as per rules.
