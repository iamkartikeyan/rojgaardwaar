ONGC Consultant Recruitment 2026 – Apply for 37 Posts | Last Date 19-06-2026

Published by: RozgarDwaar Editorial Desk | Updated: 2026-06-18

ONGC Consultant Recruitment 2026: Apply for 37 Junior Consultant & Associate Consultant Posts. Oil and Natural Gas Corporation Limited (ONGC), Well Services, Ahmedabad Asset has officially released Advertisement No. AMD /CONSLT-2026 for engagement of experienced retired ONGC executives as Junior Consultants (E1-E3) and Associate Consultants (E4-E6+) on contractual basis for supervision of Work-over Operations on charter hired and O&M operated rigs. This is a golden opportunity for retired ONGC personnel from Production, Drilling, Mechanical, Electrical and Instrumentation disciplines to serve ONGC on contract for 2 years.

Applications are invited in offline mode. The last date to submit application is 19 June 2026. Eligible retired ONGC executives can send scanned application by email or submit in person.

ONGC Consultant 2026 Remuneration Package:

Junior Consultant (E1-E3):

  • 1st Year: ₹40,000/- per month
  • 2nd Year: ₹41,350/- per month
  • Plus Conveyance ₹6,500 + Office at Residence ₹6,500 + Communication up to ₹2,000

Associate Consultant (E4-E5 & E6+):

  • 1st Year: ₹66,000/- per month
  • 2nd Year: ₹68,000/- per month
  • Plus Conveyance ₹13,000 + Office at Residence ₹13,000 + Communication up to ₹2,000

Contract period: 2 years. GST to be borne by consultants.

ONGC Consultant 2026 Eligibility Criteria:

Age Limit (as on date of advertisement): Less than 64 years.

Educational & Experience Qualification:

  • Retired ONGC executives from Production / Drilling / Mechanical / Electrical / Instrumentation disciplines
  • Junior Consultant: Retired at E1-E3 level with minimum 5 years in-line experience in Work-over / Drilling field operations
  • Associate Consultant: Retired at E4-E5 & E6+ level with minimum 5 years in-line experience in Work-over / Drilling field operations

Candidates must submit scanned copy of ONGC Identity Card (both sides) and Aadhaar Card (both sides).

ONGC Consultant 2026 Selection Process:
  1. Written Test (50 marks – Objective Type, 60 minutes, bilingual)
  2. Interaction / Interview (20 marks)
  3. Evaluation of Experience (20 marks) + Academic Qualification (10 marks)

Qualifying marks: 30% in Written Test & 50% in Interview. Final merit based on total 100 marks.

Important Dates for ONGC Consultant Recruitment 2026:
Date of Notification 04 June 2026
Last Date for Receipt of Application 19 June 2026
Date of Written Test & Interview To be intimated to shortlisted candidates
Application Fee:

Nil – No application fee is required.

How to Apply for ONGC Consultant Recruitment 2026:
  1. Download the prescribed Application / Bio-Data format from the official notification
  2. Fill the application form completely with all required details
  3. Attach scanned copies of ONGC Identity Card (both sides), Aadhaar Card (both sides) and all supporting documents
  4. Send the scanned application by email to AMDWSPC@ONGC.CO.IN
  5. OR submit the hard copy in person at Contract Cell, Room No-132, 1st floor, Avani Bhavan, ONGC, Ahmedabad Asset
  6. Mention “Application for Consultant” clearly on the envelope / subject line
