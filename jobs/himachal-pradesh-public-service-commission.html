FAQs:

✅  PPSC Full Form?

PPSC full form is Punjab Public Service Commission. The PPSC is a Punjab State Government Agency, conducts various competitive and civil examinations.

✅  What is PPSC exam?

Punjab Public Service Commission (PPSC) is performing various roles:-

✔️ To conduct competitive and civil examinations for Govt of Punjab Services / Vacancies.
✔️ To conducting screening test / Interview of the selected candidates.
✔️ To make appointments to Bihar state civil services.
✔️ To check the suitability of the candidates when promoting and transferring them from one service to another.

✅  Who is eligible for PPSC?

Both Male and Female Punjab state residents and Indian Citizens who have passed minimum 10th class, 12th class, ITI, Diploma, Degree and Post Graduates are eligible to apply.

✅  How can I apply for PPSC?

Eligible candidates may apply online / offline for Punjab PSC official website ppsc.gov.in (OR) Get latest notification through RozgarDwaar blog.
