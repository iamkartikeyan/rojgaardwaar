How to Apply Bank of India Officer Recruitment 2026?:

Eligible candidates must apply online only through the Bank of India website from 10.09.2026 to 25.09.2026. No other mode of application will be accepted.

  • Step 1: Visit www.bankofindia.bank.in, go to CAREER and open “Recruitment of Officers in various streams upto Scale IV – Project No. 2026-27/02”.
  • Step 2: Click on APPLY ONLINE → New Registration and enter name, contact details and email ID to get a provisional registration number and password.
  • Step 3: Fill the form using Save & Next. Upload photograph, signature, left thumb impression and handwritten declaration as per specifications.
  • Step 4: Preview all details, complete registration and pay the fee online.
  • Step 5: Take a printout of the submitted application for the examination and interview. Keep the registered email ID and mobile number active till the process is over.
