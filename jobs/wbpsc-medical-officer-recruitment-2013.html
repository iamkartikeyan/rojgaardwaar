WBPSC Medical Officer Recruitment 2023: Apply Online for 300 Posts - Notification and Details

Published by: RozgarDwaar Editorial Desk | Updated: 2023-10-12
Apply Online (Official Website) Download Notification PDF Official Website

WBPSC Medical Officer Recruitment 2023 Notification of 300 General Duty Medical Officer Posts, Apply Online through wbpsc.gov.in: The West Bengal Public Service Commission (WBPSC) Kolkata invites online applications from eligible Indian Citizens for recruitment of General Duty Medical Officer (GDMO) – West Bengal ESI Medical Service in Labour Department, Government of West Bengal. The WBPSC GDMO 2023 Online application @ wbpsc.ucanapply.com registration process start from 21st September 2023 to 12th October 2023. In this page, rozgardwaar.com updates West Bengal Medical Officer 2023 Vacancies, Age Limit, Salary, Eligibility Criteria, Qualification Selection Process and How to Register details.

✅ WBPSC Medical Officer 2023 Age Limit:

✔️ Not more than 36 years for Ordinary Medical Graduates.
✔️ Not more than 40 years for Post Graduate Medical Qualification.

✅ WBPSC Medical Officer 2023 Salary: Pay Level 06 ₹ 56,100 – 1,44,300/-.

✅ WBPSC Medical Officer 2023 Eligibility Criteria:

(1) Medical Qualification included in the First and Second schedules or Part-II of the Third schedule to the Indian Medical Council Act, 1956 (102 of 1956) and registration as medical practitioner in West Bengal.
(2) 06 months’ practical training as House Staff in any Government hospital or other recognized institution.

✅ How to Apply for WBPSC Medical Officer Recruitment 2023:

➢ Eligible Interested candidates should apply online through WBPSC Online Application Portal (wbpsc.ucanapply.com).
➢ The candidates are required to fill basic details and upload scanned copy of required documents.
➢ The last date for registration of online application is 12/10/2023 up to 3:00 PM.
