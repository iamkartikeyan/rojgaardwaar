BPSSC Range Officer Recruitment 2026 – Apply Online for 16 Posts | Last Date 16-08-2026

Published by: RozgarDwaar Editorial Desk | Updated: 2026-08-15
Apply Online (Official Website) Download Notification PDF Official Website

Bihar Police Subordinate Services Commission (BPSSC) has released the official notification for the recruitment of Range Officer of Forests under Advertisement No. 10/2026. A total of 16 vacancies have been announced. The online application process has started from 16 July 2026 and the last date to apply is 16 August 2026. Eligible candidates can check complete details regarding eligibility, physical standards, selection process and application procedure below.

Total Vacancies

16 Posts

21 to 37 Years (as on 01/08/2025)

BPSSC Range Officer of Forests 2026 Eligibility Criteria::
  • Educational Qualification: Candidates must possess a Bachelor’s Degree with any one of the following subjects: Animal Husbandry & Veterinary Science, Botany, Chemistry, Geology, Mathematics, Physics, Statistics, Zoology, Agriculture, Forestry or Engineering from a recognized university.
  • Age Limit: Minimum 21 years and Maximum 37 years as on 01 August 2025.
  • Age relaxation will be applicable as per Bihar Government rules for reserved categories.

  • Written Examination

  • Medical Examination
  • Document Verification
