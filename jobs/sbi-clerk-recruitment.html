Apply Online (Official Website) Download Notification PDF Official Website

State Bank of India (SBI) has released the detailed notification for Recruitment of Junior Associates (Customer Support & Sales) in clerical cadre under Advertisement No. CRPD/CR/2026-27/17. The Online Applications are invited from eligible Indian Citizens (Fresher Graduates) for appointment as Junior Associate (Customer Support & Sales) in clerical cadre in State Bank of India. The Candidates can apply for vacancies in one State/UT only.

The SBI Clerk (Junior Associate) 2026 online registration process began on 11 August 2026, and the last date to apply is 31 August 2026. On this page, banking job aspirants can find all important details of SBI Clerk Vacancy 2026, including the official notification, Circle-wise vacancies and application link.

SBI Clerk Recruitment 2026 Eligibility Criteria::
  • Educational Qualification (as on 31.12.2026): Graduation in any discipline from a recognised University or any equivalent qualification recognised by the Central Government. Candidates with Integrated Dual Degree (IDD) must have passed on or before 31.12.2026.
  • Local Language Proficiency: Candidates must be proficient (reading, writing, speaking and understanding) in the specified opted local language of the State/UT applied for. Candidates who have studied the local language in 10th or 12th standard are exempt from LLPT.
  • Age Limit (as on 01.04.2026): Minimum 20 years and Maximum 28 years (born not earlier than 02.04.1998 and not later than 01.04.2006 – both dates inclusive).
  • Age Relaxation: OBC – 3 years, SC/ST – 5 years, PwBD (Gen/EWS) – 10 years, PwBD (OBC) – 13 years, PwBD (SC/ST) – 15 years.
SBI Clerk Salary 2026::
  • Pay Scale: ₹24,050 – 1,340/3 – 28,070 – 1,650/3 – 33,020 – 2,000/4 – 41,020 – 2,340/7 – 57,400 – 4,400/1 – 61,800 – 2,680/1 – 64,480
  • Starting Basic Pay: ₹26,730/- (₹24,050 + two advance increments for graduates).
  • Approximate in-hand salary at Metro centres is around ₹46,000 per month (including DA and other allowances). Benefits include PF, NPS, Medical facilities, LFC and other perquisites.
SBI Clerk Recruitment 2026 Selection Process::
  • Online Preliminary Examination
  • Online Mains Examination
  • Local Language Proficiency Test (LLPT) – where applicable

Final Merit: Based on marks obtained in the Mains Examination only. No interview.

How to Apply for SBI Clerk Recruitment 2026::

Eligible candidates must apply ONLINE ONLY through the official SBI website. No other mode of application will be accepted.

➢ Step 1: Visit the official website https://sbi.bank.in/web/careers/current-openings.

➢ Step 2: Click on the link “Recruitment of Junior Associates 2026” or the direct Apply Online link.

➢ Step 3: Complete the registration process by providing basic details and generate Registration Number & Password.

➢ Step 4: Fill the online application form carefully with personal, educational and other required details (apply for one State/UT only).

➢ Step 5: Upload scanned copies of Photograph, Signature, Left Thumb Impression and Handwritten Declaration as per the prescribed format.

➢ Step 6: Pay the application fee online (if applicable) through Debit Card / Credit Card / Net Banking / UPI.

➢ Step 7: Submit the application form and take a printout of the confirmation page for future reference.
