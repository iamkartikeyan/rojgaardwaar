    FAQs for Maharashtra Govt Jobs::

    What are the Government Job Exams in Maharashtra?

    ✔️ Maharashtra State Service Exam
    ✔️ Maharashtra Engineering Service
    ✔️ Maharashtra Forest Service Examination
    ✔️ Maharashtra Agricultural Service Examination etc.

    What about MPSC?

    Maharashtra Public Service Commission (MPSC) is a major body to conducting Maharashtra State Govt Jobs Recruitment / Jobs Examination in every Year. MPSC is a Constitutional Body established Under Article 315 of Constitution of India.

    What types of jobs in Maharashtra Government?

    Major Maharashtra State Govt Jobs like Deputy Collector, Tehsildar, Various Engineers, Police Posts, Various Level of Officers, Sales Tax Inspector, Forest Posts, Naib Tahsildar, Clerk, Assistant, Deputy Superintendent of Land records, Taluka Agriculture Officer, District Education Officer etc.

    How to Apply Maharashtra Govt Jobs?

    Get latest Maharashtra state Government Jobs and Govt related organization / Govt agency jobs in Maharashtra state list available in RozgarDwaar Maharashtra Govt Jobs page. In this page get free Maharashtra state jobs alert for both fresher and experienced candidates.

    Which Cities gave major Govt Jobs in Maharashtra?

    ✔️ Mumbai
    ✔️ Pune
    ✔️ Thane
    ✔️ Nagpur.

    What is the minimum qualification for Maharashtra Jobs?

    ✔️ For Group C, Group D Maharashtra State Civil Services, Assistant, Clerk, Attendant, Driver etc. - Matriculation, 10th Class, 12th Class (Higher Secondary) Pass.
    ✔️ For Managers, Officers, State Services: Graduates, Post Graduates.
    ✔️ For Technical Posts: Engineering, Diploma, ITI.
    ✔️ For Professional Posts: Law, Medical, Engineers, Agriculture etc.
