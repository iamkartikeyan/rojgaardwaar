Karnataka Forest Watcher Recruitment 2026 Notification Out for 774 Posts | Apply Online up to 31-08-2026

Published by: RozgarDwaar Editorial Desk | Updated: 2026-08-28
Apply Online (Official Website) Download Notification PDF Official Website

The Karnataka Forest Department has released the official notification for the recruitment of Forest Watcher (Group ‘D’) posts for the year 2026-27. A total of 774 vacancies have been announced across various forest circles in Karnataka.

The Karnataka Forest Department Forest Watcher Recruitment 2026-27 is a great opportunity for 10th pass candidates. Interested and eligible candidates can apply online through the official portal. The last date to submit the online application is 31st August 2026. This article provides complete information including circle-wise vacancies, eligibility, physical standards, selection process, and application details.

How to Apply for Karnataka Forest Watcher Recruitment 2026?:

Eligible candidates can apply online by following the steps given below:

  • Step 1: Visit the official website aranya.gov.in.
  • Step 2: Register using a valid mobile number and Aadhaar number.
  • Step 3: Fill out the online application form with correct details.
  • Step 4: Upload scanned photograph, signature, and required documents.
  • Step 5: Pay the application fee through online mode (UPI, Net Banking, Card, etc.).
  • Step 6: Submit the application and take a printout for future reference.

FAQs::
What is the total number of vacancies in Karnataka Forest Watcher Recruitment 2026-27?:

A total of 774 Forest Watcher posts have been announced.

What is the educational qualification required for Karnataka Forest Watcher?:
