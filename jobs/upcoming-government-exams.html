Top Government Exam Categories 2026::
  • Civil Services Exams: Exams like IAS, IPS, IFS conducted by UPSC for administrative roles.
  • Banking Exams: IBPS, SBI, RBI exams for officers, clerks, and specialists in banking sector.
  • Railway Exams: RRB NTPC, JE, Group D for technical and non-technical posts in Indian Railways.
  • SSC Exams: CGL, CHSL, MTS for various central government departments.
  • State PSC Exams: State-level civil services, teaching, and administrative posts.
  • Defence Exams: NDA, CDS for entry into armed forces.
  • Engineering Services: Exams for engineers in government departments.
  • Teaching Exams: TET, CTET for school teachers.
  • Medical Exams: AIIMS, NEET for medical professionals.
  • Law Exams: CLAT, judicial services for legal roles.
