✅ Age Limit:

Minimum 18 / 21 years, Maximum 40 years as of 01.01.2026.

✅ Eligibility Criteria:

✔️ Surveyor, Mines Supervisor: Diploma in Mining / Civil.
✔️ Jail Prahari: Matric pass + Proficiency in Hindi and knowledge of Rajasthan’s Culture.
✔️ Junior Technical Assistant: B.E. / B.Tech / Diploma in Civil.
✔️ Accounts Assistant: Degree in any discipline.
✔️ Live Stock Assistant (Pashudhan Sahayak): 12th class pass + 01 Year Training Certificate OR 02 Years Diploma in Livestock Assistance.
✔️ Paramedical Staff: Degree / Diploma.
✔️ Driver: Matric Pass + LMV / HMV Driving License + 03 Years Experience.
✔️ Class IV: Matriculation (10th Class)/  Secondary pass.
✔️ Librarian: 12th Pass + Degree / Diploma in Library Science.
✔️ Conductor: Matric with 12th class pass.
✔️ NHM: Degree / Diploma. 

✅ Selection Process:

✔️ Written Examination
✔️ Trade Test (If Required)
✔️ Documents Verification
✔️ Medical Examination

✅ How to Apply:

➢ Eligible Candidates should fill Online Application Form through RSMSSB Rajasthan Online Recruitment Portal (sso.rajasthan.gov.in).
➢ The candidates first done One Time Registration (OTR) - Create Account with a valid Email Address and Mobile Number.
➢ The candidates should fill basic / personal / educational details and upload relevant documents.
➢ Apply Online on or before closing date.
