PSSSB Advt No 01/2026: Apply Online for 197 Excise and Taxation Inspector Posts

Published by: RozgarDwaar Editorial Desk | Updated: 2026-02-28
Apply Online (Official Website) Download Notification PDF Official Website

Punjab Subordinate Services Selection Board (PSSSB), Forest Complex, Sector-68, S.A.S. Nagar (Mohali) invites online applications from eligible candidates for filling up 197 vacancies of Excise and Taxation Inspector in the Excise and Taxation Department, Punjab under Advertisement No. 01/2026. (Short notification released; detailed advertisement to be uploaded on 12 February 2026.)

✅ PSSSB Excise Inspector Recruitment 2026 Eligibility Criteria:
Post Name Age Limit Qualification & Experience

Excise and Taxation Inspector

18-37 Years (as on 01.01.2026)
Relaxation as per Punjab Govt Rules

Bachelor’s Degree in any discipline from a recognized University / Institution.
Possess at least one hundred and twenty hours course with hands-on experience in the use of Personal Computer or Information Technology in Office Productivity applications or Desktop Publishing applications from a Govt. recognized institution or a reputed institution, which is ISO 9001 certified.
Matriculation examination with Punjabi as one of the compulsory or elective subjects or equivalent.
No prior experience required.

How to Apply PSSSB Excise Inspector Recruitment 2026:

Eligible candidates must apply ONLINE only through the PSSSB official website. Detailed steps will be available in the full notification from 12 February 2026.

➢ Step 1: Visit sssb.punjab.gov.in from 13 February 2026.

➢ Step 2: Register using valid email/mobile and create login credentials.

➢ Step 3: Fill the online application form with personal, educational, category, and other details.

➢ Step 4: Upload scanned copies of photograph, signature, and required certificates.

➢ Step 5: Pay the application fee online as per category and submit the form.

➢ Step 6: Take printout of the application and fee receipt for future reference.
