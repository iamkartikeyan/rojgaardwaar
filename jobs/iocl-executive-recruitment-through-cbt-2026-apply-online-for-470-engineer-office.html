IOCL Executive Recruitment 2026 Eligibility Criteria::
  • Graduate Engineer (Grade A): Full-time B.Tech/BE/Equivalent in relevant discipline (Chemical, Civil, CS/IT, Electrical, Electronics & Communication, Instrumentation, Mechanical) from AICTE/UGC recognized institute. Min. marks: Gen/EWS/OBC(NCL) ≥65%, SC/ST/PwBD ≥55%. Experience: Nil.
  • Officer (Marketing) (Grade A): 2-year full-time MBA/PG Diploma (equivalent to MBA) with Marketing specialization. Min. marks: Gen/EWS/OBC(NCL) ≥60%, SC/ST/PwBD ≥55%. Experience: Nil.
  • Officer (Law) (Grade A): Graduate Degree + LLB (or 5-year Integrated LLB) recognized by Bar Council of India. Min. marks: 60% (Gen/EWS/OBC) / 55% (SC/ST/PwBD) in both. Experience: Minimum 02 years post-qualification professional experience as on 30.06.2026.
  • Assistant Quality Control Officer (Grade A0): Master’s Degree in Chemistry (or relevant variants). Min. marks: Gen/EWS/OBC(NCL) ≥60%, SC/ST/PwBD ≥55%. Experience: Minimum 02 years post-qualification in relevant Testing/R&D/QC labs.
  • Diploma Engineer (Grade E0): 3-year full-time Diploma in Engineering in relevant discipline (Electrical, Mechanical, Safety + Industrial Safety Diploma, etc.). Min. marks: Gen/EWS/OBC(NCL) ≥65%, SC/ST/PwBD ≥55%. Experience: Nil. PwBD not eligible for Safety post.
  • Age Limit (as on 01.07.2026): Graduate/Diploma Engineer – Max 26 years; Officer (Marketing) – Max 28 years; Law Officer / Asst. QC Officer – Max 30 years (General/EWS). Age relaxation as per Govt. norms for reserved categories, PwBD, Ex-Servicemen etc.
IOCL Executive Recruitment 2026 Selection Process::
  • Computer Based Test (CBT)
  • Group Discussion (GD) and Group Task (GT)
  • Personal Interview (PI)

Final Merit: CBT 85% + GD/GT 5% + PI 10%. Minimum qualifying marks applicable in each stage as per category.

How to Apply for IOCL Executive Recruitment 2026::

Eligible candidates must apply ONLINE ONLY through the official IOCL portal. No other mode of application will be accepted.

➢ Step 1: Visit the official website https://iocl.com/latest-job-opening or the direct Apply Online link.

➢ Step 2: Click on “Click here for New Registration” and enter Name, Contact details and Email-id to generate Provisional Registration Number & Password.

➢ Step 3: Login and fill the online application form carefully with personal, educational and other required details (one application per post).

➢ Step 4: Upload scanned copies of Photograph (captured in April 2026 or later), Signature and Left Thumb Impression as per prescribed format.

➢ Step 5: Pay the application fee online (if applicable) through Debit Card / Credit Card / Net Banking / UPI.

➢ Step 6: Submit the application form and take a printout of the confirmation page for future reference.
