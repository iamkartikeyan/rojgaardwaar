GAIL ET Recruitment GATE 2027 Notification Out

Published by: RozgarDwaar Editorial Desk | Updated: 2026-09-01
Apply Online (Official Website) Download Notification PDF Official Website

GAIL (India) Limited, a Maharatna PSU under the Government of India, has issued an indicative notification for recruitment of Executive Trainees (ET) through GATE-2027 under Advertisement No. GAIL/OPEN/ET/1/2026. Eligible Fresher Engineering Graduates, who willing to appear GATE 2027 exam  Chemical, Instrumentation, Electrical and Mechanical disciplines are eligible to apply for GAIL ET 2027.

The GAIL ET Recruitment through GATE-2027 will use only GATE-2027 scores. Candidates must first register for GATE-2027 and later apply separately on the GAIL website when the detailed advertisement is released. This article covers expected vacancies, eligibility, pay scale, GATE paper mapping, important dates and official links.

Exact discipline-wise vacancy numbers will be published in the detailed GAIL advertisement. Around 70+ posts are expected on the lines of the previous cycle. GATE scores of years other than GATE-2027 will not be valid for this recruitment.

GAIL ET Recruitment 2027 Eligibility Criteria::
  • Maximum 26 years. Age relaxation as per GoI rules.
  • B.E./B.Tech in Instrumentation / relevant branch with minimum 65% marks + valid GATE-2027 (IN / EE / CH / ME) score.
  • Age cut-off date, list of equivalent branches, category-wise relaxation and final-year eligibility will be confirmed in the detailed GAIL notification. Candidates can apply for one discipline only.
GAIL ET GATE-2027 Selection Process::

GAIL will shortlist candidates on the basis of GATE-2027 marks in the relevant paper. Further stages will be as notified in the detailed advertisement:

  • GATE-2027 Score Shortlisting
  • Group Discussion and/or Personal Interview
  • Document Verification
  • Pre-employment Medical Examination
GAIL ET Recruitment 2027 Application Fee::
  • GAIL online application: Nil, as per the indicative notification.
  • GATE-2027 examination fee: As prescribed by the GATE Organizing Institute (IIT Madras) on gate2027.iitm.ac.in.

How to Apply GAIL ET Recruitment through GATE-2027?:

This is a two-step process. First appear in GATE-2027, then apply on the GAIL portal when the detailed advertisement opens.

  • Step 1: Register for GATE-2027 at gate2027.iitm.ac.in in the relevant paper (CH / IN / EE / ME) on or before 27 September 2026 (without late fee).
  • Step 2: Appear for GATE-2027 in February 2027 and note the GATE registration number.
  • Step 3: Watch the Careers section of www.gailonline.com for the detailed Executive Trainee advertisement (expected around February–March 2027).
  • Step 4: Submit the GAIL online form with GATE-2027 registration number and required documents.
  • Step 5: Keep a printout of both the GATE confirmation and the GAIL application for GD/Interview and document verification.
FAQs::
What is the total number of vacancies in GAIL ET Recruitment through GATE-2027?:

Around 70+ posts are expected. Exact numbers will be given in the detailed GAIL advertisement.

Which GATE year is valid for GAIL Executive Trainee 2027?:

Only GATE-2027 scores will be valid. Earlier GATE scores will not be accepted.

What is the pay scale for GAIL Executive Trainee?:

E-2 Grade: ₹60,000 – ₹1,80,000.

What is the selection process for GAIL ET through GATE-2027?:

Shortlisting on GATE-2027 marks, followed by Group Discussion and/or Interview, document verification and medical examination.

Is there any application fee for GAIL ET Recruitment 2027?:

The GAIL application is indicated as free of cost. GATE-2027 exam fee is payable to the GATE Organizing Institute.

What are the important dates for GAIL ET through GATE-2027?:

GATE-2027 registration: 27 August 2026 to 27 September 2026 (without late fee). Exam: February 2027. GAIL online form: tentatively February–March 2027.
