TNUSRB Recruitment 2025: Apply Online for 3665 Constable, Fireman, Jail Warder Posts | Last Date 21st September

Published by: RozgarDwaar Editorial Desk | Updated: 2025-09-20
Apply Online (Official Website) Download Notification PDF Official Website

The Tamil Nadu Uniformed Services Recruitment Board (TNUSRB), responsible for filling positions in the Tamil Nadu Police Department through competitive exams and physical assessments, has now issued a notification for Grade II roles.

After no recruitments in 2024, this drive offers opportunities for 10th standard passers to join as Grade II Police Constables (Tamil Nadu Special Police), Grade II Jail Warders, and Firemen. Online Applications can be submitted online through tnusrb.cr.2025.ucanapply.com from 22nd August 2025 to 21st September 2025.

TNUSRB Recruitment 2025 Notification PDF Download:

The Tamil Nadu Uniformed Services Recruitment Board Grade II Constable Recruitment 2025 Official Notification (Notification No. 02/2025) is accessible from 21st August 2025. Applicants possessing a 10th/SSLC qualification are urged to obtain the full notification from the official site and apply accordingly.

How to Apply for TNUSRB Recruitment 2025::

Suitable candidates are required to submit their applications online via the TNUSRB portal (tnusrb.cr.2025.ucanapply.com) beginning 21st August 2025. The final date for submission will be updated shortly.

➢ The candidates should enter all required details and upload scanned copy of Proof, Qualification and other documents.
➢ The last date for registration of online applications is 21st September 2025 up to 23:59 Hours.

What is TNUSRB?

TNUSRB stands for Tamil Nadu Uniformed Services Recruitment Board (தமிழ்நாடு சீருடை பணியாளர் தேர்வாணையம்). It organizes exams for selecting Police Constables, Sub-Inspectors, Jail Warders, Firemen, and other roles in Tamil Nadu's police sector.
