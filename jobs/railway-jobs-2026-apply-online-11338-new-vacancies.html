Railway Exams 2026-27 List::
  • RRB NTPC (Non-Technical Popular Categories): For various non-technical posts.
  • RRB JE (Junior Engineer): For the recruitment of junior engineers in the Indian Railways.
  • RRB Group D: For various posts in the Group D category, including track maintainers and helpers.
  • RRB SSE (Senior Section Engineer): For the recruitment of senior engineers.
  • RRB ALP (Assistant Loco Pilot): For the recruitment of assistant loco pilots.
  • RRB Paramedical: For various paramedical posts in Indian Railways.
  • RPF Constable: For recruitment in the Railway Protection Force.
  • RPF Sub-Inspector: For higher-level positions in the Railway Protection Force (RPF) and Railway Protection Special Force (RPSF).
  • RRB Ministerial and Isolated Categories: For various ministerial posts and isolated categories.
Railway Job Vacancy 2026-27 List::
  • General Recruitment: Open to all candidates based on merit, involving various exams conducted by Railway Recruitment Boards (RRBs).
  • Scheduled Castes (SC) and Scheduled Tribes (ST) Quota: Reserved seats for SC/ST candidates, providing benefits like age relaxation and a lower cut-off mark.
  • Other Backward Classes (OBC) Quota: Reserved positions for OBC candidates, with similar benefits as SC/ST.
  • Ex-Servicemen Quota: Positions reserved for individuals who have served in the armed forces, including age relaxations.
  • Sports Quota: Recruitment based on outstanding achievements in sports, requiring candidates to meet specific criteria.
  • Meritorious Sports Persons: Special recruitment opportunities for athletes with exceptional records at national or international levels.
  • Persons with Disabilities (PwD) Quota: Reservations for candidates with physical disabilities, along with additional advantages.
  • Divyang Quota: Specific provisions for different types of disabilities during the recruitment process.
  • Nursing Quota: Reserved for candidates with nursing qualifications, especially in paramedical roles.
  • Culture Quota: For individuals with notable contributions in cultural fields, allowing them to apply for specific positions.
  • Group 'A' Posts: These posts are filled through examinations conducted by UPSC, including the Civil Services Exam, Engineering Services Exam, and Combined Medical Services Examination.
  • Group 'B' Posts: These posts are not open for direct recruitment. Group 'B' positions are linked to Section Officers' Grade, which are upgraded roles for Group 'C' railway employees on a deputation basis.
  • Group 'C' Posts: This category includes both technical and non-technical positions, such as Clerk, Station Master, Ticket Collector, Commercial Apprentice, Traffic Apprentice, and various engineering roles (Civil, Mechanical, Electrical, Signal & Telecommunication), among others.
  • Group 'D' Posts: The Positions in this group cover various disciplines, including Trackman, Helper, Assistant Points Man, Safaiwala/Safaiwali, Gunman, and Peon.
  • Other Posts: This includes ITI Apprentices, Sports Quota, Cultural Quota, Scouts and Guides Quota, and more.
Frequently Asked Questions: :

What are the jobs in the Railway Sector?

Indian Railway jobs include Gazetted (Group 'A' and 'B'), Non-Gazetted (Group 'C' and 'D'), NTPC, Junior Engineer, RPF, RPSF, Act Apprentices, Level 1, and Level 2 posts.

How to apply for railway recruitment through rozgardwaar.com?

The rozgardwaar.com blog frequently updates the latest Indian Railway jobs. Eligible candidates should first check qualification-based railway job listings, then click on a specific Railway Name for detailed notifications and application links.

What are the qualifications for railway jobs?

Minimum qualifications include passing the 10th class or ITI for Group C & D posts, and holding a Graduate or Post Graduate degree for Group A & B posts.

How many railway zones are there in India?

There are 21 Railway Zones in India: Ahmedabad, Ajmer, Allahabad, Bangalore, Bhopal, Bhubaneshwar, Bilaspur, Chandigarh, Chennai, Gorakhpur, Guwahati, Jammu, Kolkata, Malda, Mumbai, Muzaffarpur, Patna, Ranchi, Secunderabad, Siliguri, and Trivandrum.

How many types of railway jobs are there?

Group 'A' Posts: Appointed through UPSC exams. Group 'B' Posts: Upgraded posts from Group 'C' employees. Group 'C' Posts: Technical and Non-Technical cadre posts. Group 'D' Posts: Various discipline posts including Trackman, Helper, etc. Other Posts: ITI Apprentices, Sports Quota, etc.

Which is the best job in the Railway?

The best job depends on individual qualifications and preferences. For 10th or 12th pass candidates, popular posts include Clerk, Station Master, and Ticket Collector, while engineers may prefer Group A Engineering Services.

How many jobs are there in the Indian Railways?

Each year, more than one lakh vacancies open up in the Railway sector.

Is a career in the railway possible after 12th?

Yes, individuals who have passed the 10th or 12th standard are eligible to apply for Group C and Group D railway posts.

Who is eligible to apply for railway jobs?

Indian citizens who have passed at least Matriculation/10th standard are eligible to apply. Strong technical skills are also preferred.

Why does rozgardwaar.com have a Railway Jobs list page?

The page provides weekly updates for job seekers looking for railway jobs and offers free alerts for railway job opportunities.
