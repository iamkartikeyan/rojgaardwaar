Nalanda University Non Teaching Recruitment 2026 - Apply Online for Assistant, Librarian & Engineer Posts | Last Date 31-08-2026

Published by: RozgarDwaar Editorial Desk | Updated: 2026-08-30
Apply Online (Official Website) Download Notification PDF Official Website

Nalanda University, Rajgir (an Institution of National Importance under the Ministry of External Affairs, Government of India) has released the detailed notification for recruitment to various Non-Teaching Positions on contract basis. A total of 09 vacancies are available for Librarian, Deputy Librarian, Assistant Registrar, Assistant Engineer (Electrical), Senior Assistant, Library Assistant and Assistant posts, offering excellent opportunities for eligible candidates.

The Nalanda University Non Teaching Recruitment 2026 online application process scheduled to be end on 31 August 2026. On this page, job aspirants can find all important details of Nalanda University Vacancy 2026, including the official notification and application link.

Nalanda University Non Teaching Recruitment 2026 Eligibility Criteria::
  • 1. Librarian: Master’s Degree in Library Science/Information Science/Documentation Science with at least 55% marks + 10 years experience (at least 3 years as Deputy/Additional Librarian) + Evidence of innovative library services with ICT + Ph.D. in Library Science/Documentation/Archives & Manuscript Keeping.
  • 2. Deputy Librarian: Master’s Degree in Library Science/Information Science/Documentation Science with at least 55% marks + 8 years experience as Assistant University/College Librarian + Evidence of innovative library services with ICT + Ph.D. in relevant field.
  • 3. Assistant Registrar: Master’s Degree with at least 55% marks + Minimum 5 years professional/administrative experience in University/Educational/Research Institution (administration, admission, examination, establishment, procurement, etc.) + Exceptional English speaking skills. Desirable: Management/Law qualification, GFR knowledge, basic computer skills.
  • 4. Assistant Engineer (Electrical): Bachelor’s Degree in Electrical Engineering + At least 2 years post-qualification experience as Junior Engineer or similar + Exceptional English speaking skills. Desirable: Experience in electric installations, meters, pumps, ACs, etc., knowledge of Contract Law, CPWD/PWD procedures.
  • 5. Senior Assistant: Bachelor’s Degree from a recognised University/Institution + At least 5 years experience in Administration/Establishment/Academic/Finance & Accounts/General Supervision + Exceptional English speaking skills + Proficiency in Computer Operation, noting & drafting.
  • 6. Library Assistant: Master’s Degree in Library & Information Science with 2 years experience OR Bachelor’s Degree in Library/Library & Information Science with 3 years experience in relevant field + Knowledge of Computer Applications.
  • 7. Assistant: Bachelor’s Degree from a recognised University/Institution + At least 3 years experience in Administration/Establishment/Academic/Finance & Accounts/General Supervision + Exceptional English speaking skills + Proficiency in Computer Operation, noting & drafting.
Nalanda University Non Teaching Recruitment 2026 Selection Process::
  • Written Test and/or Interview
  • Document Verification

Final Selection: Based on performance in Written Test/Interview and fulfilment of eligibility criteria.

How to Apply for Nalanda University Non Teaching Recruitment 2026::

Eligible candidates must apply ONLINE ONLY through the Google Form link. No other mode of application will be accepted.

➢ Step 1: Login to your Gmail account (create one if you do not have).

➢ Step 2: Click on the Apply Online link given above (Google Form).

➢ Step 3: Fill the online application form carefully with personal, educational and experience details for the desired post(s).

➢ Step 4: Pay the application fee of ₹500/- per position online to the University bank account and note the UTR/Transaction Reference Number.

➢ Step 5: Enter the UTR Number in the application form and upload required documents.

➢ Step 6: Submit the form and take a printout/screenshot of the confirmation for future reference.

Note: Applications received after 31 August 2026 will be rejected. Candidates can apply for multiple posts by paying separate fees.
