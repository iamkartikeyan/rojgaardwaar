RSSB Female Supervisor Recruitment 2026 Apply Online for 72 Posts | Last Date 05.02.2026

Published by: RozgarDwaar Editorial Desk | Updated: 2026-02-04
Apply Online (Official Website) Download Notification PDF Official Website

Rajasthan Staff Selection Board (RSSB / RSMSSB), Jaipur, has released the notification for direct recruitment of 72 Female Supervisor posts under Advt No. 02/2026. Eligible female candidates who have qualified Rajasthan CET (Graduation Level) 2024 are invited to apply online from 07 January 2026 to 05 February 2026.

Candidates are strongly advised to thoroughly read the detailed RSSB Female Supervisor Recruitment 2026 notification before submitting the application to verify eligibility in terms of age, qualification, CET score, and other conditions.

How to Apply RSSB Female Supervisor Recruitment 2026::

Eligible candidates must apply ONLINE only through the Rajasthan SSO Portal.

➢ Step 1: Visit the SSO Portal and login/register using your credentials.

➢ Step 2: Go to the Recruitment Portal and select the Female Supervisor application link from 07 January 2026.

➢ Step 3: Fill the online form with accurate personal, educational, and CET details.

➢ Step 4: Upload required documents (photo, signature, certificates, etc.).

➢ Step 5: Pay the application fee online and submit the form before 05 February 2026 (11:59 PM).

➢ Step 6: Take a printout of the application for future reference. Corrections can be made upto 07 February 2026 with charges.
