KRFB Recruitment 2022 Apply Online 117 Engineer, Accountant Vacancies

Published by: RozgarDwaar Editorial Desk | Updated: 2022-07-15
Apply Online (Official Website) Download Notification PDF Official Website

KRFB Recruitment 2022 Notification of Accountant, Site Supervisor and Project Engineer Vacancies: Kerala Road Fund Board (KRFB) Government of Kerala invites online applications from  qualified and experienced candidates for appointment of Accountant, Senior Accountant, Site Supervisor and Project Engineer on 01 year contract basis in KRFB and Project Monitoring Units under KRFB. Eligible candidates apply online through CMD Kerala Online Portal. The last date for registration of online applications is 15th July 2022.

Kerala Road Fund Board Recruitment 2022 (Notification No. KRFB / C / A&TP / 1 / 2022):

✅ KRFB Recruitment Age Limit:

✔️ Accountant: 36 years
✔️ Senior Accountant: 36 years
✔️ Site Supervisor: 40 years
✔️ Project Engineer: 40 years
✔️ Age relaxation as per PSC norms applicable.

✅ KRFB Recruitment Salary / Pay Scale:

✔️ Accountant: ₹ 21,175/- per month
✔️ Senior Accountant: ₹ 30,000/- per month
✔️ Site Supervisor: ₹ 25,000/- per month
✔️ Project Engineer: ₹ 42,000/- per month

✅ KRFB Recruitment Eligibility Criteria:

✔️ Accountant:

(1) B.Com with Tally from a Recognized University.
(2) Minimum 3 years experience in Accounting in a Reputed Institution.

✔️ Senior Accountant:

(1) First Class Degree in Commerce from a Recognised University.
(2) Minimum 05 years Professional Experience.
(3) Thorough knowledge of basis accounting procedures.
(4) In-depth understanding of generally accepted accounting principles.
(5) Experience with general ledger functions and the month-end/ year-end close process.
(6) Hands on experience with accounting software packages.

✔️ Site Supervisor:

(1) Diploma in Civil Engineering.
(2) Not less than 2 years of Post Qualification Experience in Govt./ Public/ Private sector infrastructure industry/ Transportation Projects like Major Road/ Bridge Projects.

✔️ Project Engineer:

(1) B.Tech in Civil Engineering from a Recognized Institution.
(2) Not less than 2 years of Post Qualification Experience in
Govt./ Public/ Private sector infrastructure industry/ Transportation Projects like Major Road/ Bridge Projects.

✅ KRFB Recruitment Application Fee: ₹ 500/- (through online payment only) for each category of post.

✅ KRFB Recruitment Selection Process: Written Test / Interview

✅ How to Apply KRFB Recruitment?

➢ Eligible Interested candidates may apply online mode only through Centre for Management Development (CMD),
Thiruvananthapuram (www.cmdkerala.net).
The Candidates will have to upload their passport size photograph (taken within six months), signature and copies of certificates proving qualification and experience while submitting the application.
➢ The last date for submitting online application is 15/07/2022 up to 5:00 PM.

What is KRFB?

Kerala Road Fund Board (KRFB), A Statutory Body of the Government of Kerala. The KRFB is established in 2001 by the Government of Kerala, is a funding agency for providing financial assistance to the transport facility projects in the state, The KRFB plays a pivotal role in the overall infrastructure development of Kerala.
