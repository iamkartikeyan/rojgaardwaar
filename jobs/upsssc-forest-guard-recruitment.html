UPSSSC Forest Guard Recruitment 2026 - Apply Online for 708 Posts | Last Date 20-07-2026

Published by: RozgarDwaar Editorial Desk | Updated: 2026-07-19
Apply Online (Official Website) Download Notification PDF Official Website

Uttar Pradesh Subordinate Services Selection Commission (UPSSSC) has published recruitment notification UPSSSC Forest Guard & Wildlife Guard Vacancy 2026 for fill up 708 Vacancies. Candidates having Intermediate (10+2) qualification and valid UPSSSC PET 2025 scorecard are eligible to apply for Van Rakshak (Forest Guard) & Vanya Jeev Rakshak (Wildlife Guard) posts. The UPSSSC online application form has started on 30 June 2026, and eligible candidates can apply until 20 July 2026.

Eligible Candidates must check the complete details regarding Post wise Vacancies, eligibility, application fee, selection process and the application process for the UPSSSC Forest Guard & Wildlife Guard Posts Recruitment 2026 provided below. (Advt. No. 12-Pariksha/2026)

UPSSSC Forest Guard & Wildlife Guard Vacancy 2026 Eligibility Criteria::
  • Age Limit (as on 01.07.2026): 18 – 40 years (Relaxation: OBC 43 years, SC/ST 45 years, Govt Employees 45 years, Ex-Servicemen as per rules)
  • Nationality: Must be a citizen of India
  • PET-2025 Qualification: Must have appeared in UPSSSC PET-2025 and possess valid Score Card
  • Qualification & Experience: Intermediate (10+2) from UP Board or equivalent recognised board.
    Preferential Qualification (any one): Territorial Army (2 years), NCC ‘B’ Certificate, Computer Application Certificate, or State-level Sportsperson.
UPSSSC Forest Guard & Wildlife Guard Vacancy 2026 Selection Process::

The selection on the basis of following steps:-

  • Shortlisting for Main Exam on the basis of PET-2025 Normalised Score (up to 15 times vacancies)
  • Written Examination (100 Marks – Objective Type, 120 Minutes)
  • Physical Standard Test (PST) – Qualifying
  • Physical Efficiency Test (PET) – Qualifying (Male: 25 km with 10 kg in 4 hrs | Female: 14 km in 4 hrs)
  • Medical Examination – Qualifying
  • Document Verification
UPSSSC Forest Guard & Wildlife Guard Vacancy 2026 Application Fee::
  • Application Fee: ₹00 (Nil for all categories)
  • Online Process Fee: ₹25/- (All Categories)
  • Total Payable: ₹25/-
  • Payment Mode: Online through Credit Card, Debit Card, Internet Banking, UPI or SBI e-Challan

How to Apply UPSSSC Forest Guard & Wildlife Guard Recruitment 2026::

Eligible & Interested candidates must apply online only through the official UPSSSC website.

➢ Step 1: Visit the official website https://upsssc.gov.in and click on the live advertisement for Forest Guard & Wildlife Guard 2026.

➢ Step 2: Login using OTP sent to registered mobile/email linked with your PET-2025 Registration Number.

➢ Step 3: Your PET-2025 details will auto-populate; fill educational qualification, post preference and preferential qualification details.

➢ Step 4: Pay ₹25/- online process fee through Credit/Debit Card, Internet Banking, UPI or SBI e-Challan.

➢ Step 5: Submit the form and take a printout of the final application form for future reference.

Note: Candidates are advised to read the Official Notification carefully before filling the application form.
