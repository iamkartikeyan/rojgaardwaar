Join Indian Navy 10+2 B.Tech Recruitment 2026 – Apply Online for 44 Executive & Technical Vacancies

Published by: RozgarDwaar Editorial Desk | Updated: 2026-01-05
Apply Online (Official Website) Download Notification PDF Official Website

Indian Navy has invited online applications from unmarried male and female candidates for the 10+2 (B.Tech) Cadet Entry Scheme (Permanent Commission) course commencing July 2026 at Indian Naval Academy, Ezhimala, Kerala. A total of 44 vacancies are available in Executive & Technical Branches (maximum 07 vacancies for women).

Candidates are strongly advised to read the detailed official notification carefully before applying to confirm eligibility criteria regarding age, qualification, JEE (Main) rank, and other requirements.

Indian Navy 10+2 B.Tech Cadet Entry July 2026:
rozgardwaar.com :
🏢 Organization Indian Navy
💼 Course / Entry 10+2 (B.Tech) Cadet Entry Scheme (Permanent Commission)
📊 Total Vacancies 44 Posts (Executive & Technical Branch)
🎯 Age Limit Born between 02 Jan 2007 & 01 Jul 2009 (both dates inclusive)
💰 Stipend / Salary ₹56,100/- per month (during training)
On commissioning: Level-10 ₹56,100 - ₹1,77,500 + MSP ₹15,500
🎓 Minimum Qualification 10+2 with 70% in PCM & 50% in English + JEE (Main) 2025 appeared
💳 Application Fee Nil
📝 Selection Process JEE (Main) 2025 CRL → SSB Interview → Medical Examination
📍 Training Location Indian Naval Academy, Ezhimala, Kerala
⏰ Last Date 19 January 2026
Indian Navy 10+2 B.Tech Entry 2026 Vacancies & Gender::
  • Total Vacancies: 44 Posts (Executive & Technical Branch)
  • Gender: Unmarried Men and Women (maximum 07 vacancies for women)
Indian Navy 10+2 B.Tech Entry 2026 Age Limit::
  • Born between 02 January 2007 and 01 July 2009 (both dates inclusive).
  • No relaxation in age limit is applicable.
Indian Navy 10+2 B.Tech Entry 2026 Eligibility Criteria::
  • Passed Senior Secondary Examination (10+2 Pattern) or equivalent from any recognised Board with at least 70% aggregate marks in Physics, Chemistry & Mathematics (PCM) and at least 50% marks in English (either in Class X or Class XII).
  • Candidates must have appeared for JEE (Main) – 2025 (for B.E./B.Tech).
  • Unmarried Indian citizens.
Indian Navy 10+2 B.Tech Entry 2026 Selection Process::

Shortlisting based on JEE (Main) All India Common Rank List (CRL) - 2025

SSB Interview (intimated via Email/SMS)

Medical Examination

Final Merit List based on SSB marks (subject to medical fitness, police & character verification)

How to Apply Indian Navy 10+2 B.Tech Entry 2026::

Eligible candidates should apply ONLINE only through the official Indian Navy recruitment website. No other mode of application will be accepted.

➢ Step 1: Visit joinindiannavy.gov.in from 03 January 2026.

➢ Step 2: Click on the 10+2 (B.Tech) Cadet Entry Scheme application link, register (if new), and fill the online form with accurate details.

➢ Step 3: Upload required documents – Date of Birth proof, 10th marksheet, 12th marksheet, JEE (Main) 2025 scorecard.

➢ Step 4: Submit the application before 19 January 2026 (11:59 PM).

➢ Step 5: Take a printout of the submitted application for future reference.
