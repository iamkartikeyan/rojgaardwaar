  • Post Graduate / Master's Degree (for higher posts).
  • Bachelor's Degree / B.Ed / Relevant Qualifications.
  • Diploma / ITI / 10th / 12th Pass (for technical / lower posts).

  • Age, Qualification, and Experience vary by post – refer detailed notification.

How to Apply Kerala PSC Recruitment 2026::

Eligible candidates must apply ONLINE only through the Kerala PSC Thulasi portal. No other mode will be accepted.

➢ Step 1: Complete One Time Registration (OTR) on www.keralapsc.gov.in if not already registered.

➢ Step 2: Log in to your profile using User ID and Password.

➢ Step 3: Click on the 'Apply Now' button against the desired Category Number in the Notification Link.

➢ Step 4: Fill the application, upload photo & signature, and submit.

➢ Step 5: Take a printout for future reference.

Frequently Asked Questions::
How to Apply Kerala PSC Jobs?:

Eligible candidates should register online through Kerala PSC One Time Registration portal - thulasi.psc.kerala.gov.in.

What is the selection process for Kerala PSC Jobs?:

Kerala PSC Recruitment selection will be made through Online Test (Written Test - OMR) and / or Interview.

Who can apply for the vacancy positions announced in the Kerala PSC Notifications?:

Online applications are invited from residents of Kerala for various service posts in the Kerala Government. Both male and female job aspirants are eligible to apply for the available positions.
