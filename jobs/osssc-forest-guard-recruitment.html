OSSSC Forest Guard Recruitment 2026 Apply Online for 1518 Vacancies | Last Date 31.01.2026

Published by: RozgarDwaar Editorial Desk | Updated: 2026-01-03
Apply Online (Official Website) Download Notification PDF Official Website

Odisha Sub-Ordinate Staff Selection Commission (OSSSC), Bhubaneswar, has announced the Combined Recruitment Examination-2025 (II) for recruitment to the Group ‘C’ posts of Forester, Forest Guard and Excise Constable. A total of 1518 vacancies are to be filled. Eligible candidates are invited to apply online.

The Candidates are strongly advised to carefully read the detailed OSSSC Combined Recruitment Examination-2025 (II) notification before applying to confirm eligibility regarding age, qualification, category, and other criteria.

How to Apply OSSSC Recruitment 2026::

Eligible candidates should apply ONLINE only through the OSSSC official portal. No other mode will be accepted.

➢ Step 1: Visit osssc.gov.in from 07 January 2026.

➢ Step 2: Complete One-Time Registration (if not already registered).

➢ Step 3: Log in and fill the online application form accurately.

➢ Step 4: Upload required documents (photo, signature, certificates).

➢ Step 5: Pay the application fee (if applicable) through online mode.

➢ Step 6: Submit the application by 07 February 2026 and take a printout.
