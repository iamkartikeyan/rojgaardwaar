OSSSC Teacher Recruitment 2025: Apply Online, 2829 Vacancies, Notification | Last Date 22 January

Published by: RozgarDwaar Editorial Desk | Updated: 2025-01-21
Apply Online (Official Website) Download Notification PDF Official Website

The Odisha Subordinate Staff Selection Commission (OSSSC) invites online applications from eligible candidates for filling up of 2829 District Cadre posts of Teachers in TGT (Arts), TGT (Science - PCM), TGT (Science - CBZ), Sanskrit Teacher, Physical Education Teacher (PET), Tribal Language Teacher, Hindi Teacher and Sevak / Sevika in Government Schools under SC & ST Development, M & BCW Department. The OSSSC Teacher Recruitment 2025 Online Registration will be available in the web portal of the Commission (www.osssc.gov.in). The last date for registration of online applications is 22nd January 2025.

✅ OSSSC Teacher Eligibility Criteria::

Minimum Educational Qualifications:

    ✔️ Sevak / Sevika: Matric with 12th class Pass with Arts / Science / Commerce or its equivalent examination with C.T. / D.I.E.T training and Odisha Teacher Eligibility Test (OTET) Pass (either Paper I or Paper II or both).

    ✔️ Tribal Language Teacher: 12th Class Pass in Arts / Science / Commerce or its equivalent examination with C.T. / D.I.E.T training and Odisha Teacher Eligibility Test (OTET) Pass (Paper-I).

    ✔️ Trained Graduate Teacher (TGT): Bachelor's Degree in relevant discipline _ Bachelor in Education (B.Ed.) / 03 years Integrated B.Ed. - M.Ed. / 02 year B.Ed. (Special Education).

    ✔️ Sanskrit Teacher: Bachelor's Degree with Sanskrit as one of the electives / Optional / Pass / Honours subject + B.Ed. (OR) Shastri (Sanskrit) and Shiksha Shastri (Sanskrit) + B.Ed./

    ✔️ Physical Education Teacher (PET): +2 Passed or its equivalent examination + C.P.Ed. / D.P.Ed. / B.P.Ed. / M.P.Ed. The candidate must have passed Odisha Secondary School Teacher Eligibility Test (OSSTET).

    ✔️ Hindi Teacher: Bachelor's Degree with Hindi as one of the elective / optional / pass / honours subject. Hindi Training Qualification - Hindi Shikshan Parangat or B.H.Ed. or B.Ed. in Hindi or B.Ed.

      Other Eligibility:

        A Candidate must be able to speak, read and write Odia and have passed the Middle School Examination with Odia as a language subject or Passed Matric or equivalent exam with Odia as the medium of examination in non language subjects or passed in Odia as language subject in the final exam of Class VII or equivalent exam or Passed a test in Odia in Middle English School Standard.
          ✅ OSSSC Teacher Selection Process::

          The Odisha OSSSC Teachers Job Vacancies selection on the following steps:-

          Stage I

          Preliminary Examination (100 Marks) (Paper I, Paper II) (MCQ Type in OMR / CBRT Mode)

          Stage II

          Main Examination (100 Marks) (MCQ Type in OMR / CBRT Mode)

          Stage III

          Documents Verification

          How to Apply OSSSC Teacher Online Application Form 2025::

          ➢ Eligible and interested candidates are required to apply online through the Odisha OSSSC Online Application Portal (osssc.gov.in).

          ➢ The Candidates must provide all the required details and upload scanned copies of a passport-size photograph, signature, left thumb impression, and a hand-written declaration.

          It is essential to have a valid personal email ID and mobile number, which should remain active throughout the entire recruitment process.

          ➢ The closing date for online application registration is 22/01/2025 (Wednesday).

          ➢ Help Desk: For queries contact Technical Assistance contact Phone No 0674-2597149 (OR) For Administrative Assistance contact Phone No 0674-2597106 (Between 10 AM to 5 PM on official days).
