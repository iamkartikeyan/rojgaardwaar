IIM Shillong Fellowship Recruitment 2025 - Apply Online for 100 Posts

Published by: RozgarDwaar Editorial Desk | Updated: 2025-07-05
Apply Online (Official Website) Download Notification PDF Official Website

The Dr. APJ Abdul Kalam Centre for Policy Research & Analysis at IIM Shillong invites applications for the recruitment of 100 posts (35 Fellows and 65 Assistant Fellows) under the Mukhya Mantri Viksit Arunachal Fellowship Programme (MMVAFP) for the Government of Arunachal Pradesh. Eligible candidates must apply online latest by 6th July 2025 to work in the Finance, Planning & Investment Department and contribute to policy formulation and implementation.

IIM Shillong Fellowship Recruitment 2025:

Post Names

Fellows, Assistant Fellows

Total Vacancies

100

Age Limit

Maximum 35 years (as on 06/07/2025)

Remuneration

₹ 60,000/- to ₹ 70,000/- per month

Educational Qualification

Master’s Degree with 60% marks

Selection Process

Written Exam, Interview

Closing Date

06/07/2025

Job Location

Arunachal Pradesh

This article provides detailed information about the IIM Shillong MMVAFP Recruitment 2025, including post names, vacancy details, eligibility criteria, remuneration, selection process, important dates, and the application process.

  • IIM Shillong MMVAFP Notification PDF
IIM Shillong MMVAFP Vacancy List 2025::
Post Name No of Posts
Fellows 35
Assistant Fellows 65
IIM Shillong MMVAFP Remuneration::

✔️ Fellows: ₹ 70,000/- per month (fixed honorarium).
✔️ Assistant Fellows: ₹ 60,000/- per month (fixed honorarium).

IIM Shillong MMVAFP Eligibility Criteria::

✔️ Age Limit (as on 06/07/2025):

✔️ Maximum 35 years for both Fellows and Assistant Fellows.
Relaxation as per Government of Arunachal Pradesh (GoAP) norms, except for educational qualifications.

✔️ Educational Qualifications:

✔️ Essential: Master’s Degree in any discipline with at least 60% marks in both Undergraduate and Postgraduate levels from Institutes of National Importance or reputed universities.
✔️ Desirable: Candidates from premier institutes like IITs/IIMs/ISB/TISS with domain experience, project management skills, experience in government schemes, ICT proficiency, and expertise in MS Office (Word, Excel, PowerPoint) or MS Projects.

✔️ Experience:

✔️ Fellows: Minimum 3 years of work experience in relevant fields.
✔️ Assistant Fellows: Minimum 2 years of work experience in relevant fields.

IIM Shillong MMVAFP Selection Process::

✔️ Written Examination
✔️ Personal Interview
✔️ Selection based on merit, academic credentials, and performance.

How to Apply for IIM Shillong MMVAFP Recruitment 2025::

➢ Eligible candidates must apply online through the link provided on the IIM Shillong website by 06/07/2025 (17:00 hrs).
➢ Specify area of interest and willingness to undertake assigned tasks during the contract period.
➢ Upload required documents: proof of qualifications, experience certificates, age proof, and other relevant credentials.
➢ Candidates must have a valid email ID and mobile number, kept active until results are declared, for all communications.
➢ Shortlisting may be based on qualifications, academic performance, or relevant experience, as decided by the screening committee.
➢ Retain a copy of the application for reference.
