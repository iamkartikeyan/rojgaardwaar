DSSSB MTS Recruitment 2026 Apply Online for 714 Vacancies | Last Date 15.01.2026

Published by: RozgarDwaar Editorial Desk | Updated: 2026-01-13
Apply Online (Official Website) Download Notification PDF Official Website

Delhi Subordinate Services Selection Board (DSSSB) has released the most awaited notification for recruitment of 714 Multi Tasking Staff (MTS) posts under various Departments / Autonomous Bodies / Local Bodies of Government of NCT of Delhi. Eligible candidates can submit their online application on the official website dsssbonline.nic.in from 17th December 2025 to 15th January 2026.

Candidates are strongly advised to read the complete DSSSB MTS Recruitment Notification 07/2025 carefully before applying to confirm eligibility regarding age, qualification, category relaxation and other conditions.

DSSSB MTS 2026 Age Limit::
  • Minimum 18 years and Maximum 27 years (as on 15-01-2026)
  • Age relaxation applicable as per Delhi Government rules (SC/ST – 5 years, OBC – 3 years, PwD – 10 years, etc.)
DSSSB MTS 2026 Eligibility Criteria::
  • Matriculation (10th Class) Pass or equivalent from a recognized Board.
  • Candidates possessing higher qualification (12th / Degree) are also eligible.
DSSSB MTS 2026 Selection Process::

One Tier Examination (Objective Type – MCQs)

Skill Test / Endurance Test / Driving Test / Trade Test (wherever applicable)

Document Verification

DSSSB MTS 2026 Minimum Qualifying Marks::

The minimum qualifying marks for different categories:-

  • General/ EWS: 40%
  • OBC (Delhi): 35%
  • SC/ ST/ PH (PwD): 30%
  • Ex-servicemen will be given 5% relaxation in their respective categories subject to a minimum of 30%.

How to Apply DSSSB MTS Recruitment 2026::

➢ Step 1: Visit the official DSSSB online portal dsssbonline.nic.in from 17-12-2025.
➢ Step 2: Click on “New Registration” (if new user) or login with existing credentials.
➢ Step 3: Fill the complete application form, upload photograph, signature & required documents.
➢ Step 4: Pay the application fee (if applicable) through SBI e-Pay.
➢ Step 5: Submit the application before 15th January 2026 (11:59 PM) and take a printout for future reference.
