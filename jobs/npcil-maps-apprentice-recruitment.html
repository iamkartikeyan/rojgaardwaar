NPCIL MAPS Apprentice Recruitment 2026 – Apply Online for 158 Posts | Last Date 30-06-2026

Published by: RozgarDwaar Editorial Desk | Updated: 2026-06-29
Apply Online (Official Website) Download Notification PDF Official Website

NPCIL MAPS Apprentice Recruitment 2026: Apply for 158 ITI, Diploma and Graduate Posts. Nuclear Power Corporation of India Limited (NPCIL), Madras Atomic Power Station (MAPS), Kalpakkam has officially released the Trade, Diploma & Graduate Apprentice Recruitment 2026 notification (Advt. No. NPCIL/MAPS/HRM/APPRENTICESHIP/2026/01) to fill 158 apprenticeship vacancies (Trade: 124, Diploma: 18, Graduate: 16) at Madras Atomic Power Station, Kalpakkam, Tamil Nadu. This is a golden opportunity for ITI, Diploma and Graduate candidates to undergo one-year apprenticeship training in a prestigious Maharatna PSU under the Department of Atomic Energy.

Eligible candidates can apply offline only. The last date for receipt of application by post is 30 June 2026. Candidates must first register on the respective apprenticeship portals (NAPS 2.0 for Trade & NATS 2.0 for Diploma/Graduate) before sending the application form to NPCIL MAPS.

NPCIL MAPS Apprentice 2026 Stipend & Benefits:

Monthly stipend is paid as per Apprentices Act 1961:

  • Trade Apprentice: ₹9,600 (after 1-year ITI) / ₹10,560 (after 2-year ITI)
  • Diploma Apprentice: ₹10,900
  • Graduate Apprentice (Non-Engineering): ₹12,300

Duration of training: Maximum 01 Year. Selected apprentices will also receive other benefits as per NPCIL rules.

NPCIL MAPS Apprentice 2026 Eligibility Criteria:

Age Limit (as on 30.06.2026):

  • Trade Apprentice: 18-24 years
  • Diploma Apprentice: 18-25 years
  • Graduate Apprentice: 21-28 years
  • Age Relaxation: 5 years for SC/ST, 3 years for OBC (NCL), 10 years for PwBD

Educational Qualification:

  • Trade Apprentice: ITI Pass Certificate in relevant trade (Full-Time Regular only)
  • Diploma Apprentice: Diploma in relevant Engineering discipline (Full-Time Regular, passed within 5 years)
  • Graduate Apprentice (Non-Engineering): Graduate Degree (BA/B.Sc./B.Com. etc.) (Full-Time Regular, passed within 5 years)

Important: Candidates who have already undergone or are undergoing apprenticeship anywhere are NOT eligible. Aadhaar + Bank linkage and Police Verification Certificate are mandatory.

NPCIL MAPS Apprentice 2026 Selection Process:
  1. Merit List prepared on the basis of marks obtained in ITI / Diploma / Graduation
  2. Document Verification
  3. Medical Examination (as per NPCIL standards)
  4. Police Verification (mandatory for prohibited area)

No written test. Final engagement is subject to verification of all original documents and medical fitness.

Application Fee:

Nil – No application fee is required for NPCIL MAPS Apprentice Recruitment 2026.

How to Apply for NPCIL MAPS Apprentice Recruitment 2026:
  1. Register on the mandatory portal: NAPS 2.0 (https://www.apprenticeshipindia.gov.in) for Trade OR NATS 2.0 (https://www.nats.education.gov.in) for Diploma/Graduate
  2. Download Application Form (Annexure-1) from www.npcil.nic.in → HR Management → Opportunities
  3. Fill the application form in Block Letters, affix recent passport-size photo and sign
  4. Attach self-attested copies of all required documents (including Police Verification Certificate)
  5. Send the complete application by Post to reach on or before 30 June 2026 at:
    Deputy Manager (HRM), Nuclear Power Corporation of India Limited, Madras Atomic Power Station, Kalpakkam – 603 102, Chengalpattu, Tamil Nadu
