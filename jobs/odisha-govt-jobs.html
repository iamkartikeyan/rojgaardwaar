Latest and Upcoming Odisha Government Jobs 2026 free job alert for both Male and Female Odisha state residents and unemployed Indian citizens. rozgardwaar.com website is one of the leading Govt Jobs Portal in India. In this page, the blog regularly updates Govt Jobs in Odisha for 12th Pass, 10th Pass, Diploma, ITI and Graduates. The Odisha state formerly named as Orissa, it is the 8th largest state in India, the state capital is Bhubaneswar.

The Odisha Government's official job agency is the Odisha Public Service Commission (OPSC) - www.opsc.gov.in. The OPSC is authorized to conduct various job examinations and interviews for the selection of candidates for various vacancy positions in the government departments and services of Odisha state.
