UKSSSC Inter Level Recruitment 2026 – Apply Online for 553 Various Posts | Last Date 07-10-2026

Published by: RozgarDwaar Editorial Desk | Updated: 2026-09-01
Apply Online (Official Website) Download Notification PDF Official Website

Uttarakhand Subordinate Service Selection Commission (UKSSSC) has released the detailed notification for Various Inter Level (Group-C) Posts Recruitment 2026 under Advertisement No. 80/UKSSSC/2026. A total of 553 permanent vacancies have been announced for Junior Assistant, Computer Assistant, Registration Clerk, Survey Lekhpal and other Inter Level posts across various departments of Uttarakhand.

The UKSSSC Inter Level 2026 online application process will start on 08 September 2026, and the last date to apply is 07 October 2026 (11:59 PM). On this page, candidates can find all important details of UKSSSC Inter Level Vacancy 2026, including official notification, eligibility, exam pattern, syllabus and application process.

UKSSSC Inter Level Recruitment 2026 Eligibility Criteria::
  • Educational Qualification: Intermediate (Matric with 12th) passed from Uttarakhand Board of School Education or any equivalent examination recognized by the Government.
  • Typing / Computer Skill (as applicable): Minimum Hindi typing speed of 4000 KDPH on computer for most clerical posts. Some posts also require English typing (25 WPM Hindi / 30 WPM English) and knowledge of MS Office / ‘O’ Level computer course.
  • Domicile: Candidates should fulfil Uttarakhand domicile / permanent resident / employment registration / education from Uttarakhand institutions conditions as prescribed in the notification.
  • Age Limit (as on 01.07.2026): Generally 18 to 42 years. For some posts (Tourism, Information, Legal Academy, Store Keeper etc.), minimum age is 21 years.
  • Age Relaxation: SC/ST/OBC (Uttarakhand) – 5 years, PwD (Divyang) – 10 years, Freedom Fighter Dependents – 5 years, Ex-Servicemen – as per rules.
UKSSSC Inter Level Salary 2026::
  • Level-02: ₹19,900 – ₹63,200 (Survey Lekhpal)
  • Level-03: ₹21,700 – ₹69,100 (Junior Assistant, Registration Clerk and most posts)
  • Level-04: ₹25,500 – ₹81,100 (Computer Assistant / Senior Assistant)
  • Level-05: ₹29,200 – ₹92,300 (Telephone Operator)

In addition to basic pay, selected candidates will get DA, HRA and other allowances as per Uttarakhand Government rules.

UKSSSC Inter Level Recruitment 2026 Selection Process::
  • Written Examination (OMR Based)
  • Skill Test / Typing Test (if applicable – qualifying nature)
  • Document Verification
  • Medical Examination

Syllabus Highlights: Hindi Grammar, Vocabulary, Reasoning, History, Geography, Polity, Economics, Current Affairs, Computer Fundamentals, and Uttarakhand History, Geography, Culture, Economy and State Current Affairs. Post-wise detailed syllabus is available on sssc.uk.gov.in.

  • General / OBC: ₹300/-
  • SC / ST / EWS: ₹150/-
  • PH (Divyang): ₹150/-
  • Orphan Candidate: Nil
  • Payment Mode: Online only

How to Apply for UKSSSC Inter Level Recruitment 2026::

Eligible candidates must apply ONLINE ONLY through the official UKSSSC website. No other mode of application will be accepted.

➢ Step 1: Visit the official website https://sssc.uk.gov.in/.

➢ Step 2: Go to Candidate Corner / Recruitment & Notification and click on the Inter Level Recruitment 2026 apply link (active from 08 September 2026).

➢ Step 3: Complete One Time Registration (OTR) and generate login credentials.

➢ Step 4: Fill the online application form carefully with personal, educational and post preference details.

➢ Step 5: Upload photograph and signature in the prescribed format.

➢ Step 6: Pay the application fee online and submit the form.

➢ Step 7: Take a printout of the application form for future reference. Correction window will be available from 12 to 14 October 2026.
