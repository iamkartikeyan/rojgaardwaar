High Court of Kerala Recruitment 2013 Personal Assistant

Published by: RozgarDwaar Editorial Desk | Updated: 2025-05-17
Apply Online (Official Website) Download Notification PDF Official Website

The High Court of Kerala has published employment notification for Kerala State Higher Judicial Service Examination 2025 for appointment of District and Sessions Judge for fill up 06 posts by direct recruitment basis. The closing date for filling online applications is 19th May 2025.

High Court of Kerala Recruitment 2025:
Name of the Exam Number of Posts

Kerala State Higher Judicial Service Exam 2025

06
Age Limit: (As on 01/01/2025):

Minimum Age: 35 years
Maximum 45 years

Pay Scale::

₹ 144840 – 194660/-

Eligibility Criteria::

  • Must be a citizen of India.

  • Must be between 35 and 45 years of age as of January 1, 2025.

  • Must be of good character.

  • Must be of sound health, active habits, and free from any physical defects or infirmities making them unfit for appointment.

  • Must not have more than one living spouse unless exempted by the Government.

  • Must be a practicing Advocate with at least seven years of standing as of January 1, 2025.

  • Must continue in practice at the time of appointment (as per Supreme Court judgment dated 19.02.2020 in Dheeraj Mor v. High Court of Delhi).

  • Must not be serving in the Union or any State government.

Selection Process::

The selection on the basis of following steps:-

✔️ Kerala State Higher Judicial Service (Preliminary) Examination.
✔️ Kerala State Higher Judicial Service (Main) Examination.
✔️ Viva Voce.

Application Fee::

✔️ ₹ 1500/- for General / OBC category candidates.
✔️ No fee for SC/ ST/ Unemployed PwBD candidates.
✔️ The fee can be made through online or offline payment mode.

How to Apply: :

➢ Eligible Candidates should apply online through Kerala HC Recruitment Online Application Portal (hckrecruitment.keralacourts.in).
➢ The candidates should fill basic / personal / educational details and upload scanned copy of Photograph, Signature and relevant documents.
➢ The final step to pay examination fee and submit your online application form.
➢ Take print out for completed application form for future reference.
