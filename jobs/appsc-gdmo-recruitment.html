APPSC GDMO Recruitment 2024: 55 Vacancies, Notification, Online Form

Published by: RozgarDwaar Editorial Desk | Updated: 2024-08-21
Apply Online (Official Website) Download Notification PDF Official Website

The Arunachal Pradesh Public Service Commission invites online applications from Indian citizens to fill 55 posts of General Duty Medical Officer (GDMO) in the Department of Health and Family Welfare, Government of Arunachal Pradesh. The candidates must apply online through Arunachal Prasdesh PSC Online Application Portal appsc.gov.in on or before 29th August 2024.

APPSC GDMO Recruitment 2024 – Job Overview:

Job Title

General Duty Medical Officer (GDMO)

Total Vacancies

55

Eligibility

MBBS, BDS, BHMS

Selection Process

Exam, Interview

Closing Date

29/08/2024

Recruitment Body

Arunachal Pradesh Public Service Commission (APPSC)

✅ APPSC GDMO Vacancy 2024 Discipline Wise:

Name of the Post

No of Vacancies

GDMO (Allopathy)

40

GDMO (Dental Surgeon)

09

GDMO (Homeopathy)

06

✅ APPSC GDMO Salary:

The GDMO Vacancy Pay Scale is Level-10 Rs. 56,100 – 1,77,500/- as admissible under rule.

✅ APPSC GDMO Eligibility Criteria:

Age Limit: Not exceeding 35 years  as on 29/08/2024. Age relaxation as per Arunachal Pradesh Government rules.

Educational Qualifications:

(1) A Recognized Medical qualification in Allopathy / Dentistry / Homeopathy.
(2) Completion of Compulsory rotating Internship.

✅ APPSC GDMO  Selection Process:

Written Examination (Objective Type)

Viva Voce

✅ APPSC GDMO Application Fee:

For APST Candidates

Rs. 150/-

For Other Candidates

Rs. 200/-

For PwD Candidates

Nil

✅ How to Apply APPSC GDMO Recruitment 204:

➢ Eligible Candidates must mandatorily Register for Arunachal Pradesh PSC One Time Registration (OTR). Those who har,e already registered in OTR need not register again and they can directly login to fill APPSC GDMO Vacancy 2024 Online Form using the same user ID and password.

➢ The candidates must upload following documents during the online registration:-

  • Signature: On white paper with black ink (40-50 KB in JPG format).
  • Recent Passport Size Photo: Color, white background, front-facing, without headgear or sunglasses (40-100 KB in JPG format).
  • Self-Attested Photocopies: Degree/Postgraduate/Post Graduate Diploma certificates and mark sheets issued by the relevant university authority.
  • Marksheet/Grade: If degree certificate is unavailable, marksheets from recognized autonomous colleges are acceptable.
  • Self-Attested Photocopy of Matriculation Certificate: For proof of age.
  • Self-Attested Photocopies: APST certificate and PRC/Aadhar Card (for General candidates).
  • Self-Attested Photocopy: Medical Council/Dental Council/Homeopathy Council Registration Certificate (if applicable).
  • Disability Certificate: For PwBD candidates, issued by the certifying authority as per the Rights of Persons with Disabilities Act, 2016.

    • ➢ The last date for registration of online applications is 29/08/2024 up to 5:00 PM.
