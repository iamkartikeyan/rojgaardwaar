Goa PSC Recruitment 2024: Apply Online, 25 Various Posts

Published by: RozgarDwaar Editorial Desk | Updated: 2025-09-18
Apply Online (Official Website) Download Notification PDF Official Website
Goa Public Service Commission (GPSC) Recruitment 2025 Notification Out. The GPSC has released Advertisement No. 09 Year 2025, inviting online applications for 38 Junior Scale Officer positions in the Goa Civil Service. Eligible candidates can apply through the official portal from now until September 19, 2025. Review the eligibility, age limit, pay scale, selection process, and application details below.
GPSC Recruitment 2025 – 38 Junior Scale Officer Posts:
rozgardwaar.com:

Job Title

No of Posts

Junior Scale Officer (Goa Civil Service)

38

Age Limit::
  • Maximum 40 years as on 19/09/2025.
  • Age relaxation applicable for reserved categories as per government norms.
Pay Scale::
  • As per revised pay matrix Level 10.
Eligibility Criteria::
  • Degree from a recognized University established by an Act of Parliament, State Legislature, or deemed University under the UGC Act, 1956, or an approved foreign University, or equivalent qualification recognized by State/Central Government.
  • Knowledge of Konkani.
  • Desirable: Knowledge of Marathi.
Selection Process::
  • Screening Test (Computer Based Recruitment Test - CBRT)
  • Competitive Test
  • Oral Interview

How to Apply::

Eligible candidates must apply online via the Goa PSC application portal (cbes.goa.gov.in) by September 19, 2025.

Registration Steps:

  • Click the "Register" button on the portal.
  • Enter a username (lowercase letters and digits only).
  • Provide email ID and mobile number.
  • Create a password and click "Sign Up."
  • Enter OTP received on email and mobile.
  • Click "Verify" to complete registration.
