BSSC Office Attendant Recruitment 2025 Apply Online 3727 Posts, Salary up to ₹ 56900 | Last Date 21 November

Published by: RozgarDwaar Editorial Desk | Updated: 2025-11-20
Apply Online (Official Website) Download Notification PDF Official Website

Bihar Staff Selection Commission (BSSC) has released employment notification inviting online applications from 10th pass candidates for the recruitment of 3727 Office Attendant posts across various offices and departments in Bihar. The online application process for the BSSC Office Attendant Vacancy 2025 will be open from 25th August 2025 to 21st November 2025.

BSSC Office Attendant Age Limit::
  • 18–37 years as on 01/08/2025 for Unreserved Male.
  • Upper Age Relaxation: 40 years for Backward Class, Extremely Backward Class (Male/Female), and Unreserved Female; 42 years for SC/ST (Male/Female); 10 years additional relaxation for Disabled candidates of all categories.
BSSC Office Attendant Salary::
  • Pay Scale: ₹ 18,000–56,900/- per month (Pay Level 1, Grade Pay ₹ 1800 as per 7th Pay Commission).
  • In-hand Salary: Approximately ₹ 23,220/- per month (including DA and HRA, after NPS and PF deductions).
BSSC Office Attendant Educational Qualifications::
  • Candidates must have passed Matriculation (10th Class) or equivalent from a recognized board.
BSSC Office Attendant Application Fee::
  • General/BC/EBC (Male): ₹ 540/-
  • SC/ST (Bihar Residents), All Female (Bihar Residents), All Divyang (SC/ST): ₹ 135/-
  • All Candidates from Outside Bihar: ₹ 540/-
  • Payment Method: Online, non-refundable.
BSSC Office Attendant Selection Process::
  • Stage 1: Written Exam – A multiple-choice exam with 100 questions (400 marks) on General Hindi, General Mathematics, and General Knowledge. Duration: 2 hours. Negative marking: 1 mark per wrong answer.
  • Stage 2: Document Verification – Qualified candidates from the written exam will undergo verification of original documents/certificates.

How to Apply for BSSC Office Attendant Recruitment 2025::

Eligible candidates must apply online through the BSSC portal from 25th August 2025 to 16th October 2025. Follow these steps:

  1. Visit the official BSSC Office Attendant Online Application Portal (onlinebssc.com/officeattendantspecial/).
  2. Navigate to the “Notices” section.
  3. Click on the “BSSC Office Attendant 2025” notice.
  4. Select “Apply Online” to access the registration page.
  5. Fill in details (name, father’s name, etc.) as per the 10th marksheet.
  6. Cross-check all information and click “Save and Next.”
  7. Upload required documents in the specified format.
  8. Complete the payment through the BSSC payment portal.
  9. Submit the application and download it for records.
  10. Take a printout of the application form for future reference.

Documents to Upload: Scanned copies of Photograph, Signature, and relevant certificates (e.g., 10th marksheet).
