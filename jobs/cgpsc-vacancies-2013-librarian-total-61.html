CGPSC Vacancies 2013 Librarian (Total 61 Posts)

Published by: RozgarDwaar Editorial Desk | Updated: 2013-06-04
Apply Online (Official Website) Download Notification PDF Official Website

Chhattisgarh Public Service Commission (CGPSC) invites online application from Interested candidates for the post of Librarian in the department of Higher Education, Chhattisgarh.

Post Name

No of Vacancies

Age Limit

Scale of Pay

Librarian

61 (UR-20, OBC-07, SC-06, ST-16, PH-12)

21 to 30 Years

Rs.15600-39100/- + GP Rs.6000/-

Educational Qualification: Candidates must possess Library Science / Information Science / Documentation Science / Postgraduate Degree with at least 55% from recognized university etc.

Application Fee: Rs.400/- for General and OBC / CL and all category candidates from other states (Rs.300/- for SC/ST/OBC-NCL Candidates belonging to Chhattisgarh.

How To Apply: Eligible candidates are required to apply online on or before 27th June 2013.

Apply online: https://www.mponline.gov.in/Portal/Examinations/CGPSC/Forms/LIB/FormEntry.aspx

Detailed Advertisement: http://psc.cg.gov.in/pdf/Advertisement/adv_Librarian2013.PDF
