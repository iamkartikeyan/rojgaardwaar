How to Use GATE 2026 Score for PSU Jobs & Government Recruitment in India:

GATE 2026 was successfully conducted by IIT Guwahati on 7, 8, 14 & 15 February 2026. Results will be announced on 19 March 2026. Scorecards will be available from 27 March 2026 on the official portal.

Steps to apply for GATE 2026 PSU Jobs:

  1. Visit gate2026.iitg.ac.in and download your official GATE 2026 scorecard.
  2. Monitor notifications on individual PSU websites and apply online using your GATE registration number and score.
  3. Upload required documents (photo, signature, educational certificates).
  4. No separate application fee for most GATE-based shortlisting.
  5. Shortlisted candidates are called directly for interview.

GATE 2026 score is valid for 3 years for higher studies and many PSU recruitments.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)::
1. What is GATE 2026?:

GATE (Graduate Aptitude Test in Engineering) tests understanding of engineering, technology, and science subjects. It is used for M.Tech/PhD admissions and PSU recruitment through GATE 2026 in India.

2. Who is eligible for GATE 2026 score based govt jobs?:

Fresh B.E./B.Tech and M.Sc graduates (or final-year students) with valid GATE 2026 scores are eligible for PSU jobs through GATE 2026.

3. What are the benefits of GATE 2026 for job opportunities in India?:

GATE 2026 scores help secure high-paying GATE score based government jobs like Executive Trainee and Management Trainee in top PSUs without extra exams.

4. Which companies recruit through GATE 2026 scores?:

Major PSUs such as NTPC, ONGC, BHEL, IOCL, GAIL, SAIL, NIC, POWERGRID, and many more hire for GATE 2026 PSU Jobs.

5. When can I apply for PSU recruitment with GATE 2026 scores?:

Applications open after GATE 2026 results (19 March 2026). Each PSU releases separate notifications.

6. What roles can I expect in PSUs through GATE 2026?:

Popular roles include Executive Trainee, Engineer Trainee, Management Trainee, Graduate Trainee, and Technical Officer.

7. What were the GATE 2026 exam dates?:

The exam was held on 7, 8, 14 & 15 February 2026.

8. Where can I check GATE 2026 results and apply for jobs?:

Results on gate2026.iitg.ac.in. Apply directly on PSU career portals using your GATE 2026 score.
