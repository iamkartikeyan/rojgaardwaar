DMRC Executive Finance Recruitment 2026: Notification, Application Form | Last Date 10-08-2026

Published by: RozgarDwaar Editorial Desk | Updated: 2026-08-06
Apply Online (Official Website) Download Notification PDF Official Website

Delhi Metro Rail Corporation (DMRC) has invited applications through Broadcast Engineering Consultants India Limited (BECIL) for engagement of Executive/Finance purely on contractual basis for a period of one year (extendable based on performance).

DMRC Executive Finance Recruitment 2026 notification has been released for 01 post. Eligible candidates can submit applications through Speed Post / Registered Post till 30 July 2026. This article provides complete details about DMRC Executive Finance 2026 including eligibility, salary, selection process and application procedure.

DMRC Executive Finance Recruitment 2026:
rozgardwaar.com:

Post Name

Executive / Finance

Total Vacancies

01 Post

Age Limit

18 – 32 years

Salary

₹54,880/- per month (Consolidated)

Qualification

CA / CMA (Fresher can also apply)

Application Fee

₹295 (Others) | Nil (SC/ST/PwBD)

Selection Process

Shortlisting + Interview / Assessment

Job Location

Delhi

Last Date

10 August 2026 (up to 18:00 Hrs)

DMRC Executive Finance 2026 Eligibility Criteria::

Criteria

Details

Educational Qualification

CA / CMA from ICAI / ICMAI (Fresher can also apply)

Age Limit

Minimum 18 years & Maximum 32 years (as on the date of publication of advertisement). Age relaxation as per Government of India guidelines.

Duration of Engagement

Initially for one year (can be curtailed or extended based on satisfactory performance)

Key Responsibilities: Preparation of Financial Statements, Direct and Indirect Taxation.

DMRC Executive Finance 2026 Salary & Benefits::
  • Consolidated monthly remuneration: ₹54,880/- (inclusive of all benefits such as Accommodation, Transport, Medical, LTA etc.)
  • EPF contribution as per rules (employer contribution also applicable)
  • Gratuity as per Gratuity Act
  • Medical health insurance policy to be taken by the employee himself
  • Leaves as per statutory compliance
  • Notice period on resignation: 60 days
  • Surety Bond of ₹1,00,000/- + Training cost (if any) – candidate has to serve minimum one year
DMRC Executive Finance 2026 Selection Process::
  • Scrutiny and shortlisting of applications based on eligibility criteria
  • Shortlisted candidates will be called for Interview / Assessment / Skill Test by DMRC
  • Final selection by the Client / Principal Employer (DMRC)

Preference may be given to candidates with higher qualifications or additional relevant experience.

How to Apply DMRC Executive Finance Recruitment 2026?:

Applications will be accepted only through Speed Post or Registered Post. No other mode will be entertained.

  • Step 1: Download the prescribed application form from the notification PDF.
  • Step 2: Fill the application form carefully and attach self-attested copies of all educational and supporting documents.
  • Step 3: Prepare a Demand Draft of ₹295/- (for other categories) in favour of “Broadcast Engineering Consultants India Limited” payable at Noida. SC/ST/PwBD candidates are exempted from fee.
  • Step 4: Put the application form, documents and Demand Draft in a sealed envelope.
  • Step 5: Send it through Speed Post / Registered Post to the following address so as to reach on or before 30 July 2026 (18:00 Hrs):

Broadcast Engineering Consultants India Limited (BECIL)
BECIL Bhawan, C-56/A-17, Sector-62
Noida – 201307, Uttar Pradesh
