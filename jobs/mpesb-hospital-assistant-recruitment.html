MPESB Hospital Assistant Recruitment 2026 – Apply Online for 1200 Posts | Last Date 23 May 2026

Published by: RozgarDwaar Editorial Desk | Updated: 2026-05-21
Apply Online (Official Website) Download Notification PDF Official Website

Madhya Pradesh Employees Selection Board (MPESB) has published recruitment notification MPESB Hospital Assistant Vacancy 2026 for fill up 1200 Vacancies. 10th Pass candidates are eligible to apply for Hospital Assistant posts on permanent basis. The MPESB online application has started on 07 May 2026, and eligible candidates can apply until 21 May 2026 (11:59 PM).

Eligible Candidates must check the complete details regarding Post wise Vacancies, eligibility, application fee, selection process and the application process for the MPESB Hospital Assistant Posts Recruitment 2026 provided below.

MPESB Hospital Assistant Vacancy 2026 Eligibility Criteria::
  • Age Limit (as on 01.01.2026): 18 – 40 years (Age relaxation extra as per MP Government Rules)
  • Qualification: 10th Class (Matriculation) Passed from a recognized Board.
MPESB Hospital Assistant Vacancy 2026 Selection Process::

The selection on the basis of following steps:-

  • Written Examination
  • Medical Examination
  • Document Verification

  • SC / ST / OBC / EWS / PH (Divyang): ₹250/-

  • Portal Charges: Extra (as applicable)

How to Apply MPESB Hospital Assistant Recruitment 2026::

Eligible & Interested candidates must apply online only through the official MPESB portal.

➢ Step 1: Visit the official website https://esb.mp.gov.in and click on the recruitment link.

➢ Step 2: Register / Login using valid mobile number and email ID.

➢ Step 3: Fill the online application form carefully with all personal, educational and category details.

➢ Step 4: Upload required documents and pay the application fee + portal charges online.
