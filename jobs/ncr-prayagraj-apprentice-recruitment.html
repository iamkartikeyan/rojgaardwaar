NCR Prayagraj Apprentice Recruitment 2026 – Apply Online 3205 Posts | Last Date 31-08-2026

Published by: RozgarDwaar Editorial Desk | Updated: 2026-08-20
Apply Online (Official Website) Download Notification PDF Official Website

Railway Recruitment Cell (RRC), North Central Railway (NCR), Prayagraj has released the Act Apprentice Recruitment Notification 2026-27 for engagement of 1,853 Apprentices under the Apprentices Act, 1961. Eligible ITI passed candidates can apply online for apprenticeship training in various divisions and workshops of North Central Railway.

On 14th August 2026, The NCR RRC Prayagraj has released Addendum notification for Inclusion of Engineering Trade Slots of (JHS, PRYJ, AGC) units. Total Vacancies 1853 increased to 3205 Apprentice trade posts. The last date for online registration has been also extended up to 31st August 2026. This article provides complete details about vacancies, eligibility criteria, age limit, application fee, selection process, important dates and online application link.

Educational Qualification::
  • Passed 10th Class (Matriculation/SSC) or equivalent under the 10+2 system with at least 50% aggregate marks from a recognized Board.
  • Possess an ITI / National Trade Certificate (NTC) in the relevant trade issued by NCVT/SCVT recognized by the Government of India.
  • Valid NCVT/SCVT affiliated ITI Certificate in the relevant trade is mandatory.
  • Domicile shall be as per RRC/NCR notification.
Age Limit (As on 07.07.2026)::
  • Minimum Age: 15 Years
  • Maximum Age: 24 Years
  • SC/ST: 05 Years relaxation.
  • OBC: 03 Years relaxation.
  • PwBD: 10 Years relaxation.
  • Ex-Servicemen: As per Government rules.
Selection Process::
  • Merit List based on average percentage of 10th & ITI Marks.
  • Document Verification.
  • Medical Examination.
  • No Written Examination and No Interview.
Application Fee::
Category Fee
General / OBC / EWS ₹100/-
SC / ST / PwBD / Female Nil

The application fee should be paid online through the payment gateway during the online application process.

How to Apply for RRC NCR Apprentice Recruitment 2026?:
  • Visit the official RRC/NCR Apprentice application portal (examerp.com).
  • Read the notification carefully before applying.
  • Register using a valid email ID and mobile number.
  • Fill in the online application form with correct personal and educational details.
  • Upload the required documents, photograph and signature.
  • Pay the application fee online, if applicable.
  • Submit the application on or before 31st August 2026 up to 11:59 PM IST.
  • Take a printout of the submitted application for future reference. 

FAQs::
How many vacancies are available in RRC NCR Apprentice Recruitment 2026?:

A total of 3205 Act Apprentice vacancies are available.

What is the last date to apply online?:

The last date to submit the online application is 07 August 2026.

What is the educational qualification required?:

Candidates should have passed 10th with at least 50% marks and possess an NCVT/SCVT ITI Certificate in the relevant trade.

What is the application fee?:

₹100 for General/OBC/EWS candidates. SC/ST/PwBD and Female candidates are exempted.

How will candidates be selected?:

Selection will be based on a merit list prepared from the average percentage of 10th and ITI marks, followed by Document Verification and Medical Examination.
