BEML Non Executive Recruitment 2026 - Apply Online for 10 Posts for Fresher Diploma | Last Date 08-09-2026

Published by: RozgarDwaar Editorial Desk | Updated: 2026-08-31
Apply Online (Official Website) Download Notification PDF Official Website

BEML Limited, a Schedule ‘A’ Public Sector Undertaking under the Ministry of Defence, Government of India, has released Advertisement No. KP/S/14/2026 dated 18 August 2026 for the recruitment of Non-Executive Personnel on Tenure Basis. A total of 10 vacancies are available for candidates possessing a Diploma in Electronics. Eligible candidates can apply online through the official BEML careers portal. This article provides the available details from the short advertisement. Candidates are advised to refer to the detailed notification for complete eligibility, age limit, pay scale, application fee, last date and selection process.

BEML Non-Executive 2026 Eligibility Criteria::
  • Age Limit: 29 years for General / EWS, 34 years for SC / ST and 32 years for OBC.
  • Qualification: 3 Year Full time Polytechnic Engineering Diploma in Electronics & Communication, Electrical & Electronics, Instrumentation, Industrial Electronics, Mechatronics, with first Class (60%).
BEML Non-Executive 2026 Selection Process::

The selection process will be as per the detailed advertisement and may include one or more of the following stages:

  • Computer-Based Test / Written Test
  • Skill / Trade Test
  • Interview / Assessment
  • Document Verification
  • Medical Examination
BEML Non-Executive 2026 Application Fee::
  • Rs. 200/- for General / EWS / OBC category candidates.
  • No fee for SC / St / PwBD category candidates.
  • Payment Mode: Online.

How to Apply for BEML Non-Executive Recruitment 2026?:

Eligible candidates must apply online only through the official BEML careers portal. Follow these steps:

  • Step 1: Visit the official website www.bemlindia.in/careers.
  • Step 2: Locate Advertisement No. KP/S/14/2026 and open the detailed advertisement.
  • Step 3: Read the complete eligibility criteria carefully.
  • Step 4: Register and fill the online application form with personal, educational and other required details.
  • Step 5: Upload the necessary documents, pay the application fee (if applicable) and submit the form.
  • Step 6: Take a printout of the submitted application for future reference.
