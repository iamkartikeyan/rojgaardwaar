Frequently Asked Questions::

What are the qualification for agricultural government jobs?

✔️ B.Sc. Degree or Diploma in Horticulture / Agriculture or relevant discipline.
✔️ B.E. / B.Tech engineering graduate in Agriculture engineering or relevant engineering disciplines.
✔️ Post Graduate / Ph.D in Agriculture or Horticulture or relevant area.

What are the career opportunities in agriculture?

✔️ Agricultural Scientists Recruitment Board (ASRB) - Agricultural Research Service
✔️ Agricultural Field Officer
✔️ Agriculture Officer
✔️ Scientist
✔️ Botanist
✔️ Farm Manager
✔️ Agromet Oberver
✔️ Young Professionals
✔️ Subject Matter Specialist
✔️ Agricultural Faculty Posts etc.

Which govt sector provides job opportunity for agriculture?

Most of the Central / State Government sector includes Banking, Public Sector Companies and other Organizations provides career opportunity for Agriculture Graduates and Horticulturist.

Top Agriculture related Govt Ministries/ Organizations / Companies are

✔️ Indian Council of Agricultural Research (ICAR)
✔️ National Fertilizer Limited (NFL)
✔️ Fertilisers and Chemicals Travancore (FACT)
✔️ Ministry of Agriculture and Farmers Welfare
✔️ National Seeds Corporation
✔️ Indira Gandhi Agricultural University (IGAU)
✔️ Krishi Vigyan Kendras (KVK)
✔️ Indian Agricultural Research Institute (IARI)

Difference between Agriculture vs Horticulture Jobs?

The Horticulture Jobs involve small scale gardening, garden cultivation work. But, Agriculture involves large scale of cultivation of crops as well as animal farming.
