India's law enforcement is managed by various agencies, including those at the State and Central Government levels. Central Government Agencies are under the jurisdiction of the Government of India, operating under the Ministry of Home Affairs. State and Union Territory Agencies, on the other hand, are managed by each respective State or Union, with oversight from the Chief Minister and Home Minister.

Top Police Recruitment Boards::

✔️ Union Public Service Commission (UPSC): The UPSC conducts exams for top-level police officers like IPS.
✔️ Staff Selection Commission (SSC): The SSC conducts various police exams, including CAPF, SI, and Constable exams.
✔️ State Public Service Commissions (PSC): Each state has its own Public Service Commission for conducting state-level police recruitment exams.
✔️ Central Reserve Police Force (CRPF): Conducts recruitment for various posts in the CRPF.
✔️ Border Security Force (BSF): Holds recruitment for different ranks within the BSF. Indo-Tibetan Border Police (ITBP): Manages recruitment for various positions within the ITBP.
✔️ Sashastra Seema Bal (SSB): Carries out recruitment for SSB personnel. Railway Protection Force (RPF): Responsible for recruitment in the railway security sector.
✔️ Central Armed Police Forces (CAPF): Conducts exams for forces like CRPF, BSF, ITBP, SSB, and Assam Rifles.
✔️ State Police Recruitment Boards: Each state in India has its own Police Recruitment Board responsible for recruiting police personnel at various levels.

Police Recruitment Salary::

✔️ For Constable Posts: Level-3 Pay Matrix ₹ 21,700 - 69,100/- (As per 7th CPC)
✔️ For Head Constable Posts: Pay Level 4, ₹ 25,500 - 81,100/- (as per 7th CPC)
✔️ For Assistant Sub Inspector Posts: Pay Level 5, ₹ 29,200 - 92,300/- (as per 7th CPC)
✔️ For Sub Inspector Posts: Level-6, ₹ 35,400 - 1,12,400/- (as per 7th CPC)

Police Recruitment Eligibility Criteria::

✔️ Constable: Matriculation or 10th class pass.
✔️ Assistant Sub Inspector: Matriculation with 12th class Pass.
✔️ Head Constable: Bachelor's degree or equivalent qualification.

Frequently Asked Questions::

What type of Central Police Agencies List?

✔️ Central Armed Police Forces (CAPF): Border Security Force (BSF), Central Industrial Security Force (CISF), Central Reserve Police Force (CRPF), Indo-Tibetan Border Police (ITBP), National Security Guards (NSG), Railway Protection Force (RPF), Special Protection Group (SPG) and Sashastra Seema Bal (SSB).

✔️ Central Investigation and Intelligence Institutions: Central Bureau of Investigations (CBI), Directorate of Revenue Intelligence, Indian Income-tax Department, National Investigation Agency (NIA), Narcotics Control Bureau (NCB), Bureau of Police Research & Development (BPR&D) and National Crime Records Bureau (NCRB).

What type of State Police Agencies List?

District, Division wise Police Department, Criminal Investigation Department (CB-CID), Metropolitan police, Traffic Police, State Armed Police Forces
