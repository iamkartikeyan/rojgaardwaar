JSSC JTGLCCE Recruitment 2026 (611 Posts) – Notification, Online Form | Last Date 30-06-2026

Published by: RozgarDwaar Editorial Desk | Updated: 2026-06-25
Apply Online (Official Website) Download Notification PDF Official Website

Jharkhand Staff Selection Commission (JSSC) has released Advertisement No. 01/2026 dated 07.05.2026 for Jharkhand Technical/Special Qualification Graduate Level Combined Competitive Examination-2026 (JTGLCCE-2026). Online applications are invited for 611 regular vacancies across 10 posts under various departments of Jharkhand Government.

The JSSC JTGLCCE Recruitment 2026 online registration starts from 01st June 2026 and closes on 30th June 2026. This article provides complete information about JSSC JTGLCCE 2026, including vacancy details, eligibility criteria, selection process, syllabus pattern, application fee, and online application link.

Selection Process::

The selection will be done through two stages of Computer Based Test:

  • Preliminary Examination (Qualifying) – 120 MCQs (General Studies, Jharkhand GK, Maths, Science, Mental Ability)
  • Main Examination – 3 Papers (Hindi, Tribal/Regional Language, Technical + GS)

Final merit will be prepared on the basis of Paper-2 and Paper-3 of the Main Exam.

Application Fee::
  • UR / EWS / OBC / Other States: ₹100/-
  • Jharkhand SC / ST: ₹50/-
  • PwD (Jharkhand): NIL
  • Payment Mode: Online only.

How to Apply JSSC JTGLCCE Recruitment 2026?:

Eligible candidates can apply online through the JSSC portal. Steps are as follows:

  • Step 1: Visit https://jssc.jharkhand.gov.in/ and click on the online application link for JTGLCCE-2026.
  • Step 2: Register with valid email and mobile number.
  • Step 3: Fill the application form with personal, educational and other details carefully.
  • Step 4: Upload scanned photo, signature and required documents.
  • Step 5: Pay the application fee online and submit the form.
  • Step 6: Take printout of the final submitted application for future reference.
