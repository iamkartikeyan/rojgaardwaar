RCFL Management Trainee Recruitment 2026 – Apply Online for 94 Posts | Last Date 10-09-2026

Published by: RozgarDwaar Editorial Desk | Updated: 2026-08-25
Apply Online (Official Website) Download Notification PDF Official Website

Rashtriya Chemicals and Fertilizers Limited (RCF Ltd), a Navratna Central Public Sector Undertaking under the Government of India, has released the recruitment notification for Management Trainee posts across various disciplines. A total of 94 vacancies have been announced. The online application process will start from 08 August 2026 and the last date to apply is 24 August 2026. The last date for registration of online application is extended up to 10th September 2026.

RCFL Management Trainee Recruitment 2026 Eligibility Criteria::
  • Educational Qualification: Full-time B.E./B.Tech / Master’s Degree / CA / CMA / MBA / PGDM as per the specific discipline from a recognized University/Institute with minimum 60% marks (55% for SC/ST candidates).
  • Discipline-wise Qualification:
    • Chemical: B.E./B.Tech in Chemical / Petrochemical Engineering
    • Boiler: B.E./B.Tech (Chemical/Mechanical/Electrical/Instrumentation) + Boiler Operation Engineer’s Certificate
    • Mechanical: B.E./B.Tech in Mechanical Engineering
    • Electrical: B.E./B.Tech in Electrical Engineering
    • Instrumentation: B.E./B.Tech in Instrumentation Engineering
    • Materials: B.E./B.Tech (Chemical/Mechanical/Electrical/Instrumentation)
    • Civil: B.E./B.Tech in Civil / Construction Engineering
    • Fire: B.E./B.Tech in Fire or Fire & Safety
    • CC Lab: Ph.D in Chemistry or B.E./B.Tech in Chemical Engineering
    • Industrial Engineering: B.E./B.Tech in Industrial Engineering (or Engineering Graduate + PG in Industrial Engineering)
    • Information Technology: B.E./B.Tech in IT / Computer Science / Computer Engineering
    • Rajbhasha: Master’s Degree in Hindi / English with the other as compulsory/elective subject
    • Finance: CA / CMA or B.Com / BMS / BAF / BBA + MBA / PGDM in Finance
    • Marketing: Science / Engineering / Agriculture Graduate + MBA / PGDM in Marketing or Agri-Business
  • Age Limit (as on 01.02.2026): Maximum 27 years for UR/EWS candidates.
    Age Relaxation: OBC (NCL) – 30 years | SC/ST – 32 years | PwBD – Additional relaxation as per rules | Ex-Servicemen & others as per Government norms.
  • Experience: No prior experience required (Freshers can apply).
RCFL Management Trainee Recruitment 2026 Selection Process::
  • Online Written Test (Computer Based Test)
  • Personal Interview
  • Document Verification
  • Pre-Employment Medical Examination

Final merit will be prepared based on performance in Online Test and Interview (weightage as per company rules).

RCFL Management Trainee Recruitment 2026 Application Fee::
  • General / OBC (NCL) / EWS: ₹1,000/- + Bank Charges + applicable GST
  • SC / ST / Ex-Servicemen / PwBD / Female candidates: Nil (Exempted)
  • Payment Mode: Online (Internet Banking / Debit Card / Credit Card)

How to Apply for RCFL Management Trainee Recruitment 2026::

Eligible candidates can apply online through the official website of RCF Ltd. Follow the steps given below:

➢ Step 1: Visit the official website www.rcfltd.com and click on “APPLY ONLINE” under the Careers / Recruitment section.

➢ Step 2: Click on “Click here for New Registration” and enter your Name, Contact details and Email ID to generate Provisional Registration Number and Password.

➢ Step 3: Login and fill the online application form carefully with all required details.

➢ Step 4: Upload scanned photograph, signature, left thumb impression and handwritten declaration as per the prescribed specifications.

➢ Step 5: Preview the application, make corrections if required, and click on “COMPLETE REGISTRATION”.

➢ Step 6: Pay the application fee (if applicable) through online mode and submit the form.

➢ Step 7: Take a printout of the submitted application form for future reference.

FAQs::
What is the total number of vacancies in RCFL Management Trainee Recruitment 2026?:

A total of 94 Management Trainee posts have been announced across 14 disciplines.

What is the last date to apply for RCFL Management Trainee 2026?:

The last date to submit the online application is 10 September 2026 (upto 05:00 PM).

What is the stipend and salary for RCFL Management Trainee?:

During one-year training period, candidates will get a stipend of ₹60,000 per month. After successful training, they will be absorbed in E1 Grade with approximate gross emoluments of ₹86,320 per month.

What is the age limit for RCFL Management Trainee Recruitment 2026?:

Maximum age limit is 27 years for UR/EWS candidates as on 01.02.2026. Age relaxation is applicable for reserved categories as per rules.

Is there any bond required for RCFL Management Trainee?:

Yes. Candidates have to execute a bond of ₹2,00,000 for a period of 4 years (including training period).

What is the selection process for RCFL Management Trainee posts?:

Selection will be based on Online Written Test followed by Personal Interview, Document Verification and Medical Examination.

Who is exempted from application fee in RCFL MT Recruitment 2026?:

SC, ST, Ex-Servicemen, PwBD and Female candidates are exempted from paying the application fee.
