SAIL RSP Apprentice Recruitment 2026: Notification for 1110 Posts | Apply Online up to 26-08-2026

Published by: RozgarDwaar Editorial Desk | Updated: 2026-08-25
Apply Online (Official Website) Download Notification PDF Official Website

Steel Authority of India Limited (SAIL), Rourkela Steel Plant (RSP) invites applications from eligible Indian citizens for engagement of Apprentices under the Apprentices Act, 1961 for the year 2026-27.

SAIL Rourkela Steel Plant Apprentice Recruitment 2026 has been released for a total of 1110 Apprentice posts including Trade Apprentices and Graduate/Technician Apprentices. The apprenticeship training will be for a period of one year. Interested candidates must first register on the respective government portals and then appear for Walk-in as per the schedule. This article provides complete details about SAIL RSP Apprentice Recruitment 2026.

SAIL Rourkela Apprentice 2026 Selection Process::

Selection will be done on the basis of academic merit through Walk-in process.

  • 30% weightage to marks in Class 10th
  • 70% weightage to marks in ITI / Diploma / Degree

  • Graduate Apprentice: ₹12,300/- per month
  • Technician Apprentice (Diploma): ₹10,900/- per month

How to Apply for SAIL Rourkela Apprentice Recruitment 2026?:

Step 1: Candidates must first register on the respective government portals:

  • For Trade Apprentices (ITI): Register at https://apprenticeshipindia.org/
  • For Graduate & Technician Apprentices: Register at https://nats.education.gov.in

Step 2: After registration, apply against SAIL Rourkela Steel Plant on the respective portal.

Step 3: Take a printout of the application and appear for Walk-in as per the schedule with all required documents.

Documents Required at Walk-in::
  • Original Institution Leaving Certificate
  • Self-attested copy of Matriculation Certificate (for DOB)
  • Self-attested copy of Provisional Pass Certificate
  • Self-attested copy of Caste Certificate (SC/ST/OBC/PwBD)
  • Self-attested copy of Aadhaar Card
  • Medical Fitness Certificate from Government Hospital
  • Apprentice Registration copy from NAPS/NATS Portal.
