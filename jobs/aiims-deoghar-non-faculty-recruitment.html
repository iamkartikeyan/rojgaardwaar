AIIMS Deoghar Non Faculty Recruitment 2026 – Apply for 11 Posts | Last Date 20-07-2026

Published by: RozgarDwaar Editorial Desk | Updated: 2026-07-19
Apply Online (Official Website) Download Notification PDF Official Website

AIIMS Deoghar Non Faculty Recruitment 2026: Apply Offline for 11 Contractual Posts. All India Institute of Medical Sciences (AIIMS) Deoghar has officially released the notification for contractual engagement of 11 Non-Faculty posts on purely contract basis for 11 months (extendable on performance basis or till regular appointment). This is an excellent opportunity for eligible candidates to join a prestigious AIIMS institution under the Ministry of Health & Family Welfare, Government of India.

Applications are invited in offline mode only. The last date for receipt of the completed application form is 20 July 2026. Candidates must download the prescribed application form from the official website and send the hard copy along with required documents and Demand Draft to the Recruitment Cell, AIIMS Deoghar.

AIIMS Deoghar Non Faculty 2026 Remuneration:

Selected candidates will receive consolidated monthly pay (no other allowances):

  • ₹1,08,320 p.m. – Blood Transfusion Officer, Senior Programmer, Public Relation Officer
  • ₹89,760 p.m. – Assistant Blood Transfusion Officer, Law Officer, Security Officer
  • ₹71,840 p.m. – Asst. Engineer (Electrical), Sanitation Officer, Biomedical Engineer
  • ₹56,640 p.m. – Laundry Manager, Perfusionist

The engagement is purely contractual for 11 months (extendable on performance or till regular appointment).

AIIMS Deoghar Non Faculty 2026 Eligibility Criteria:

Age Limit (as on date of advertisement):

  • As mentioned against each post (relaxation as per Govt. of India rules for SC/ST/OBC/PwD/Govt. servants)

Educational Qualification & Experience (Post-wise essential requirements):

  • Blood Transfusion Officer: MBBS + 5 years experience in Blood Bank OR MD in Blood Transfusion Medicine + 1 year experience
  • Senior Programmer: BE/B.Tech/MCA/B.Sc. (with Diploma in Computer Application) + 10 years experience in IT Systems/Networking/Programming
  • Public Relation Officer: Degree + PG/Diploma in PR/Corporate Communication/Journalism/Mass Communication + 10 years experience
  • Assistant Blood Transfusion Officer: MBBS + 2 years experience in Blood Bank + registered with State Medical Council
  • Law Officer: Degree in Law + 6 years experience as legal practitioner
  • Security Officer: Degree + Officers from Police/Armed Forces/Para Military with analogous post or 2/3 years in PB-2 GP 4800/4600
  • Asst. Engineer (Electrical): B.E./B.Tech (Electrical) + 5 years experience in design/engineering of civil projects (preferably hospital)
  • Sanitation Officer: 12th Pass + 1-year Health Sanitary Inspector Course + 10 years experience in sanitation (preferably 200-bedded hospital)
  • Biomedical Engineer: B.E./B.Tech in Biomedical Engineering OR Diploma + 2 years experience
  • Laundry Manager: 12th Pass + Diploma/Certificate in Dry Cleaning/Laundry Technology + 12 years experience in mechanized laundry
  • Perfusionist: B.Sc. + Certificate in Perfusion Technology + 1 year experience in Clinical Perfusion
AIIMS Deoghar Non Faculty 2026 Selection Process:
  1. Screening of Applications
  2. Document Verification
  3. Interview

Shortlisted candidates will be informed via email for document verification and interview. No TA/DA will be paid.

How to Apply for AIIMS Deoghar Non Faculty Recruitment 2026:
  1. Download the prescribed application form from the official website www.aiimsdeoghar.edu.in
  2. Fill the application form carefully in all respects
  3. Attach self-attested photocopies of all required documents (educational certificates, experience certificates, age proof, caste certificate if applicable, etc.)
  4. Prepare Demand Draft for the applicable application fee
  5. Enclose the original Demand Draft with the application
  6. Send the complete application through offline mode (Speed Post / Registered Post) to the address given below so as to reach on or before 20 July 2026
  7. Address: Recruitment Cell, All India Institute of Medical Sciences, Deoghar, At- Devipur Campus, Ramsagar, Deoghar-814117, Jharkhand
  8. Candidates working in Govt./Autonomous bodies must enclose No Objection Certificate (NOC)
