MRPL Fire Officer Recruitment 2026 – Apply Online for 05 Posts | Last Date 16-07-2026

Published by: RozgarDwaar Editorial Desk | Updated: 2026-07-15
Apply Online (Official Website) Download Notification PDF Official Website

Mangalore Refinery and Petrochemicals Limited (MRPL), a subsidiary of Oil and Natural Gas Corporation Limited (ONGC), invites online applications from eligible candidates for the post of Fire Officer on Fixed Term Employment basis.

The MRPL Fire Officer Recruitment 2026 for 05 Vacancies Online Application process starts from 17th June 2026 and closes on 16th July 2026. This article provides complete information about MRPL Fire Officer Jobs 2026, including eligibility criteria, pay scale, selection process, official notification, and online application link.

MRPL Fire Officer Recruitment 2026 – 05 Posts:
rozgardwaar.com:

Post Name

Fire Officer (Executive Level L1)

Total Vacancies

05 Posts (UR-4, OBC (NCL)-1)

Age Limit

Maximum 50 Years (UR) | 53 Years (OBC-NCL)
(as on 16.07.2026)

Pay Scale

₹13 Lakh per annum (5–6 years experience)
₹14 Lakh per annum (more than 6 years experience)

Minimum Qualification

Bachelor’s Degree in Fire / Fire & Safety Engineering with minimum 60% marks

Experience

Minimum 5 years post-qualification experience in Fire/Fire & Safety in Oil & Gas / Petrochemicals / Fertilizer Industry

Application Fee

₹118/- (General / OBC / EWS)
Nil (SC / ST / PwBD / Ex-Servicemen)

Selection Process

Shortlisting + Interview

Job Location

Mangalore, Karnataka

Last Date

16 July 2026

MRPL Fire Officer Vacancy 2026 Details::
Position No. of Posts Reservation Qualification & Experience
Fire Officer
(Executive Level L1)		 05 UR-4
OBC (NCL)-1		 Bachelor’s Degree in Fire / Fire & Safety Engineering with minimum 60% marks AND Minimum 5 years post-qualification experience in Fire/Fire & Safety in Oil & Gas / Petrochemicals / Fertilizer Industry
Job Description::
  • Managing the fire station and shift related activities.
  • Upkeep and maintenance of fire protection facilities and responding to emergencies.
  • Emergency planning and preparedness.
  • Co-ordination with various departments for Fire and Safety related activities.
  • Imparting Fire and Safety related training to employees and workers.
Selection Process::

Selection will be made through shortlisting of eligible candidates followed by Personal Interview. The final selection will be based on performance in the Interview.

Application Fee::
  • General / OBC (NCL) / EWS: ₹118/- (₹100 + 18% GST)
  • SC / ST / PwBD / Ex-Servicemen: Exempted
  • Payment Mode: Online through SBI e-Collect

17 June 2026

How to Apply MRPL Fire Officer Recruitment 2026?:

Eligible candidates must apply online only through the MRPL website. The online application steps are as follows:-

  • Step 1: Visit https://www.mrpl.co.in/careers and click on the advertisement link.
  • Step 2: Register by creating Login ID and Password.
  • Step 3: Login and fill the online application form with correct details.
  • Step 4: Upload scanned copies of Photograph, Signature, Educational Certificates, Experience Certificates and other required documents.
  • Step 5: Pay the application fee (if applicable) through SBI e-Collect and submit the form.
  • Step 6: Take a printout of the submitted application form for future reference.
