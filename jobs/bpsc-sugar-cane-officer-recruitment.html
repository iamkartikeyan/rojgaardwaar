BPSC Sugar Cane Officer Recruitment 2026 - Apply Online for 44 Posts | Last Date 31 May 2026

Minimum Graduate candidates are eligible to apply for Sugar Cane Officer posts. The BPSC online application form has started on 07 May 2026, and eligible candidates can apply until 31 May 2026.

BPSC Sugar Cane Officer Vacancy 2026 Eligibility Criteria::
Qualification & Experience::
Post Name Essential Educational Qualification
Sugar Cane Officer Bachelor’s Degree in any discipline from a recognized University. Preference will be given to candidates having qualification in Agriculture or allied subjects.
  • Age Limit (as on 01.08.2026): Minimum 20/21/22 years (post-specific) and Maximum – UR (Male): 37 years, OBC/Female: 40 years, SC/ST: 42 years. Age relaxation as per BPSC / Bihar Government norms.
BPSC Sugar Cane Officer Recruitment 2026 Selection Process (Full Details)::

The selection will be conducted through the 72nd Combined Competitive Examination (CCE) in three successive stages:

  • Stage-I: Preliminary Examination – Objective Type (Screening Test). Only qualified candidates will proceed to Main Examination.
  • Stage-II: Main Examination – Descriptive / Written Examination (conventional type papers).
  • Stage-III: Interview / Personality Test – For candidates who qualify the Main Examination.
  • Document Verification & Medical Examination (as per BPSC rules)

Final merit list will be prepared on the basis of marks obtained in Main Examination + Interview. Preliminary Examination is only of qualifying nature.

BPSC Sugar Cane Officer Vacancy 2026 Application Fee::
  • All Candidates: ₹100/- per examination (72nd CCE)
  • Candidates without Aadhaar: Additional ₹200/- (Biometric Fee) per examination
  • Payment Mode: Online only through the BPSC portal. Fee once paid will not be refunded.

How to Apply BPSC Sugar Cane Officer Recruitment 2026::

Eligible & Interested candidates must apply online only through the BPSC Online Application Portal.

➢ Step 1: Visit the official BPSC website https://bpsc.bihar.gov.in and go to the Online Application Portal (bpsconline.bihar.gov.in).

➢ Step 2: Complete One Time Registration (OTR) – only one OTR is allowed per candidate.

➢ Step 3: Login and fill the online application form carefully with personal, educational, category, and other details.

➢ Step 4: Upload all required documents in PDF format (max 100 KB each), live webcam photograph, scanned signatures (Hindi & English), and thumb impression.

➢ Step 5: Pay the application fee + biometric fee (if applicable) online.

➢ Step 6: Submit the form and take a printout of the finally submitted application form for future reference. Keep the Application Number safe.
