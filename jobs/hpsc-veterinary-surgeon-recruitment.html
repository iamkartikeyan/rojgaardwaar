HPSC Veterinary Surgeon Recruitment 2026 Online Form for 162 Vacancies - Notification PDF

Published by: RozgarDwaar Editorial Desk | Updated: 2026-01-16
Apply Online (Official Website) Download Notification PDF Official Website

Haryana Public Service Commission (HPSC) has released an official notification for the recruitment of 162 Veterinary Surgeon posts in the Animal Husbandry & Dairying Department, Haryana. Eligible candidates can apply online through the official HPSC portal. The last date to submit the application form is 19 February 2026.

This recruitment is being conducted as per the relevant Service Rules of Haryana Government. Candidates must possess the prescribed educational qualifications, age eligibility, and professional registration to apply for these posts.

HPSC Veterinary Surgeon Recruitment 2026:

🎯 Age Limit 22 – 42 Years

Eligibility Criteria:
  • Bachelor of Veterinary Science & Animal Husbandry (B.V.Sc & A.H.) from a recognized University
  • Adequate knowledge of Hindi
  • Registered as Veterinary Practitioner with Haryana Veterinary Council / Indian Veterinary Council
  • Desirable: MVSc / Ph.D. in Veterinary or Animal Husbandry branches
Salary Structure:
  • Pay Level: FPL-9
  • Monthly Salary: ₹53,100 – ₹1,67,800
  • Other allowances as per Haryana Government rules

  • Minimum Age: 22 Years
  • Maximum Age: 42 Years

How to Apply for HPSC Veterinary Surgeon Recruitment 2026:

Candidates who meet the eligibility criteria must submit their application online only through the official Haryana Public Service Commission (HPSC) website. No offline applications will be accepted.

  • Step 1: Visit the official website: hpsc.gov.in
  • Step 2: Register using your Mobile Number / Aadhaar Number.
  • Step 3: Login with the generated User ID & Password.
  • Step 4: Fill the application form carefully with correct personal, educational, and category details.
  • Step 5: Pay the application fee through the available online payment mode.
  • Step 6: Download the application form, take a printout, sign it, scan it, and upload the signed copy.
  • Step 7: Submit the form finally and save the confirmation receipt / PDF for future reference.
General Instructions:
  • Only online applications will be accepted.
  • Eligibility will be determined on the closing date of application.
  • Certificates issued after the closing date will not be accepted.
  • Application fee once paid is non-refundable.
  • Admit Card will be issued online only.
  • Mere issuance of Admit Card does not confirm eligibility.
  • Service Rules are available on the Animal Husbandry Department, Haryana website.
