UCO Bank IT Specialist Officer Recruitment 2026 - Apply Online for 20 Posts | Last Date 18-09-2026

Published by: RozgarDwaar Editorial Desk | Updated: 2026-08-31
Apply Online (Official Website) Download Notification PDF Official Website

UCO Bank has invited online applications from eligible candidates for appointment to Specialist Officer posts on a regular basis under Advertisement No. HO/HRM/RECR/2026-27/COM-01. The recruitment covers Manager-level roles in Artificial Intelligence/Machine Learning and Cyber Security. The UCO Bank IT Specialist Officer Recruitment 2026 online registration process started on 29th August 2026 and will close on 18th September 2026. This article covers vacancy details, eligibility criteria, pay scale, application fee, selection process, official notification, and the online application link.

UCO Bank Specialist Officer 2026 Eligibility Criteria::

Post Name

Age Limit (as on 01.08.2026)

Manager – AI/ML Engineer

25 – 35 years

Full-time B.E./B.Tech in CS/IT/AI & ML/Data Science with 50% marks, or MCA / M.Tech/M.E. in the same fields with 50% marks + minimum 3 years post-qualification experience in AI, GenAI, NLP or content analysis projects. Preferred: certification in AI, GenAI, NLP or ML.

Manager – Cyber Security

25 – 35 years

Full-time B.Tech/B.E. in CS/CSE/Software Engineering/IT/Electronics/ECE or equivalent with 50% marks, or MCA / M.Tech/M.Sc in the same fields with 50% marks + minimum 3 years hands-on experience in offensive cyber security (Red Team, AI Red Team, pentesting, LLM/Frontier AI security testing, or advanced vulnerability assessment). Preferred: advanced degree in Cyber Security or IT.

Upper age relaxation: SC/ST – 5 years, OBC (NCL) – 3 years, PwBD – 10 years, eligible Ex-Servicemen/ECOs/SSCOs – 5 years, and persons affected by 1984 riots – 5 years, as per Government norms.

How to Apply UCO Bank Specialist Officer Recruitment 2026?:

Eligible candidates must apply online only through the UCO Bank career portal. No other mode of application will be accepted. The steps are as follows:

  • Step 1: Visit https://uco.bank.in, go to Career → Recruitment Opportunities and open Advertisement No. HO/HRM/RECR/2026-27/COM-01.
  • Step 2: Click on “Click Here To Apply Online”, complete registration with a valid email ID and mobile number, then log in.
  • Step 3: Fill the application form and select the post for which you are applying.
  • Step 4: Upload photograph, signature and documents — identity & address proof (max 100 KB), proof of birth (max 100 KB), educational certificates (max 1 MB), caste/disability certificate if applicable (max 1 MB), and experience certificates if applicable (max 1 MB).
  • Step 5: Pay the application fee online, submit the form and take a printout of the application for future reference. The last date to print the application is 21 September 2026.
