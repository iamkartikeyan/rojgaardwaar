2. About Kendriya Vidyalaya Sangathan (KVS) & Organizational Profile:

Kendriya Vidyalaya Sangathan (KVS) is a premier constitutional, statutory, or central public sector enterprise mandated with executing crucial governance, infrastructure, financial, administrative, and public utility operations. Employing thousands of personnel across regional, zonal, and departmental wings, Kendriya Vidyalaya Sangathan (KVS) maintains state-of-the-art administrative facilities, merit-driven evaluation systems, and extensive healthcare, residential, and retirement security infrastructures. Serving in Kendriya Vidyalaya Sangathan (KVS) offers public recognition, structured promotions, and defined pension security.

4. Salary Structure, Allowances & Monthly In-Hand Pay:

Selected candidates for Multi-Tasking Staff (MTS) / Office Attendant / Peon will receive compensation under the pay matrix of PRT / TGT / PGT (Level-6 to Level-8). In addition to Basic Pay, employees are entitled to comprehensive allowances:

  • Dearness Allowance (DA): Paid at the prevailing government-notified rate (currently 50%+ of Basic Pay), revised every 6 months to offset cost-of-living increases.
  • House Rent Allowance (HRA): Disbursed based on posting city classification: 30% for Class X (Metros), 20% for Class Y (State Capitals), and 10% for Class Z (Rural/Towns). Government accommodation/quarters may be allotted in lieu of HRA.
  • Transport Allowance (TA): Fixed monthly allowance plus applicable DA to support daily commuting expenses.
  • Comprehensive Medical Facility: Full medical reimbursement / cashless treatment for employee and dependent family members under CGHS, ECHS, or company medical panel.
  • Children Education Allowance (CEA): Annual reimbursement of up to ₹27,000 per child (up to two children) plus hostel subsidies.
  • Pension & Social Security: Covered under the Defined National Pension System (NPS) / Unified Pension Scheme with statutory matching government contributions.
  • Monthly In-Hand Net Pay: After mandatory statutory deductions (NPS 10%, Professional Tax, TDS), the net credited salary is estimated between ₹35,150 and ₹43,150 per month.
5. In-Depth Educational Qualification & Experience Criteria:

To ensure high administrative and technical standards, candidates must strictly satisfy the following minimum criteria:

  • Mandatory Degree / Certificate: Teaching / B.Ed / Graduate / 10th Pass from a recognized Board / University / Institute.. The degree/diploma must be obtained from a University or Institution recognized by UGC, AICTE, NCVT, or State Government.
  • Minimum Aggregate Marks: A minimum aggregate of 50% to 60% marks (or equivalent CGPA) in the qualifying degree for General/OBC candidates, with passing marks required for SC/ST/PwBD candidates.
  • Final Year Students: Candidates appearing in their final semester/year are eligible to submit applications provided they acquire their provisional degree/marksheet on or before 2026-09-04.
  • Professional Registrations: For technical, nursing, legal, or trade posts, candidates must hold valid council registrations at the time of online document verification.
