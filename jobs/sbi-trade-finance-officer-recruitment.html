SBI Trade Finance Officer Recruitment 2026: Apply Online for 35 Posts | Last Date 19-09-2026

Published by: RozgarDwaar Editorial Desk | Updated: 2026-08-31
Apply Online (Official Website) Download Notification PDF Official Website

State Bank of India (SBI) has invited online applications from eligible candidates for appointment of Trade Finance Officer – International Banking Group (TFO-IBG) on a regular basis under Advertisement No. CRPD/SCO/2026-27/15. The recruitment covers Junior Management Grade Scale-I and Middle Management Grade Scale-II posts. The SBI Trade Finance Officer Recruitment 2026 online registration process started on 29th August 2026 and will close on 19th September 2026. This article provides complete details on vacancies, eligibility, pay scale, application fee, selection process, official notification and the online application link.

SBI Trade Finance Officer 2026 Eligibility Criteria::

Post Name

Age Limit (as on 31.07.2026)

Qualification & Experience

TFO-IBG (JMGS-I)

21 – 30 years

Graduate in any discipline from a Government recognised University/Institution + Certification in Forex Operations from IIBF (dated on or before 31.07.2026) + minimum 1 year post-qualification experience in Trade Finance processing and operations as Officer/Official in a scheduled commercial bank (maker role preferred)

TFO-IBG (MMGS-II)

23 – 32 years

Graduate in any discipline from a Government recognised University/Institution + Certification in Forex Operations from IIBF (dated on or before 31.07.2026) + minimum 3 years post-qualification experience in Trade Finance processing as Officer/Official in a scheduled commercial bank

Preferred certifications (dated on or before 31.07.2026): CDCS / CSDG by LIBF, CTFP or GTC by ICC Academy, CITF by LIBF, or Certificate in International Trade Finance by IIBF.

SBI Trade Finance Officer 2026 Selection Process::

The selection will be conducted through the following stages:

  • Shortlisting
  • Interview (100 marks)

Meeting the minimum qualification and experience does not guarantee a call for interview. The Bank’s shortlisting committee will decide the parameters and the number of candidates to be called. The merit list will be prepared only on interview scores. If more than one candidate scores the same cut-off marks, ranking will be decided by age in descending order.

SBI TFO Recruitment 2026 Application Fee::
  • General / EWS / OBC candidates: ₹750/- (Application Fee + Intimation Charges, non-refundable)
  • SC / ST / PwBD candidates: No fee / intimation charges
  • Payment Method: Online mode only through Debit Card / Credit Card / Internet Banking. Fee once paid will not be refunded or adjusted against any other recruitment.

How to Apply SBI Trade Finance Officer Recruitment 2026?:

Eligible candidates must apply online only through the SBI careers portal. Keep a valid email ID and mobile number active till the result is declared. The steps are as follows:

  • Step 1: Visit the SBI careers page or open the direct apply link for Advertisement No. CRPD/SCO/2026-27/15.
  • Step 2: Scan and keep ready the latest photograph, signature and all required PDF documents before starting registration.
  • Step 3: Fill the online form carefully. You may save a partly filled form (provisional registration number and password will be generated). Editing of saved information is allowed only three times.
  • Step 4: Upload photograph, signature, detailed resume, ID proof, date of birth proof, caste/PwBD certificate (if applicable), degree certificate, IIBF Forex Operations certificate, experience certificates, preferred certificates (if any), duly filled biodata form, Form-16/offer letter, NOC (if working in Government/PSU/PSB) and the mandatory undertaking.
  • Step 5: Pay the fee online (if applicable), submit the application and take a printout of the system-generated form and e-receipt for future reference.
