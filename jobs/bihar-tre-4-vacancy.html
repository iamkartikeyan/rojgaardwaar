Bihar TRE 4.0 Vacancy 2026 Notification Out – Apply Online for 32388 Posts | Last Date 30-09-2026

Published by: RozgarDwaar Editorial Desk | Updated: 2026-08-20
Apply Online (Official Website) Download Notification PDF Official Website

Bihar Public Service Commission (BPSC) has issued Advertisement No. 14/2026 for the Teacher Recruitment Examination 4.0 (TRE-4.0), opening applications for 32,388 School Teacher positions under the Education Department of the Government of Bihar. Online registration and fee payment start on 01 September 2026, with the final submission deadline set for 30 September 2026.

This fourth phase of the School Teacher Recruitment Examination offers opportunities across Primary, Middle, Secondary and Higher Secondary levels. Aspiring teachers can find complete details of BPSC TRE 4.0 Vacancy 2026, including notification and application links, on this page.

BPSC TRE 4.0 Eligibility Criteria 2026::
  • Nationality: Must be a citizen of India.
  • Educational & Training Qualification: As prescribed by NCTE for the respective school level. Special school teachers require RCI-recognised qualification.
  • TET Requirement:
    • Primary (1–5): CTET Paper-I or BTET Paper-I
    • Middle (6–8): CTET Paper-II or BTET Paper-II
    • Secondary (9–10) & Special: STET Paper-I
    • Higher Secondary (11–12): STET Paper-II
  • Teachers appointed before 2012 who cleared Dakshata Pariksha are exempt. Candidates appearing in STET 2026 may apply provisionally but must qualify before the Preliminary Exam.
  • Qualification Snapshot:
    • Primary: Senior Secondary + 2-year D.El.Ed / 4-year B.El.Ed or Graduation + D.El.Ed (as per NCTE).
    • Middle: Graduation + D.El.Ed / B.Ed or 4-year integrated B.A./B.Sc.Ed with subject combination.
    • Secondary: Graduation/Post-Graduation (relevant subject, min 50%) + B.Ed.
    • Higher Secondary: Post-Graduation (relevant subject, min 50%) + B.Ed.

Candidates must carefully verify subject-specific combinations and additional pathways (Computer Science, Physical Education, languages, Madrasa Board) in Sections 2(A)–2(R) of the notification.

BPSC TRE 4.0 Age Limit 2026::
  • Minimum Age (as on 01.08.2026): 18 years (Primary & Middle) | 21 years (Secondary & Higher Secondary)
  • Maximum Age (as on 01.08.2024):
    • Unreserved Male: 37 years
    • Unreserved Female / BC / EBC: 40 years
    • SC / ST / Transgender: 42 years
  • Relaxations: PwD – 10 years; Ex-servicemen – 3 years + service period (max age 57); Serving Bihar Govt employees – 5 years (max 5 attempts under this relaxation).
BPSC TRE 4.0 Salary 2026::

Posts fall under non-gazetted School Teacher cadre.

BPSC TRE 4.0 Selection Process 2026::
  • Stage 1 – Preliminary Examination: Objective General Studies paper (150 marks, 2 hours). 5-option format with 1/3 negative marking for wrong or unanswered questions (except option E). Qualifying marks: General 40%, BC 36.5%, EBC 34%, SC/ST/Women/PwD 32%. Shortlisting ratio: 2.5 to 10 times vacancies.
  • Stage 2 – Main Examination: Objective papers (150 marks each, 2 hours) specific to school level. Part I (Language) is qualifying (min 30%). Merit based on Part II (Primary) or Part II + Part III (other levels). Same negative marking and qualifying criteria apply. No re-evaluation.

Guest teachers (Atithi Shikshak) receive service weightage of 5 marks per year (max 25 marks) added to qualifying scores.

How to Apply for BPSC TRE 4.0 Recruitment 2026::

Eligible candidates must apply ONLINE ONLY through the official BPSC OTR portal. No other mode is accepted.

➢ Step 1: Visit https://bpsconline.bihar.gov.in and complete One Time Registration (OTR) using a valid email and mobile number.

➢ Step 2: Link DigiLocker (Meri Pehchan) for document consent or proceed via browser.

➢ Step 3: Log in and complete the six-step profile (Personal, Address, Other, Qualification, Experience, Photo/Signature). Upload live webcam photo and Hindi + English signatures.

➢ Step 4: Lock the profile after review.

➢ Step 5: Select Advertisement No. 14/2026, choose post/subject and up to 38 preferred districts in priority order.

➢ Step 6: Pay the application fee online via SBI gateway and submit the form.

➢ Step 7: Download and print the confirmed application form for records.

Note: Post/subject cannot be changed after fee payment. Maximum 5 attempts allowed under the relevant rules. Reservation benefits apply only to Bihar domicile candidates.

FAQs::
What is the total number of vacancies in BPSC TRE 4.0 Recruitment 2026?:

A total of 32,388 School Teacher posts are available across Primary (3,847), Middle (8,563), Secondary (3,877) and Higher Secondary (16,101) levels.

What is the last date to apply for BPSC TRE 4.0 2026?:

The last date for online application is 30 September 2026. Fee payment closes on 29 September 2026.

What is the age limit for BPSC TRE 4.0 Recruitment 2026?:

Minimum 18 years (Primary/Middle) or 21 years (Secondary/Higher Secondary) as on 01.08.2026. Maximum age ranges from 37 to 42 years depending on category, with applicable relaxations.

What is the educational qualification required for BPSC TRE 4.0 2026?:

NCTE-prescribed qualifications (D.El.Ed / B.Ed / Graduation / Post-Graduation with subject combination) plus the relevant TET (CTET/BTET/STET) for the school level applied for.

What is the selection process for BPSC TRE 4.0 2026?:

Preliminary Examination followed by Main Examination. Final merit is based on Main Exam marks only. There is no interview.

What is the salary for BPSC TRE 4.0 School Teachers?:

Starting pay ranges from ₹25,000 (Primary) to ₹32,000 (Higher Secondary) per month plus admissible allowances.

How to apply for BPSC TRE 4.0 Recruitment 2026?:

Candidates must complete OTR and apply online only through the official BPSC portal bpsconline.bihar.gov.in between 01 September 2026 and 30 September 2026.
