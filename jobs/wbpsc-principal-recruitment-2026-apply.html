WBPSC Principal Recruitment 2026: Apply Online for 12 Posts. The Public Service Commission, West Bengal (WBPSC) has officially released Advertisement No. 05/2026 for recruitment to 12 posts of Principal in the District Institute of Education and Training (DIET) under the School Education Department, Government of West Bengal. The posts are temporary but likely to be permanent. This is a golden opportunity for eligible candidates with Master’s Degree, M.Ed. and teaching experience in Elementary Teachers’ Training Institutes.

The online application started on 30 April 2026 and the last date to apply online has been extended up to 03 July 2026 (upto 3:00 PM). Eligible candidates can apply through the official website psc.wb.gov.in.

WBPSC Principal 2026 Pay Scale:

Selected candidates will be appointed in Pay Level-17 under WBS (ROPA) Rules, 2019:

  • Basic Pay: ₹67,300 – ₹1,73,200 per month

Other allowances as admissible under West Bengal Government rules will also be paid.

WBPSC Principal 2026 Eligibility Criteria:

Age Limit (as on 01.01.2026):

  • Maximum 42 years
  • Age Relaxation: 5 years for SC/ST, 3 years for OBC of West Bengal

Educational Qualification & Experience:

  • Master’s Degree of two years duration (regular) with Honours at Graduation level having at least 55% marks in any school teaching subject
  • M.Ed. / M.Ed. (Elementary) OR M.A. in Education with 55% marks + Diploma/Degree in Primary/Elementary Education with 55% marks
  • Five years’ teaching experience in Primary or Elementary Teachers’ Training Institute
  • Knowledge of Bengali language (spoken & written) – tested at interview (exempted for Nepali mother tongue candidates)
  • Computer Literacy
WBPSC Principal 2026 Selection Process:
  1. Screening Test (MCQ type – for shortlisting only)
  2. Interview (final merit prepared on the basis of Interview marks only)
Important Dates for WBPSC Principal Recruitment 2026:
Commencement of Online Application 30 April 2026
Last Date to Apply Online (Extended) 03 July 2026 (upto 3:00 PM)
Last Date for Fee Payment (Extended) 03 July 2026 (upto 3:00 PM)
Edit Window 25 May to 29 May 2026 (upto 3:00 PM)
Application Fee:
  • General / OBC : ₹210/- + applicable service charges
  • SC / ST (of West Bengal) & PwBD (40% and above) : Nil

Fee is to be paid online only. No claim for refund of fee will be entertained.

How to Apply for WBPSC Principal Recruitment 2026:
  1. Visit the official website https://psc.wb.gov.in
  2. Click on the link for “Advertisement No. 05/2026 – Principal in DIET”
  3. Register / Login and fill the online application form with all required details
  4. Upload scanned photograph, signature and all supporting documents
  5. Pay the application fee (if applicable)
  6. Submit the form and take a printout for future reference
