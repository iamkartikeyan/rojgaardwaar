Haryana PSC PGT Recruitment 2026 - Apply Online (1672 Computer Science Posts)

Published by: RozgarDwaar Editorial Desk | Updated: 2026-02-05
Apply Online (Official Website) Download Notification PDF Official Website

Haryana Public Service Commission (HPSC) invites online applications from eligible candidates for the posts of Post Graduate Teachers (PGTs) in the subject of Computer Science for Rest of Haryana Cadre and Mewat Cadre. The HPSC PGT Computer Science Recruitment 2026 Online Registration starts from 16 February 2026.

The Candidates are strongly advised to carefully read the detailed official notification before applying to confirm eligibility regarding age, qualification, experience, category, and other criteria. The HPSC PGT Computer Science Vacancy 2026 online registration close on 9th March 2026.

HPSC PGT Computer Science Recruitment 2026:
rozgardwaar.com:
Organization

Haryana Public Service Commission (HPSC)

Post Name

Post Graduate Teacher (PGT) – Computer Science

Total Vacancies

1672 Posts

Age Limit

18 – 42 Years (as on 09.03.2026) with usual relaxations

Pay Scale

₹47,600 – ₹1,51,100/- + Usual allowances

Minimum Qualification

M.Sc. Computer Science / MCA / B.E./B.Tech (CS/IT) with 55% marks + HTET/STET + Hindi Knowledge

Application Fee

₹1000/- (Gen/UR/EWS/OBC); ₹250/- (SC/BC/PwD/ExS); Nil (PwD Haryana)

Selection Process

Screening Test + Subject Knowledge Test + Interview / Viva Voce

Job Location

Haryana State (Rest of Haryana & Mewat Cadre)

Last Date

09 March 2026 (05:00 PM)

HPSC PGT Computer Science Vacancy 2026 Details:
Discipline / Cadre No of Vacancies
PGT Computer Science – Rest of Haryana 1594
PGT Computer Science – Mewat Cadre 78
HPSC PGT Computer Science Vacancy 2026 Eligibility Criteria:
Criteria Details
Age Limit (as on 09.03.2026)
  • Minimum 18 Years & Maximum 42 Years.
  • Relaxation: 5 years SC/BC/PwBD/ExS, etc. as per Haryana Govt rules.
Educational Qualification
  • M.Sc. Computer Science (Regular 2-year) / MCA (Regular 3-year) / B.E./B.Tech (CS/IT) with minimum 55% aggregate marks from recognized university.
  • Matric with Hindi/Sanskrit or 10+2/B.A./B.Sc./B.Com with Hindi as one subject.
  • HTET / STET qualified.
  • Consistent good academic record.

How to Apply HPSC PGT Computer Science Recruitment 2026:

Eligible candidates must apply ONLINE only through the HPSC portal. No other mode of application will be accepted.

➢ Step 1: Visit hpsc.gov.in and complete registration / login.

➢ Step 2: Fill the online application form with personal, educational, and qualification details.

➢ Step 3: Upload scanned photograph, signature, and required documents/certificates.

➢ Step 4: Pay the application fee online.

➢ Step 5: Submit the application and take printout of the confirmation page for future reference.
