Assam Police Grade IV Staff Recruitment 2026 (256 Posts) Notification & Online Form

Published by: RozgarDwaar Editorial Desk | Updated: 2026-02-21
Apply Online (Official Website) Download Notification PDF Official Website

STATE LEVEL POLICE RECRUITMENT BOARD (SLPRB), ASSAM invites online applications from eligible Indian citizens who are permanent residents of Assam for filling up 256 posts of Grade IV Staff (Multitasking & Maintenance Staff - Non-Technical) across Assam Police (181 posts), Assam Commando Battalions (6 posts), and DGCD & CGHG (69 posts). The Assam Police Grade IV Recruitment 2026 Online Application starts from 22nd January 2026.

The Candidates are strongly advised to carefully read the detailed official notification before applying to confirm eligibility regarding age, qualification, physical standards, language proficiency, employment exchange registration, and trade-specific certificates. There is no application fee. The last date for online registration is 22nd February 2026, with edit option available for 5 days thereafter (subject to approval).

Criteria Details

Citizenship & Residency

Indian Citizen & Permanent Resident of Assam (Proof required post-final list; exempt for SC/ST/OBC/MOBC)

Employment Exchange

Must be registered with local Employment Exchange in Assam

Language

Must speak Assamese or any other State language fluently

Age (as on 01.01.2026)

18–40 Years (Police/F&ES/DGCD); 18–25 Years (Commando Battalions)
Relaxation: 5 yrs SC/ST(P)/ST(H), 3 yrs OBC/MOBC

Educational Qualification

Minimum Class VIII passed; Maximum HSSLC/ Matric + Class XII passed

Trade-Specific (Desirable for Marks)

Cook/Barber/Water Carrier/Dhobi/Cobbler: SSC Training Certificate or 1-2 years experience from registered institute (5 marks)

  • Height (Male): Gen/OBC/MOBC/SC: 160 cm | ST(P)/ST(H): 158 cm
  • Height (Female): Gen/OBC/MOBC/SC: 150 cm | ST(P)/ST(H): 147.5 cm
  • Chest (Male only): Gen/OBC/MOBC/SC/ST(P): Min. 80 cm (expansion +5 cm) | ST(H): Min. 77 cm (expansion +5 cm)

How to Apply Assam Grade IV Recruitment 2026::

Eligible candidates must apply ONLINE only through the SLPRB portal. Single registration for all advertisements.

➢ Step 1: Register on the portal with valid mobile number, name as per Class VIII/HSLC/Birth Certificate, Employment Exchange No., Qualification, District, Gender, Category, DOB, Aadhaar etc.

➢ Step 2: Note down the generated Application ID.

➢ Step 3: Login, apply for Grade IV advertisement, select post preference, upload photo/signature/documents (Class VIII cert, Employment Exchange Card, Aadhaar, Caste cert if applicable, Trade Training/Experience cert if any).

➢ Step 4: Submit the application before 22 February 2026. Edit option available for 5 days post-deadline (once, subject to approval).
