BSSC Sub Statistical Officer Recruitment 2025 Online Form for 682 Posts

Published by: RozgarDwaar Editorial Desk | Updated: 2025-04-19
Apply Online (Official Website) Download Notification PDF Official Website

Bihar Staff Selection Commission (BSSC) Patna has published employment notification for recruitment of Sub Statistical Officer/ Block Statistical Officer for fill up total 682 posts. Eligible Candidates must fill Online Application Form from 1st April to 21st April 2025. On this Page, Job Aspirants can read The BSSC Sub Statistical Officer Vacancy details, Age Limit, Qualification, Selection Process and Apply Online details.

BSSC Sub Statistical Officer Recruitment 2025 for 682 Posts:

Post Name

No of Posts

Sub Statistical Officer/ Block Statistical Officer

682

Age Limit::

Minimum Age Limit: 21 years (for all categories)
Maximum Age Limit: 37 years for UR (Male), 40 years for BC & Extremely BC, 40 years for UR (Female), 42 years for SC & ST, Plus 10 years for PwBD.

Monthly Salary (Pay Scale)::

Pay Level – 7.

Eligibility Criteria::

Bachelor's degree in any one of the subjects Economics/ Mathematics/ Statistics from a recognized university.

Selection Process::

The recruitment selection on the basis of following methods:

✔️ Preliminary Examination
✔️ Main Examination
✔️ Documents Verification
✔️ Medical Examination

Application Fee::

✔️ ₹ 295/- for General / OBC / EWS (CL & NCL) Category Candidates.
✔️ No fee for SC / ST / Employees of MOIL Limited Candidates.
✔️ The fee should be made through Online Payment Mode (Credit Card / Debit Card / UPI / Net Banking etc.)

How to Apply: :

➢ Eligible candidates should apply for the BSSC Official website . The registration process involves the following steps:

Step 1: Eligible candidates must apply online for the BSSC Sub Statistical Officer 2025 via the Online Application Portal at www.onlinebssc.com.

Step 2: After completing the registration process, log in to the home page of www.onlinebssc.com using the username and password sent to your registered mobile number and email ID.

Step 3: Candidates are required to enter their personal details, correspondence address, and permanent address. Click the "Save" button. Verify the information on the displayed page and, at the bottom, find the "Edit" and "Next" buttons. Click the "Edit" button if you need to make any changes to personal details or addresses; otherwise, click "Next" to proceed to the payment page.

Step 4: Make the application fee payment using the SBI bank Online payment gateway or ICICI bank Online payment gateway. After making the payment, keep the receipt for future reference.

Step 5: After successful payment, enter your educational details and save before submitting.

Step 6: On the next page, upload a recent photograph (soft copy) and a scanned signature.

Step 7: After uploading the photograph and signature, take a LIVE PHOTO and upload it.

Step 8: After successfully uploading the live photo, accept the terms and conditions and click the "Submit" button for final submission. The online application process will be completed.
