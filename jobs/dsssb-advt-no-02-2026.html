DSSSB Advt No. 02/2026 Apply Online for 216 Various Group B & C Posts

Published by: RozgarDwaar Editorial Desk | Updated: 2026-07-14
Apply Online (Official Website) Download Notification PDF Official Website

DSSSB Advertisement No. 03/2026: Apply Online for 1979 Junior Scientific Assistant, TGT, Special Educator & Other Posts. Delhi Subordinate Services Selection Board (DSSSB) has released Advertisement No. 03/2026 for recruitment of 1979 posts in various departments of Government of NCT of Delhi including Forensic Science Laboratory, Directorate of Education, New Delhi Municipal Council, Department of Delhi Archives, Information Technology Department and Labour Department.

This is a major recruitment drive for Group B & Group C posts such as Junior Scientific Assistant (various disciplines), TGT (Computer Science / Special Education / Domestic Science), Special Educator (Primary), I.T. Assistant, Fitter, Lift Operator and other technical positions.

Online applications will be accepted from 16 June 2026 (12:00 Noon) to 15 July 2026 (23:59 hours). Eligible candidates can apply only through the official DSSSB portal dsssbonline.nic.in.

DSSSB 03/2026 Pay Scale:

Selected candidates will be appointed in the pay matrix as per 7th Central Pay Commission (post-wise details available in notification). In addition, DA, HRA, TA and other allowances as admissible under GNCTD rules will be paid.

DSSSB 03/2026 Eligibility Criteria:

Age Limit (as on closing date of application – 15.07.2026):

  • Most posts: 18–27 / 30 / 32 / 35 / 37 years (post-specific)

Age Relaxation:

  • SC/ST: 5 years
  • OBC (Delhi): 3 years
  • PwBD: 10 years (15 years for SC/ST PwBD, 13 years for OBC PwBD)
  • Ex-Servicemen: Military service period + 3 years
  • Departmental Candidates: Up to 40 years (higher for reserved categories)

Educational Qualification (post-specific):

  • Junior Scientific Assistant posts: Master’s Degree in relevant science subject / Forensic Science
  • TGT / Special Educator posts: Bachelor’s Degree + B.Ed / Special B.Ed + CTET
  • Technical / IT posts: Graduation / Diploma / ITI with computer knowledge

Full qualification details are given against each post code in the notification.

DSSSB 03/2026 Selection Process:
  1. Computer Based Test (One Tier Examination) – Post-specific scheme (I-T-G / I-T-T / I-T-T Teaching)
  2. Document Verification / e-Dossier

Final selection is based on marks obtained in the CBT. Negative marking of 0.25 marks applies for every wrong answer.

DSSSB 03/2026 Exam Scheme (One Tier):
Scheme Post Codes Duration Questions Marks
One Tier General (I-T-G) 34/26, 39/26, 40/26 2 Hours 200 MCQs 200
One Tier Technical (I-T-T) 21/26 to 33/26, 35/26 to 38/26, 45/26 2 Hours 200 MCQs 200
One Tier Technical/Teaching (I-T-T) 41/26 to 44/26 2 Hours 200 MCQs 200

Minimum qualifying marks: 40% (UR/EWS), 35% (OBC Delhi), 30% (SC/ST/PwBD).

How to Apply for DSSSB Advertisement No. 03/2026:
  1. Visit the official DSSSB portal https://dsssbonline.nic.in
  2. Complete One-Time Registration (OTR) if not already done
  3. Login with your credentials and select Advertisement No. 03/2026
  4. Fill all personal, educational and category details carefully
  5. Upload recent passport-size photograph and signature
  6. Pay the application fee (if applicable) through SBI e-Pay
  7. Submit the form and take a printout of the confirmation page
  8. Only one application per post code is allowed
