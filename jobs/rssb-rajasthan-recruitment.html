RSSB NHM Recruitment 2025 - Online Form for 13398 Nurse, Assistant & Other Posts

Published by: RozgarDwaar Editorial Desk | Updated: 2025-05-01
Apply Online (Official Website) Download Notification PDF Official Website

Rajasthan Staff Selection Board (RSSB), Government of Rajasthan has published employment notification for recruitment of Community Health Officer, Nurse, Data Entry Operator, Assistant, Lab Technician and other various para medical positions on Contractual Basis. The RSSB Vacancy 2025 Online Registration start from 18th February and scheduled to be end on 19th March 2025. The RSSB NHM Vacancy 2025 Online Form Registration Reopen from 2nd April to 1st May 2025.

Age Limit: :

Minimum 18 years and Maximum 40 years as on 1st January 2025.
The Age relaxation as per Rajasthan Government rules.

Monthly Salary::

Level -3, ₹ 21,700 - 69,100/- and other allowances admissible to Central Government Employee from time to time under the rule.

Eligibility Criteria::

(1) Matriculation (10th Class) Pass or its equivalent qualification from Government recognized board (As on 30/12/2024).

(2) Physical Standards: Height: Male 170 CMS & Female : 157 CMS, Chest Male Only : 80-85 CMS, Weight - As per Medical standards.

Selection Process::

The recruitment selection on the basis of following methods:

✔️ Examination
✔️ Document Verification
✔️ Medical Examination

Application Fee::

✔️ ₹ 600/- for General / OBC / EBC (CL) category candidates.
✔️ ₹ 400/- for Reserved Category candidates.
✔️ ₹ 300/- for Error Correction Charges.
✔️ Payment Method - Online Mode.

How to Apply: :

➢ Eligible Candidates should fill Online Application Form through RSMSSB Rajasthan Online Recruitment Portal (sso.rajasthan.gov.in).
➢ The candidates first done One Time Registration (OTR) - Create Account with a valid Email Address and Mobile Number.
➢ Next Apply RSSB Vacancy 2025 Apply Online option from April 2025.
➢ The candidates should fill basic / personal / educational details and upload relevant documents (Recent Photograph, Signature, Identify Proof etc.).
➢ The last date for registration of online applications is 01/05/2025 (Monday) up to 11:59 PM.

Frequently Asked Questions::

What positions are available in the RSSB Vacancy 2025?

The recruitment includes positions for Community Health Officer, Nurse, Data Entry Operator, Assistant, Lab Technician, and other various para-medical roles. All these positions are on a contractual basis.

When does the online registration for RSSB Vacancy 2025 close?

The online registration for these vacancies begins on 2nd April 2025 and is scheduled to end on 1st May 2025.

How to apply for the RSSB recruitment for paramedical positions?

Candidates can apply online through the official RSSB website at rssb.rajasthan.gov.in. They need to fill out the application form, upload necessary documents, and pay the application fee as per the instructions provided on the site.

What are the eligibility criteria for RSSB recruitment 2025?

Specific eligibility criteria like educational qualifications, age limits, and experience vary by position. Generally, candidates need relevant educational qualifications in fields like nursing, medical technology, or data entry for respective roles. Age limits are typically set between 21 to 40 years, but it's advisable to check the detailed advertisement for exact requirements.
