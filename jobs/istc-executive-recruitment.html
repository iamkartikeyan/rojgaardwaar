ISTC Executive Recruitment 2026 – Apply Online for 09 Engineer, IT, Finance, HR Posts | Last Date 18-09-2026

Published by: RozgarDwaar Editorial Desk | Updated: 2026-09-02
Apply Online Now Download Notification PDF ← Back to Portal Home

India Ship Technology Centre (ISTC), a Section-8 Company under the Ministry of Ports, Shipping and Waterways, Government of India, has invited online applications from Indian citizens for Executive posts on a contract basis. The recruitment is notified under Vacancy Notification No. ISTC/HR/01/2026-27/RECRUITMENT PH. 1 (B) dated 25 August 2026.

The ISTC Executive Recruitment 2026 online application process is open from 01st September 2026 to 18th September 2026. This article covers vacancy details, eligibility, consolidated pay, selection process, application fee, official notification and the apply-online link.

✅ ISTC Executive Recruitment 2026 Selection Process:

Selection will be as follows:

  • Scrutiny / shortlisting of online applications
  • Written Test (for Assistant Manager and Executive posts, if applications are large)
  • Certificate Verification
  • Personal Interview

Rank lists will be based on written test (if held) and interview marks. In case of a tie, older candidates will be ranked higher. No TA/DA will be paid. Call letters will be issued only by email / ISTC website.

✅ ISTC Executive Recruitment 2026 Application Fee:

  • General / OBC / EWS and others: ₹400 + bank charges (non-refundable)
  • SC / ST candidates: Exempted
  • Payment Method: Online only (Debit Card / Credit Card / Internet Banking) from 01.09.2026 to 18.09.2026

✅ How to Apply ISTC Executive Recruitment 2026?

Candidates should apply online only through the ISTC / SAP portal. Do not send printouts or certificates by post. Submit only one application.

  • Step 1: Read the User Manual and FAQ on istcindia.org before filling the form.
  • Step 2: Complete one-time registration on the SAP online portal and apply for the relevant post between 01.09.2026 and 18.09.2026.
  • Step 3: Upload certificates of age, qualification, experience and caste, along with a recent colour photograph.
  • Step 4: Pay ₹400 online if fee is applicable and ensure the application status shows “In process”.
  • Step 5: Save the registration number and a printout of the submitted form for future correspondence at career@istcindia.org / info@istcindia.org.
