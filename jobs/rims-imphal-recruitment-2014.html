RIMS Imphal Recruitment 2021 Walk in Interview | 40 Junior / Senior Residents, Medical Doctor Vacancies

Published by: RozgarDwaar Editorial Desk | Updated: 2021-08-20
Apply Online (Official Website) Download Notification PDF Official Website

RIMS Imphal Recruitment 2021: Regional Institute of Medical Sciences (RIMS) Imphal intended to urgently required for Doctors for COVID 19 duty for engagement of Senior Resident, CMO and Junior Residents on 06 months contract basis. The Walk in Interview scheduled to be held on 27th August 2021.

Name of the Post

No of Vacancies

Senior Resident

10

CMO / Critical Care Dr.

10

Junior Resident (Non Academic)

20

✅ Upper Age Limit: 45 years.

✅ Consolidated Pay:

✔️ Senior Resident: ₹ 1,10,000/- per month
✔️ CMO / Critical Care Dr.: ₹ 90,700/- per month (pay in case of MBBS candidate) OR ₹ 1,10,000/- (in case of MD / MS candidate)
✔️ Junior Resident: ₹ 90,000/- per month

✅ Qualification and Experience:

✔️ Senior Resident: Post Graduate Degree (MD) in Anaesthesiology. Candidate must be registered with the Manipur Medical Council.
✔️ CMO / Critical Care Dr.: MBBS Degree from University / Institute recognized by MCI / NMC.
✔️ Junior Resident: MBBS Degree from University / Institute recognized by MCI / NMC. Manipur Medical Council Registration.

✅ Selection Process: Walk in interview.

✔️ Walk in Interview Date: 27th August 2021
✔️ Timing: From 11:00 AM
✔️ Venue: Conference Room of Jubilee Hall, RIMS, Imphal.

✅ How to Apply: Eligible Interested candidates apply in the prescribed application format. Submit filled application along with the copies of educational qualification, experience etc., to "The Office of the Administrative Officer, RIMS, Imphal" latest by 23/08/2021 up to 4:00 PM.

Detailed Notification >>

✅ Archived Jobs:

Office of the Medical Superintendent, Regional Institute of Medical Sciences (RIMS) Hospital Imphal, Manipur invites applications in prescribed application format for appointment of Junior Residents, Office Assistant and Research Assistant on contract basis. The last date for receipt of applications is 5th May 2021 (For Senior Residents).

Name of Post

Total Vacancies

Junior Residents

39

Research Assistant

01

Office Assistant

01

✅ Age Limit: Maximum 35 years for Research Assistant / Office Assistant. 
✅ Pay Scales:

✔️ Junior Residents: Pay Level 10 ₹ 56100/- as per 7th CPC
✔️ Research Assistant: ₹ 31,000/- per month
✔️ Office Assistant: ₹ 25,000/- per month

✅ Educational Qualifications:

✔️ Junior Residents: MBBS. All doctors should be registered under Manipur Medical Council or MCI.

✔️ Research Assistant: Graduate Degree in relevant subject (BDS) with 03 years of work experience (OR) Post Graduate in relevant subject or related discipline.

✔️ Office Assistant: Degree in Arts / Science. Computer Typing Speed of 125 kdpm or 25 wpm (net).

✅ Selection Process: Interview.

✅ Application Fee: Not Specified.

✅ How to Apply:

✔️ Junior Residents: The last date for submission of application form is 05/05/2021 up to 4:00 PM at Medical Superintendent Office. The applicants are to appear before the Selection Board at the Conference Room (South) of Jubliee Hall at 11:00 AM on 25th May 2021.

✔️ Research Assistant:  Interested candidates can apply in prescribed application format and submit scan copy of the application along with attested copies of caste, experience, educational certificate etc. email to rims.imphal@gov.in on or before 24/04/2021 up to 4:00 PM.

✔️ Office Assistant: Interested candidates can apply in prescribed application format and submit scan copy of the application along with attested copies of caste, experience, educational certificate etc. email to rims.imphal@gov.in on or before 22/04/2021 up to 4:00 PM.
