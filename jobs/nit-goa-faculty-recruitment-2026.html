NIT Goa Faculty Recruitment 2026 – Apply Online for 28 Posts | Last Date 30-09-2026

Published by: RozgarDwaar Editorial Desk | Updated: 2026-09-02
Apply Online Now Download Notification PDF ← Back to Portal Home

National Institute of Technology Goa (NIT Goa), an Institute of National Importance under the Ministry of Education, Government of India, has invited applications from Indian Nationals for faculty posts of Assistant Professor, Associate Professor and Professor. The recruitment is notified under Advertisement No. NITGOA/RECT/2026/OW/14 dated 31 August 2026.

The NIT Goa Faculty Recruitment 2026 last date for submission of the online application is 30th September 2026 (5:00 PM). The hard copy with supporting documents must reach the Institute by 7th October 2026 (5:00 PM). This article covers vacancy details, eligibility, credit points, application fee, selection process, official notification and the apply-online link.

✅ NIT Goa Faculty Recruitment 2026 Eligibility Criteria:

Associate Professor

Level-13A2
Entry Pay ₹1,39,600

Ph.D. with 6 years after Ph.D. including 3 years as Assistant Professor Grade-I (Level-12), or 9 years total experience with 3 years after Ph.D. and 3 years at Level-12. Credit points: 50.

Professor

Level-14A
Entry Pay ₹1,59,100

Ph.D. with 10 years after Ph.D. or 13 years total experience including 7 years after Ph.D. and 3 years as Associate Professor in Level-13A2 (or 4 years in Level-13A1 / combination). Credit points: 80.

Experience in Institutions of repute, R&D laboratories or relevant industry will be considered as per NIT recruitment rules. Serving regular faculty of NIT Goa need not send NOC or apply through proper channel.

✅ NIT Goa Faculty Recruitment 2026 Selection Process:

Eligible applications will be processed as follows:

  • Shortlisting on credit-point score, experience and research / academic performance
  • Presentation
  • Interview

Government / Semi-Government / PSU / University employees should apply through proper channel or produce NOC and vigilance clearance at the time of interview. An advance copy may be sent.

✅ NIT Goa Faculty Recruitment 2026 Application Fee:

  • General / OBC candidates: ₹1,500 per department per post
  • Women candidates: ₹500 per department per post
  • SC / ST / PwD / EWS: Exempted
  • Indian Nationals applying from abroad: USD 50 (all categories)
  • Payment Method: Online, non-refundable

✅ How to Apply NIT Goa Faculty Recruitment 2026?

Candidates must apply online through the Institute recruitment portal and also send a hard copy with supporting documents. Apply well before the last date; no extension will be granted.

  • Step 1: Visit www.nitgoa.ac.in/faculty_recruitment.html and read the instructions carefully.
  • Step 2: Confirm eligibility for the post, department, pay level, experience and credit points.
  • Step 3: Fill the online form, pay the fee (if applicable) and submit it on or before 30 September 2026, 5:00 PM.
  • Step 4: Send the signed hard copy of the application with all supporting documents so that it reaches NIT Goa by 7 October 2026, 5:00 PM.
  • Step 5: Serving employees of Government / Semi-Government / PSU / Universities should apply through proper channel and keep NOC and vigilance clearance ready for the interview.

✅ FAQs:

What is the total number of vacancies in NIT Goa Faculty Recruitment 2026?

28 posts (16 Assistant Professor, 8 Associate Professor and 4 Professor).

What is the qualification for NIT Goa Faculty Recruitment 2026?

Ph.D. is essential for all posts, along with the prescribed post-Ph.D. experience and cumulative credit points (except Assistant Professor Grade-II).

What is the pay scale in NIT Goa Faculty Recruitment 2026?

Level-10 (₹70,900) for Assistant Professor Grade-II, Level-12 (₹1,01,500) for Grade-I, Level-13A2 (₹1,39,600) for Associate Professor and Level-14A (₹1,59,100) for Professor.

What is the selection process for NIT Goa Faculty Recruitment 2026?

Shortlisting on credit points, experience and academic/research performance, followed by presentation and interview.

What is the application fee for NIT Goa Faculty Recruitment 2026?

₹1,500 for UR/OBC, ₹500 for women, nil for SC/ST/PwD/EWS, and USD 50 for Indian Nationals applying from abroad.

What is the last date for NIT Goa Faculty Recruitment 2026?

Online application: 30 September 2026, 5:00 PM. Hard copy: 7 October 2026, 5:00 PM.
