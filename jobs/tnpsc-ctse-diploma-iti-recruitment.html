TNPSC CTSE Diploma ITI Recruitment 2026: Apply Online for 839 Posts | Last Date 15-08-2026

Published by: RozgarDwaar Editorial Desk | Updated: 2026-08-14
Apply Online (Official Website) Download Notification PDF Official Website

Tamil Nadu Public Service Commission (TNPSC) has released Notification No. 06/2026 for Combined Technical Services Examination (Diploma / ITI Level). Online applications are invited for 839 vacancies in various technical posts. Eligible candidates can apply online from 17 July 2026 to 15 August 2026.

Eligible Candidates must check the complete details regarding Post-wise Vacancies, eligibility criteria, exam pattern, selection process and the online application process for the TNPSC CTSE Diploma / ITI Recruitment 2026 provided below.

How to Apply for TNPSC CTSE Diploma / ITI Recruitment 2026::

Eligible candidates must apply only through Online Mode.

➢ Step 1: Visit the official TNPSC website www.tnpsc.gov.in.

➢ Step 2: Click on the link for Combined Technical Services Examination (Diploma / ITI Level).

➢ Step 3: Register / Login using One Time Registration (OTR).

➢ Step 4: Fill the online application form carefully and select the posts.

➢ Step 5: Upload required documents and pay the examination fee.

➢ Step 6: Submit the application and take a printout for future reference.
