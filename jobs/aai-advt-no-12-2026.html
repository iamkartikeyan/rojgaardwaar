AAI Advt No 12/2026: Apply Online for 389 Manager and Junior Executive Posts | Last Date 07-09-2026

Published by: RozgarDwaar Editorial Desk | Updated: 2026-08-13
Apply Online (Official Website) Download Notification PDF Official Website

Airports Authority of India (AAI), a leading Public Sector Enterprise under the Ministry of Civil Aviation, has released the recruitment notification for the posts of Manager and Junior Executive in various disciplines.

The AAI Recruitment 2026 (Advt. No. 12/2026) has been released for a total of 389 posts of Manager (Group-A, E-3) and Junior Executive (Group-B, E-1). The Online applications are open from 8th August 2026 to 7th September 2026. This article provides complete details about AAI Manager & Junior Executive Recruitment 2026 including post-wise vacancies, eligibility, pay scale, and important dates.

How to Apply for AAI Recruitment 2026?:

Candidates can apply online through the official AAI website. Follow the steps below:

  • Visit the official website: https://aai.aero
  • Go to Careers → Recruitment section.
  • Click on the link for Advt. No. 12/2026.
  • Register and fill the online application form.
  • Upload photograph, signature, left thumb impression, and handwritten declaration.
  • Pay the application fee (if applicable) and submit the form.
  • Take a printout of the application for future reference.
