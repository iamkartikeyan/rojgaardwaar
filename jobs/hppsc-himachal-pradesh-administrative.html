The Himachal Pradesh state residents and Indian candidates must read official notifications and then apply online.

Important Dates::

Opening date for registration of Online Application Form: January 2025
Last date for registration of Online Application Form: 19/02/2025 / 20/02/2025 / 24/02/2025 / 27/02/2025.
Tentative date of Examination / Interview: Will be notified later.

Age Limit: :

Professor: 50 years & below.
Associate Professor: 45 years & below.
Assistant Professor: 45 years & below.
The Upper Age relaxation - 05 years for SC / ST / OBC / PwD.

Monthly Salary (Pay Scale)::

Professor: Pay Band Level 31, ₹ 148800 - 218600/-
Associate Professor: Pay Band Level 30, ₹ 129700 - 214300/-
Assistant Professor: ₹ 37400 - 67000/- + GP ₹ 8900/-

Eligibility Criteria::

(a) A recognized medical qualification included in the first or second Schedule or Part-II of the third Schedule (other than Licentiate qualifications) to the Indian Medical Council Act, 1956.
(b) A post-graduate degree in the concerned specialty.
(c) Teaching Experience.

Selection Process::

The recruitment selection on the basis of following methods:

✔️ Screening Test and Descriptive Subject Aptitude Test
✔️ Personality Test
✔️ Documents Verification

Application Fee::

✔️ ₹ 600/- for General and Other State Candidates.
✔️ ₹ 150/- for SC, ST, OBC and EWS Category Candidate, No fee for Ex-servicemen.
✔️ The fee should be made through Online Payment Mode (Credit Card / Debit Card / UPI / Net Banking etc.)

How to Apply: :
➢ Eligible and Interested candidates should apply online through HPPSC Online Recruitment Application (ORA) portal.
➢ Before Applying online, the candidates must read detailed official notification and if you eligible start your registration.
➢ The candidates must enter your Personal, Educational qualification, experience and Other details.
➢ Upload scanned copy of required documents & Certificates.
➢ Before submitting the application, ensure you review the preview and verify all fields carefully. Save a printed copy of the final form for your future reference.
