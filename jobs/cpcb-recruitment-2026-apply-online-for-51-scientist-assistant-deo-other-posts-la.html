CPCB Recruitment 2026 - Apply Online for 51 Scientist, Assistant, DEO & Other Posts | Last Date 07-09-2026

Published by: RozgarDwaar Editorial Desk | Updated: 2026-08-29
Apply Online (Official Website) Download Notification PDF Official Website

Central Pollution Control Board (CPCB) has released the detailed notification for recruitment of various posts on contract basis under the project “Strengthening of Environmental Regulators (SER)-Phase-III Project” on River Ganga. A total of 51 vacancies are available for Scientist, Assistant Manager, Administrative Assistant, Scientific Assistant, Taxonomist, Data Entry Operator, Field Assistant and other posts, funded by National Mission for Clean Ganga (NMCG) under Namami Gange Programme.

The CPCB Recruitment 2026 online registration process will begin on 24 August 2026, and the last date to apply is 07 September 2026. On this page, aspirants can find all important details of CPCB Vacancy 2026 including the official notification and application link.

CPCB Recruitment 2026 Eligibility Criteria::
  • Scientist-D (01 Post | Age: 50 Years): First class Master’s in Environmental Science/Natural Science OR First Class B.E./B.Tech (Environmental/Chemical/Civil/Energy & Environment/Biotechnology) + Cumulative impact factor ≥10 (UGC Care/Scopus) OR Minimum 5 Books/Chapters (ISBN) OR Minimum 5 Technical Reports of CPCB/SPCB. Experience: 7 years relevant practical experience in river pollution control, industrial pollution, sewage & environmental management.
  • Scientist-C (08 Posts | Age: 45 Years): Same qualification as Scientist-D but impact factor ≥7 OR 3 Books/Chapters OR 3 Technical Reports. Experience: 5 years relevant experience.
  • Scientist-B (12 Posts | Age: 35 Years): First class Master’s in Environmental Science/Natural Science OR First Class B.E./B.Tech (relevant branches) + Valid GATE (90%ile+) / NET-JRF. Experience: 3 years relevant experience (research/teaching also considered).
  • Assistant Manager (Finance) (03 Posts | Age: 35 Years): First class Master’s in Commerce (Finance & Accounting/Taxation) / CA / MBA (Finance & Accounting). Experience: 8 years post-qualification (Tally, QuickBooks, TDS/GST/PF/ESI returns, payroll, MS Office, tax experience minimum 2 years).
  • Administrative Assistant (01 Post | Age: 35 Years): First Class Bachelor’s Degree + working knowledge of computers. Experience: Minimum 5 years administration work in Govt./PSU/Autonomous bodies (₹4200 GP or equivalent) + skill test in noting & drafting. Desirable: First Class PG Degree.
  • Scientific Assistant (13 Posts | Age: 35 Years): First class Master’s in Environmental Science/Natural Science OR First Class B.E./B.Tech (relevant) + Valid GATE (90%ile+) / NET-JRF.
  • Taxonomist (01 Post | Age: 35 Years): First Class M.Sc. Desirable: M.Sc. Botany/Zoology with ≥75% + GATE (90%+)/NET-JRF + impact factor ≥7 OR 1 Book/Chapter OR 1 Technical Report of CPCB/SPCB.
  • Data Entry Operator (04 Posts | Age: 32 Years): First class Bachelor’s Degree in Science / BCA + Skill test in MS Office + English typing @45 wpm & Hindi typing @35 wpm (computer only).
  • Field Assistant (08 Posts | Age: 27 Years): 10+2 in Science with minimum 55% + Knowledge of water/wastewater sampling and sample preservation.

Note: Engagement is purely on contract basis initially for one year (extendable based on performance & requirement). Age relaxation as per Government of India rules for reserved categories.

How to Apply for CPCB Recruitment 2026::

Eligible candidates must apply ONLINE ONLY through the official CPCB website. No other mode of application will be accepted.

➢ Step 1: Visit the official website https://cpcb.gov.in/.

➢ Step 2: Go to the Career / Recruitment section and click on the relevant advertisement link for SER-Phase-III Project.

➢ Step 3: Complete Registration using a valid E-mail ID and active mobile number. Note down the Registration Number.

➢ Step 4: Login using Registration No. and Date of Birth.

➢ Step 5: Fill the online application form carefully with personal, educational, experience and other required details. Select the desired post(s).

➢ Step 6: Upload scanned copies of Photograph, Signature and Experience Certificate (as per instructions on the portal).

➢ Step 7: Re-check all entries, submit the application and ensure the status shows as “Submitted”. Take a printout of the application for future reference.
