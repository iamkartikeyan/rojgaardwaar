MPESB Group 2 Sub Group 3 Vacancy 2025 Apply Online for 454 Posts | Last Date 12 November

Published by: RozgarDwaar Editorial Desk | Updated: 2025-11-11
Apply Online (Official Website) Download Notification PDF Official Website

The Madhya Pradesh Employees Selection Board (MPESB), Bhopal, has released a revised notification for the Group 2 Sub Group 3 Recruitment 2025, inviting online applications from eligible Indian citizens for filling 454 various technical and administrative posts in different departments of Madhya Pradesh Government. This recruitment drive offers opportunities for graduates and diploma holders in fields like engineering, science, agriculture, and computer applications to secure stable government jobs in Madhya Pradesh.

The MPESB Group 2 Sub Group 3 Online Application Form starts from 29th October 2025 and closes on 12th November 2025 (11:59 PM). Candidates are advised to review the revised notification thoroughly before submitting their applications.

MPESB Group 2 Sub Group 3 2025 Age Limit::
  • 18 to 40 years as on 01.01.2025.
  • Additional age relaxation as per Madhya Pradesh Government rules for reserved categories, ex-servicemen, and PwD.
MPESB Group 2 Sub Group 3 2025 Eligibility Criteria::
  • Junior Silk Inspector: Bachelor's degree in Sericulture, Agriculture, Biology, along with computer proficiency.
  • Field Officer: Bachelor's in Agriculture, Biology, and basic computer skills.
  • Biomedical Engineer: B.E./B.Tech in Biomedical Engineering with at least 1 year experience in a 300-bed hospital.
  • Occupational Therapist Technician: Degree in Occupational Therapy with valid registration from MP Allied Health Council.
  • Sanitation Inspector: B.Sc. in Science (Biology/Chemistry) or Diploma in Sanitary Inspector training.
  • Assistant Engineer (Civil/Electrical/Mechanical): B.E./B.Tech in respective engineering branch.
  • Lab Technician (Science): B.Sc. or Diploma in Laboratory Technology, DMLT, or BMLT.
  • Inspector (Weights & Measures): Graduate in Science or Engineering, preferably with Physics or Mathematics.
  • Junior Supply Officer: Any Bachelor's degree, preference for Commerce, Management, or Public Administration.
  • Biochemist: M.Sc. in Biochemistry or Chemistry.
  • Statistician: Graduate or Postgraduate in Statistics or Mathematics with Statistics as a subject.
  • Manager: Graduate or Postgraduate in relevant field as per departmental requirements.
  • Occupational Therapist: Bachelor's in Occupational Therapy (BOT).
  • Senior/Junior Laboratory Assistant: B.Sc. in Biology, Chemistry, or related subjects.
  • Dairy Dock Assistant: B.Sc. in Dairy Technology, Food Technology, or Animal Husbandry.
  • Laboratory Assistant / Senior Assistant (Gu.ni.): B.Sc. in Science (Biology or Chemistry).
  • Chemist: M.Sc. or B.Sc. in Chemistry based on the position level.
  • Lab Assistant: B.Sc. in Science or Diploma in Laboratory Technology.
  • Steward: Graduate with Diploma in Hotel Management or Catering as required by the department.
  • Assistant Programmer: BCA, B.Sc. Computer Science, or Graduate with PGDCA/DOEACC 'A' Level.
  • Data Entry Operator (DEO): Graduation plus Diploma in Computer Applications with typing proficiency.

How to Apply MPESB Group 2 Sub Group 3 Recruitment 2025::

The MPESB Online Registration is conducted via the Mponline Portal. Follow these steps for MPESB Group 2 Sub Group 3 Online Application:

➢ Step 1: Visit the MPESB Online Application Portal starting 29th October 2025 and select the Group 2 Sub Group 3 Exam.

➢ Step 2: Complete registration with personal details, upload photo, signature, and educational/experience certificates as required.

➢ Step 3: Pay the fee online and submit the form by 12th November 2025 (11:59 PM). Use the correction window up to 17th November 2025 if needed.
