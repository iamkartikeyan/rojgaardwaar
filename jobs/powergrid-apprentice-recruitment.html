POWERGRID Apprentice Recruitment 2026 - Apply Online for 270 Posts | Last Date 10-09-2026

Published by: RozgarDwaar Editorial Desk | Updated: 2026-08-24
Apply Online (Official Website) Download Notification PDF Official Website

Power Grid Corporation of India Limited (POWERGRID), a Maharatna Central Public Sector Enterprise under the Ministry of Power, Government of India, has released the POWERGRID Apprentice Recruitment 2026 notification for engagement of apprentices under the Apprentices Act, 1961. A total of 270 apprenticeship training slots are available across 10 regional establishments for a duration of one year.

The POWERGRID Apprentice Recruitment 2026 online application process will remain open from 25 August 2026 to 10 September 2026. There is no application fee and no written examination or interview. Selection is purely on the basis of merit. This article provides complete details including region-wise & trade-wise vacancies, eligibility criteria, stipend, important dates and how to apply.

POWERGRID Apprentice 2026 Eligibility Criteria::
  • ITI–Electrician: Full-time ITI in Electrician trade.
  • Diploma (Electrical/Civil): Full-time 3-year Diploma in the respective branch.
  • Graduate (Electrical/Civil): Full-time B.E./B.Tech/B.Sc. (Engg.) in the respective branch
    (Part-time / Correspondence / Distance / Online mode not accepted).
  • Passing Year: Must have passed the final examination between 11.09.2024 and 10.09.2026
  • Age Limit: Minimum 18 years completed as on the date of application (No upper age limit mentioned)
  • Other Conditions: Indian citizen. Not already undergone / currently undergoing apprenticeship training
    Not having more than 1 year of job experience. Medically fit as per Apprentices Act, 1961.

POWERGRID Apprentice 2026 Selection Process::
  • Screening of applications on the basis of eligibility and merit of marks
  • Candidates shortlisted in the ratio of 1:5 for Document Verification
  • Final merit list prepared after Document Verification
  • No Written Examination or Interview
  • Selected candidates will execute a tripartite apprenticeship contract on NATS/NAPS portal

How to Apply for POWERGRID Apprentice Recruitment 2026?:

Eligible candidates must follow these steps to apply online:

  • Step 1: Register on the NATS portal (for Diploma/Graduate) at nats.education.gov.in or NAPS portal (for ITI) at apprenticeshipindia.gov.in and complete 100% profile.
  • Step 2: Note down your NATS/NAPS registration/enrolment number.
  • Step 3: Visit the official POWERGRID website www.powergrid.in → Careers → Engagement of Apprentices → Apply Online.
  • Step 4: Select only one region/state of your choice and fill the online application form.
  • Step 5: Upload the required documents (photograph, signature, educational certificates, caste certificate, bank details, etc.) and submit the application before 10 September 2026.

Note: Candidates can apply to only one region. Incomplete applications will be rejected without intimation.
