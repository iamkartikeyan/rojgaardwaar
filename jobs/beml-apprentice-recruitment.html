BEML Apprentice Recruitment 2026 – Apply Online for 1346 Posts | Last Date 08-09-2026

Published by: RozgarDwaar Editorial Desk | Updated: 2026-08-25
Apply Online (Official Website) Download Notification PDF Official Website

BEML Limited, a Government of India Enterprise under the Ministry of Defence, invites online applications for engagement of Apprentices for one-year duration at its various manufacturing units. A total of 1,346 vacancies are available across Bangalore, Mysore, KGF and Palakkad complexes in Graduate, Diploma and ITI trades, offering an excellent opportunity for freshers.

The BEML Apprentice Recruitment 2026 online registration process began on 18 August 2026, and the last date to apply is 08 September 2026. On this page, apprenticeship aspirants can find all important details of BEML Apprentice Vacancy 2026, including the official notification and application link.

BEML Apprentice Vacancy Trades::
  • BE/B.Tech (Mechanical, Electrical & Electronics, Electronics & Communication, Civil, Computer Science/IT, Automobile, AI & ML, Aeronautical etc.)
  • Diploma (Mechanical, Electrical, Electronics, Civil, Automobile, Computer Science etc.), General Graduates (BBA, B.Com, BCA/B.Sc, BA)
  • ITI Trade Apprentices (Fitter, Electrician, Welder, Turner, Machinist, COPA, Mechanic Diesel, Carpenter, Plumber, Sheet Metal Worker, MMV, Electronic Mechanic, Tool & Die Making etc.).
BEML Apprentice Recruitment 2026 Eligibility Criteria::

Educational Qualification:

  • Graduate (Technical) – 4-year Full Time Engineering: Mechanical, Electrical & Electronics, Civil, Computer Science, Electronics & Communication, Automobile, Information Science, AI & ML, Aeronautical and other allied branches from a Govt. recognised University/Institution.
  • Graduates (General) – 3-year Full Time: B.Sc, B.Com, BCA, BBA, BBA (HR) or equivalent.
  • Diploma – 3-year Full Time: Electrical & Electronics, Civil, Computer Science, Electronics & Communication, Automobile, Instrumentation, Mechanical Engineering and allied.
  • ITI – Full Time (1/2 year as per trade): Fitter, Welder, Turner, Machinist, Electrician, COPA, Mechanic Diesel, Carpenter, Tool & Die Making, Sheet Metal Worker, Plumber, MAEE, MMV, Electronic Mechanic etc. with NTC.

Age Limit:

  • As per the Apprentices Act, 1961 and Apprenticeship Rules (relaxations applicable for reserved categories).

Other Eligibility:

  • Candidates must first register on NAPS (for ITI) or NATS (for Degree/Diploma) portals and obtain registration/enrolment number before applying on BEML website.

BEML Apprentice Recruitment 2026 Selection Process::
  • No Written Examination
  • No Interview
  • Merit List prepared category-wise for the respective trade in each location (in the ratio of 1:5 times the training slots)
  • Document Verification

Applications will be screened as per eligibility criteria. Shortlisted candidates will be called for document/certificate verification in order of merit. Reservation applicable as per Apprentices Act 1961 and Apprenticeship Rules 1992. Communication will be sent via registered email. No TA/DA will be paid.

How to Apply for BEML Apprentice Recruitment 2026::

Eligible candidates must apply ONLINE ONLY. No other mode of application will be accepted. There is no application fee.

➢ Step 1: First register yourself on the respective portal:

  • For ITI candidates: https://apprenticeshipindia.gov.in (NAPS)
  • For Degree/Diploma candidates: https://nats.education.gov.in (NATS)

Complete/update your profile and upload all required documents to get NAPS/NATS registration/enrolment number.

➢ Step 2: After obtaining the registration number, visit https://recruitment.bemlindia.in → Apprentices → Login/Registration.

➢ Step 3: Fill the online application form carefully with personal, educational and other required details.

➢ Step 4: Upload necessary documents as prescribed.

➢ Step 5: Submit the application form and take a printout of the confirmation page for future reference.

FAQs::
What is the total number of vacancies in BEML Apprentice Recruitment 2026?:

A total of 1,346 Apprentice posts are available across Bangalore (400), Mysore (227), KGF (608) and Palakkad (111) complexes.

What is the last date to apply for BEML Apprentice 2026?:

The last date to submit the online application is 08 September 2026.

What is the stipend for BEML Apprentices 2026?:

Graduate (Technical/General): ₹12,300/-, Diploma: ₹10,900/-, ITI: ₹9,600/- or ₹10,560/- (depending on trade) per month.

What is the educational qualification required for BEML Apprentice 2026?:

BE/B.Tech / Diploma / ITI / Graduation (BBA, B.Com, BCA/B.Sc etc.) in the relevant trade/stream from a Government recognised institution, as applicable.

What is the selection process for BEML Apprentice 2026?:

Merit List (category-wise, trade-wise, location-wise in 1:5 ratio) followed by Document Verification. There is no written exam or interview.

Is there any application fee for BEML Apprentice 2026?:

No. Application is completely free of cost.

How to apply for BEML Apprentice Recruitment 2026?:

First register on NAPS (ITI) or NATS (Degree/Diploma) portal, then apply online on the official BEML recruitment website between 18 August 2026 and 08 September 2026.
