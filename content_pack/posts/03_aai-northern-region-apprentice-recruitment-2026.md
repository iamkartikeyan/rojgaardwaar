# AAI Northern Region Apprentice Recruitment 2026

**Source:** https://www.indgovtjobs.in/2026/08/aai-northern-region-apprentice.html  
**IndGovtJobs page date:** 29 August 2026  
**Vacancies:** 205  
**Published deadline:** 16 September 2026

## Original rewritten content

Airports Authority of India (Northern Region) has published an apprenticeship opportunity for graduate, diploma and ITI candidates for the 2026–27 training cycle. The listed intake is 205 tentative seats across engineering and trade disciplines. The page states that eligible candidates should hold a relevant full-time degree, diploma or ITI qualification and meet the specified passing-year condition. The published monthly stipends are ₹15,000 for graduate apprentices, ₹12,000 for diploma apprentices and ₹9,600 for ITI apprentices. Selection is described as merit based, followed by document verification and any additional steps prescribed in the notification. Applicants should pay close attention to domicile/state eligibility, discipline-wise seat distribution, age limits and the official apprenticeship portal instructions before submitting the application.
