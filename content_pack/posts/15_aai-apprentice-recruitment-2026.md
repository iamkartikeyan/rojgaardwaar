## Original rewritten content

Airports Authority of India has announced an apprenticeship intake of 36 graduate and diploma positions for its Western Region. The opportunity is intended for eligible fresh candidates seeking structured apprenticeship training rather than a regular permanent appointment. The published deadline is 30 September 2026. Applicants should confirm the accepted degree or diploma disciplines, age requirements, training duration, stipend, regional eligibility and the registration steps on the official apprenticeship system. Apprenticeship recruitment normally depends on the exact qualification and passing-year conditions, so candidates should compare their certificates against the notified disciplines before applying.
