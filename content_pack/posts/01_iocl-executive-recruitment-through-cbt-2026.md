## Original rewritten content

Indian Oil Corporation Limited (IOCL) has announced Executive recruitment through a Computer Based Test for multiple engineering, marketing, law, quality-control and diploma-engineering roles. The notification covers Grade A, A0 and E0 positions and uses a multi-stage selection route involving CBT, group discussion/group task and personal interview. The published eligibility varies by role, with relevant engineering degrees, MBA/PG qualifications, law qualifications, M.Sc. Chemistry or engineering diplomas depending on the post. The listed pay bands range from ₹30,000–₹1,20,000 to ₹50,000–₹1,60,000. The application window is shown as closing on 3 September 2026 at 5:00 PM. Candidates should verify the discipline-specific marks, experience, age limits, fee and official application instructions in the notification before applying.
