# SBI Clerk Recruitment 2026

**Source:** https://www.indgovtjobs.in/2026/08/sbi-clerk-recruitment.html  
**IndGovtJobs page date:** 18 August 2026  
**Vacancies:** 9766  
**Published deadline:** 31 August 2026

## Original rewritten content

State Bank of India has notified Junior Associate (Customer Support & Sales) recruitment in the clerical cadre. The published vacancy is 9,766 posts, comprising regular and backlog positions distributed across State/UT circles. Eligibility includes graduation in any discipline and proficiency in the specified local language for the selected State/UT, subject to the conditions in the notification. The selection structure includes an online preliminary examination, an online mains examination and a local language proficiency test where applicable; there is no interview for final merit. The page lists a pay scale of ₹24,050–₹64,480 with graduate increments and applicable allowances. Applicants are required to choose one State/UT and should check the official notification for circle-wise vacancies, language requirements, age rules, fee category and document specifications.
