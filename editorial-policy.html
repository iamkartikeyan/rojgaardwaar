Editorial Policy & Publishing Workflow

At ROZGARDWAAR, we are committed to maintaining the highest standard of editorial integrity, transparency, and factual accuracy. Our editorial policies govern every stage of researching, structuring, writing, and publishing government job updates.

1. Sourcing Protocol

We do not copy competitor job aggregation blogs. Our editors source recruitment announcements exclusively from primary public authorities:

  • Weekly official editions of the Ministry of Information & Broadcasting’s Employment News / Rozgar Samachar.
  • Gazette of India and State Official Gazettes.
  • Official `.gov.in`, `.nic.in`, and `.ac.in` websites of Union ministries, constitutional commissions (UPSC, SSC), State PSCs, High Courts, and Central PSUs.

2. Multi-Tier Verification Process

Before any recruitment record is published on ROZGARDWAAR, it must pass through a strict 3-tier checklist:

  1. Authenticity Check: Verification that the notification PDF contains an official advertisement number, competent signatory, and official seal.
  2. Parameter Extraction: Accurate manual extraction of eligibility parameters (exact educational degrees, age cutoffs, caste relaxation slabs, physical fitness standards, and fee exemptions).
  3. Direct Link Association: Direct mapping of the official PDF notification URL and official application portal, ensuring zero intermediate redirect ad pages.

3. Original Interpretation & Analysis

Rather than dumping raw text, our editors provide original, helpful context such as qualification fit, salary calculation after 7th Pay Commission allowances, career progression trajectory, and eligibility pitfalls to beware of.
