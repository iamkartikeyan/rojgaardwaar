About RojgaarDwaar

RojgaarDwaar (“Your Doorway to Government Jobs”) is an independent, non-partisan Indian employment discovery platform founded with the objective of eliminating opacity, confusion, and deceptive advertisements in the government recruitment landscape.

Our Core Mission

Millions of young Indian men and women aspire to secure careers in the Central Civil Services, Indian Railways, Public Sector Banks, Armed Forces, Paramilitary, State Administration, Police, Teaching, and Public Sector Undertakings (PSUs). However, navigation across hundreds of regional examination boards and decentralized employment gazettes often leads to missed deadlines, fraudulent application links, and misinterpretation of eligibility requirements.

RojgaarDwaar bridges this gap by aggregating, curating, and structuring recruitment notices directly from official gazettes and departmental portals into easy-to-read cards, comprehensive key summary tables, eligibility calculators, and authentic link directories.

Our Editorial Standards

  • Primary Source Verification: Every single recruitment record on RojgaarDwaar is verified against the official gazette notification, employment news publication, or verified government department portal.
  • Zero Fake Apply Buttons: We never employ misleading “Click to Download” or fake “Instant Apply” buttons that redirect to third-party ad networks. All links point directly to the authorized portal.
  • Practical Guidance: We provide original, concise “Should You Apply?” insights to help candidates identify whether their qualification profile and physical criteria match the requirements before paying examination fees.
  • Clarity on Expirations: Expired recruitment notices are clearly demarcated to prevent applicants from submitting fees to concluded drives.
