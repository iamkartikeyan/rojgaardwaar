Corrections Policy & Errata Handling

RojgaarDwaar is dedicated to delivering flawless recruitment information. However, when errors occur or when recruitment authorities issue official corrigenda, addenda, date extensions, or vacancy revisions, we respond promptly and transparently.

1. Handling Government Corrigenda & Extensions

Government examination bodies (such as SSC, UPSC, IBPS, and State PSCs) periodically extend application deadlines or revise vacancy breakdowns. When such official notices are released, our editorial team updates the corresponding job record on RojgaarDwaar within 2 to 4 hours of official publication and updates the "Last Verified" date badge.

2. Reader-Reported Corrections

If you identify a typographical error, an outdated link, or an incorrect qualification detail in any of our job posts, we welcome your feedback. Please email us at editor@rojgaardwaar.in with:

  • The Job Title or URL of the page on RojgaarDwaar.
  • A specific description of the error.
  • A reference or link to the official government notification PDF.

3. Turnaround Commitment

All reader correction submissions are reviewed by our lead editor within 24 business hours. If verified, the correction is implemented immediately with a timestamped record.
