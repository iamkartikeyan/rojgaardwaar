Primary Source Verification Directory

To ensure strict reliability and zero misinformation, ROZGARDWAAR monitors only primary, verified, and statutory government recruitment portals. Below is a directory of official institutions whose recruitments we cover:

1. Union & Constitutional Commissions

  • UPSC: Union Public Service Commission (upsc.gov.in)
  • SSC: Staff Selection Commission (ssc.gov.in)
  • NTA: National Testing Agency (nta.ac.in)

2. Indian Railways

  • Railway Recruitment Control Board (RRB): All 21 Regional Boards (rrbapply.gov.in / indianrailways.gov.in)
  • Railway Recruitment Cells (RRC): Northern, Western, Eastern, Southern, Central & North Eastern Railway Zones.

3. Banking & Financial Institutions

  • IBPS: Institute of Banking Personnel Selection (ibps.in)
  • Reserve Bank of India (RBI): (opportunities.rbi.org.in)
  • State Bank of India (SBI): (sbi.co.in/careers)
  • NABARD & SIDBI: (nabard.org / sidbi.in)

4. Armed Forces, Paramilitary & Defence PSUs

  • Join Indian Army: (joinindianarmy.nic.in)
  • Join Indian Navy: (joinindiannavy.gov.in)
  • Indian Air Force: (afcat.cdac.in / agnipathvayu.cdac.in)
  • DRDO & ISRO: (drdo.gov.in / isro.gov.in)
  • CAPF (CRPF, BSF, CISF, ITBP, SSB): Ministry of Home Affairs (mha.gov.in)

5. State Public Service Commissions & Subordinate Boards

Monitored across UPPSC, UPSSSC, BPSC, BSSC, MPSC, TNPSC, KPSC, WBPSC, RPSC, RSMSSB, MPPSC, MPESB, HSSC, PSSSB, GPSC, TGPSC, APPSC, Kerala PSC, OSSC, APSC, JSSC, and all other Indian State Boards.
